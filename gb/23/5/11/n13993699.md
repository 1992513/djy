<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>图说英国70年来第一个国王加冕</h2>
<h6></h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2023年05月11日讯】</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993717" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993717" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993717-GettyImages-1252751916-e1683796975717.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993717" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993717-GettyImages-1252751916-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993717" class="wp-caption-text">（Ben Stansall &#8211; WPA Pool/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>加冕后的查尔斯头戴“帝国王冠”（Imperial State Crown），手里拿着象征着基督世界的“主权之球”（Sovereign’s Orb）和象征着王权“十字架权杖”（Sceptre with Cross），走出威斯敏斯特大教堂。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993723" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993723" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993723-GettyImages-1487963656-e1683797142527.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993723" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993723-GettyImages-1487963656-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993723" class="wp-caption-text">( Leon Neal/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>加冕结束后，国王和王后回到白金汉宫，然后出现在阳台上，向下面的民众致意。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993727" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993727" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993727-GettyImages-1487961846-e1683797261311.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993727" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993727-GettyImages-1487961846-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993727" class="wp-caption-text">（Christopher Furlong/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>威廉王子一家的三个孩子总是非常吸引人们的注意。夏洛特公主和路易王子看到空军飞行表演的时候非常高兴，尤其是路易王子露出非常兴奋的表情。未来的王位继承人乔治王子（右一）这次担任了礼宾。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993730" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993730" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993730-GettyImages-1488008443-e1683797357711.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993730" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993730-GettyImages-1488008443-600x399.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="399"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993730" class="wp-caption-text">（Brandon Bell/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>白金汉宫门前的广场上聚集了大批民众。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993736" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993736" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993736-GettyImages-1252749983-e1683797783983.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993736" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993736-GettyImages-1252749983-600x410.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="410"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993736" class="wp-caption-text">（Aaron Chown &#8211; WPA Pool/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>查尔斯坐在拥有1,000年历史的加冕座椅上，头戴圣爱德华王冠（St Edward’s Crown），手里分别拿着代表王权的“十字架权杖”（Sceptre with Cross）和“鸽子权杖”（Sceptre with Dove）。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993738" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993738" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993738-GettyImages-1487984918-e1683797864509.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993738" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993738-GettyImages-1487984918-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993738" class="wp-caption-text">（Richard Heathcote/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>加冕礼当天，伦敦阵雨不断。空军进行飞行表演的时候，正赶上大雨，表演的规模缩小了一些，但是好在“红箭”的表演团队仍然成功地将天空染成了代表英国国旗颜色的红白蓝。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993740" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993740" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993740-GettyImages-1487990686-e1683797955550.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993740" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993740-GettyImages-1487990686-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993740" class="wp-caption-text">（Richard Heathcote/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>比较少见的马上军乐队，需要一边驾驭马匹，一边吹拉乐器，真是不容易，尤其是打鼓的这位。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993746" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993746" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993746-GettyImages-1487948876-e1683798065825.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993746" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993746-GettyImages-1487948876-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993746" class="wp-caption-text">（Rob Pinney/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>金叶装饰的马车，再加上旁边身穿红色制服的王室侍从，如果去掉旁边的路灯、现代装束的行人和候车亭，是不是让人感觉回到了几百年前？</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993755" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993755" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993755-GettyImages-1252756810-e1683798153513.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993755" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993755-GettyImages-1252756810-600x388.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="388"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993755" class="wp-caption-text">（Aaron Chown &#8211; WPA Pool/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>查尔斯身穿的这件衣服由金线织成，也就是在丝绸上缠上金线，再缝制成衣服，重量有两公斤。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993760" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993760" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993760-GettyImages-1487921164-e1683798236683.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993760" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993760-GettyImages-1487921164-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993760" class="wp-caption-text">（ Jeff J Mitchell/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>加冕礼也是王室的聚集，图为西班牙国王夫妇和比利时国王夫妇。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993764" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993764" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993764-GettyImages-1487950887-e1683798332189.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993764" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993764-GettyImages-1487950887-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993764" class="wp-caption-text">（Dan Mullan/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>成千上万的人聚集到伦敦市中心，希望可以亲眼看到国王的加冕仪仗队。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993766" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993766" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993766-GettyImages-1252743499-e1683798422261.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993766" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993766-GettyImages-1252743499-600x897.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="897"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993766" class="wp-caption-text">（Victoria Jones &#8211; WPA Pool/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>加冕礼期间下议院议长莫当特（Penny Mordaunt）大出风头。她的另外一个头衔是枢密院议长（Lord President of the Council），职责是举着国王的国事宝剑（Sword of State）。</p>
<p>她举着这把宝剑保持图中所示的姿势超过一个小时，被称赞臂力惊人。加冕礼前，她披露，为了做好准备，她“练习俯卧撑”。她也是英国史上首次担任这个重要角色的女性。</p>
<p>当然，她的量身定做的服装也吸引了许多好评。这条裙子以及配套的披风和发带据报导需要2,000镑。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993768" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993768" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993768-GettyImages-1252740519-e1683798513384.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993768" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993768-GettyImages-1252740519-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993768" class="wp-caption-text">（SUSANNAH IRELAND/AFP via Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>加冕期间的抗议人群。主张共和的抗议者认为女王的去世意味着现在就是英国实现共和的好机会。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993771" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993771" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993771-GettyImages-1487627828-e1683798596181.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993771" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993771-GettyImages-1487627828-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993771" class="wp-caption-text">（ Brandon Bell/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>英国王室70年一次的加冕礼吸引了许多王室的铁杆粉丝，包括来自美国和澳大利亚的粉丝。他们早早就在附近露营，抢到可以观看加冕仪仗队的最佳地点。有人甚至在4月27日就赶来了，等待了九天。</p>
<figure id="attachment_13993774" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-13993774" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993774-GettyImages-1488146874-e1683798682135.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-13993774" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id13993774-GettyImages-1488146874-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-13993774" class="wp-caption-text">（Finnbarr Webster/Getty Images)</figcaption></figure>
<p>5月7日，英国各地的民众举行了5,000场街头聚餐，庆祝国王加冕。图为温莎的居民在温莎公园的草地上聚餐。</p>
