<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>承传中华文化 CACC举办2023中国节</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14023268-DSC01343.jpeg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
