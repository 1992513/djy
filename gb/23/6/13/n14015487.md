<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>You&amp;Me乐团“时光旅行”献唱经典老歌</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015481-DSC07813-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>波士顿“You & Me”乐团6月11日举办“时光旅行”公演，获满场观众热捧。（刘景烨/大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2023年06月14日讯】（大纪元记者刘景烨剑桥市报导）时隔疫情3年，波士顿“You &amp; Me”乐团再度举办年度音乐会，以经典老歌带领大家“时光旅行”，再获满场观众的热烈追捧。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14015486" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14015486" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015486-DSC07851.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14015486" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015486-DSC07851-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14015486" class="wp-caption-text">波士顿“You &amp; Me”乐团6月11日举办“时光旅行”公演，成员们合影。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p style="font-weight: 400">无论是疫情之前的两届“草根音乐祭”，还是如今的“时光旅行”，You &amp; Me乐团的年度音乐会每次都得到波城听众的热情欢迎。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14015482" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14015482" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015482-DSC07817.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14015482" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015482-DSC07817-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14015482" class="wp-caption-text">波士顿“You &amp; Me”乐团6月10日举行“时光旅行”音乐会，献唱经典老歌。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p style="font-weight: 400">今年的音乐会还是在剑桥市（Cambridge）的“Lilypad”音乐室举办。据乐团成员介绍，这次卖了近60张票。现场座无虚席，不少没有座位的观众站在后排，并激动地鼓掌、欢呼、挥舞灯光。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14015483" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14015483" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015483-DSC07828.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14015483" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015483-DSC07828-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14015483" class="wp-caption-text">波士顿“You &amp; Me”乐团6月10日举行“时光旅行”音乐会，献唱经典老歌。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p style="font-weight: 400">音乐会期间，主持人Emily和Mandy幽默风趣，她们的串场词迎来此起彼伏的笑声。团长阿伯黄崇校名声在外，弹奏吉他，从容不迫。资深歌手刘双州唱歌时深情投入，一首“山丘”引人共鸣；新加入的歌手杨智晴活泼伶俐，带动全场的轻快氛围。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14015484" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14015484" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015484-DSC07837.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14015484" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015484-DSC07837-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14015484" class="wp-caption-text">观众向乐团成员们献花。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p style="font-weight: 400">15位乐团成员在一个多小时间倾情演出，得到观众们的热烈欢呼和掌声。不少观众意犹未尽，高喊“Encore（再来一首）”，还有多位观众向乐团成员们献花感谢。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14015485" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14015485" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015485-DSC07838.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14015485" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/06/id14015485-DSC07838-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14015485" class="wp-caption-text">驻波士顿台北经济文化办事处处长孙俭元及夫人倪雪娥观赏6月10日的“You &amp; Me”乐团演出。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p style="font-weight: 400">驻波士顿台北经济文化办事处处长孙俭元及夫人倪雪娥，以及侨教中心主任潘昭荣，也都到场观赏这场音乐会。</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400">演出最后，主持人还宣布，You &amp; Me将于9月9日在牛顿市（Newton）举办“台湾音乐祭”，希望再与大家见面。◇</p>
