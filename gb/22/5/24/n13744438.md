<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>海狼级潜艇南海撞海山 美海军发布调查发现</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2021/11/id13353876-000_9PB6U7.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
