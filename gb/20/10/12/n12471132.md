<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>1张纸改善视力！胡乃文推荐3碗药膳 养眼补肾</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2020/10/eyesight-chinese-medicine0-1013-health11.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>手机一直滑，完全停不下来，结果眼睛疲劳、涩痛，视力不知不觉变差??教你帮眼睛伸一伸懒腰，让它不再疲劳。</p><h2><span style="color:#188638">长时间看手机 蓝光让眼睛疲劳、视力下降</span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>手机、电脑等3C产品的蓝光会伤眼睛。这是因为，蓝光的波长短，容易造成散射，眼睛需要用力聚焦来看3C屏幕。时间长了，睫状肌就会紧绷，弹性下降，眼睛疲劳不适，视力也受影响。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>盯着手机屏幕时，水晶体也会吸收蓝光，长期可能造成水晶体的蛋白质变性、变混浊，就如同把水晶体“煮熟”。年轻人可能增加早发型白内障的风险。</p><p>特别是小孩子，他们的眼睛还没有发育成熟，更难抵御蓝光的伤害。建议父母们在孩子6岁以前，尽可能不要用3C产品来当他们的保母。</p><p></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#188638">一张纸改善视力！2个动作让视力变年轻</span></h2><p>常做2个护眼动作，改善眼球疲劳、涩痛，让视力变年轻。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p><strong>1. “一张纸”眼球运动</strong></p><p>人的眼睛有六对眼肌。锻炼眼球肌肉，让这些肌肉活动自如，眼睛就会更灵活，同时舒缓视力疲劳。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2020/10/eyesight-chinese-medicine1-1013-health11.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>锻炼眼球肌肉，让眼睛肌肉活动自如，眼睛就会更灵活，同时舒缓视力疲劳。（胡乃文开讲）</figcaption></figure></div><p>推荐一个很简单的锻炼眼肌的方法。拿一张A4纸，举在眼前或者贴在墙上，然后用眼睛分别看纸的左上角、右上角、右下角、左下角，顺时钟做14次，再反时钟做14次。然后，用力闭眼、放松，再用力闭眼、放松。就可以了。</p><p><strong>2. 睡前热敷眼睛</strong></p><p>睡前3分钟，把双手搓热，闭上双眼，用微温的手敷眼睛。反复14次。做这个动作，能通经活络，改善眼睛周围的血液循环，也能让眼睛更好地进入休眠。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>以前我在学速读时，老师就曾教我们这一招，因为它对于让视力敏锐、提升阅读速度有帮助。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p></p><h2><span style="color:#188638">3味药膳 养眼、补肾又补血</span></h2><p>《本草备药》中提到，将枸杞、菊花用等份的量，做成蜜丸服用，能够使人“永无目疾”。我以前还没有学中医药时，就曾尝试把枸杞跟菊花捣一捣做成药丸，当时没有用蜜，因为枸杞也是黏糊糊的，直接吃下。只觉得香甜好吃，常吃对眼睛也有帮助。</p><p>提到养眼药膳，也要先讲枸杞菊花茶。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><p><strong>枸杞菊花茶：常用的明目茶饮</strong></p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2020/10/eyesight-chinese-medicine2-1013-health11.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source
