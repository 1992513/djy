<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>三七散瘀止血 治伤有灵效 别和川七弄混了</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2019/02/sanqi.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>三七又称参三七、田七，是五加科人参属，假人参的变种，主产地在中国的云南及广西深山中。中国人叫它三七，是因为它有三个分枝，各长七片叶子；也有人说，是因它在种植后三至七年收获而得名。三七在初夏开淡黄绿色小花，伞形花序单个顶生。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000">三七散瘀、止血、止痛 是伤科要药</span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>三七性温，味甘微苦，入肝、胃、大肠经。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>三七的功效，可用“止血、散瘀、止痛”六个字来概括之，所以历来都用三七做为伤科金疮杖疮要药，<span style="color:#333333">很少当成补品食用。</span>三七可治吐血、衄血、下血、血痢、血崩、经水不止，产后恶血不下、血晕、血痛、赤目痈肿、虎咬蛇伤诸病、金刃箭伤。</p><p>治疗目赤：目赤是瘀血引起的，三七能化瘀血。</p><p>治疗痈肿（脓疮，即恶性肿毒）：三七与醋磨涂之就散，若已破者用三七粉末掺之。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>三七有去瘀化血功能，可缩短凝血的时间，是云南白药之主要成分。</p><p>若骨折日久疼痛不止者，可用左归丸加三七、没药、乳香。脑血栓或脑溢血引起的后遗症，可用小活络丹加三七。以上用方加减要请中医师诊断后才可处方，不可自行用药。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p></p><h2><span style="color:#008000">三七的故事</span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p>从前有个猎人上山去打猎，看到一只老虎。猎人马上举起弓，朝老虎射了一箭。箭虽射中，他追过去，却发现老虎跑了，地上留下一摊血。</p><p>猎人心想，以老虎中箭的情形，一定会流血不止而死，于是打算明天再来找那只老虎。</p><p>隔天上山时，猎人在另一山头看到那只受伤的老虎。他很惊讶，见老虎竟然还活着，而且正在吃一种草药。当他举弓要再射那老虎时，老虎却很敏捷地跑了。猎人就采回老虎吃的草药，治疗出血的疾病，发现那草药果然很有效。此药就是三七。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><h2><span
