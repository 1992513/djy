<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>在美国公路遭警察Pull Over 保命“黄金三原则”</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/06/id14276512-37fb99905dbd979245e5e033.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>新移民在美国开车，最怕后视镜突然亮起红白蓝相间的警灯，紧接着一声短促的警笛（Yelp）。</p><p>这时千万别慌，保持冷静，老老实实pull over（靠边停车）。在正式进入对话设计之前，先帮你科普一个高速公路上的冷知识，以及最关键的安全保命原则。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>在高速公路</strong><strong>pull over</strong><strong>你的是哪种警察？</strong></span></h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>在美国的Highways / Interstates（高速公路）上，你最常遇到的不是市警（Officer），而是State Police（州警），在很多州他们也被称为Highway Patrol（高速公路巡逻队）。</p><p>州警的正确称呼是Trooper。他们通常戴着类似牛仔帽的标志性宽檐帽（Campaign Hat），制服笔挺，专门负责管辖全州的高速公路、跨县犯罪和交通事故。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>美国遭遇</strong><strong>Pull Over</strong><strong>的“黄金三原则”</strong></span><strong>?</strong></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p><strong>1. 双手放在方向盘上（</strong><strong>Keep your hands on the steering wheel</strong><strong>）</strong>：这是最重要的一条！美国警察默认人人有枪。你的双手必须待在10点和2点钟方向，让警察看得清清楚楚。</p><p><strong>2. 不要主动乱动、翻找证件</strong>：千万不要在警察走到车窗之前，就转身去手套箱（Glove compartment）或掏口袋找证件。警察会以为你在拔枪。一切动作等警察发出指令后再做。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p><strong>3. 动作要缓慢、口头报备</strong>：当警察问你要证件时，一边伸手一边嘴上说：“It&#x27;s in my glove box, I&#x27;m reaching for it now.”（在我的手套箱里，我现在拿给你。）</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>情境一：高速公路“超速”被州警</strong><strong>拦下</strong></span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p>这是最常见的场景。我们设计一段标准、礼貌且“懂规矩”的实用对话：</p><p><strong>Trooper</strong><strong>：</strong>Good afternoon. The reason I pulled you over is you were doing 85 in a 70 mph zone. License and registration, please.</p><p>（下午好。我拦下你是因为你在限速 70英里的区域开到了85英里。请出示驾照和行照。）</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p><strong>You</strong><strong>（</strong><strong>双手放在方向盘上</strong><strong>）：</strong>Yes, Trooper / Officer. My license is in my wallet, and the registration is in the glove box. May I reach for them?</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>（好的，长官。我的驾照在钱包里，行照在手套箱里。我可以拿给您吗？ ）</p><p><strong>Trooper</strong><strong>：</strong>Yes, go ahead.</p><p>（好的，拿吧。）</p><p><strong>You</strong><strong>（</strong><strong>缓慢拿出证件递过去</strong><strong>）：</strong>Here you go, Trooper.</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><p>（给您，长官。）</p><p><strong>Trooper</strong><strong>：</strong>Thank you. Is there any particular reason you were rushing today?</p><p>（谢谢。你今天开这么急，有什么特别的原因吗？）</p><p><strong>老美避坑指南</strong><strong>：</strong>这时候千万不要跟警察编故事或者狡辩说：“No, I wasn&#x27;t speeding!”。承认错误或者说不知道就行。</p><p><strong>You</strong><strong>：</strong>I&#x27;m sorry, Trooper. I didn&#x27;t realize I was going that fast. I&#x27;ll definitely be more careful.</p><p>（对不起，长官。我没察觉自己开那么快。我以后一定会注意。）</p><p><strong>Trooper</strong><strong>：</strong>Stay in your vehicle. I’m going to run your information. I&#x27;ll be right back.</p><p>（待在车里别动。我去核对一下你的资料，马上回来。）</p><p>几分钟后，警察从警车回到你车窗，可能给你开Warning（警告信）或者 Ticket（罚单）</p><p><strong>Trooper</strong><strong>：</strong>Alright, I’m giving you a warning this time. Keep your speed down, safety first. Have a safe drive.</p><p>（好吧，这次我只给你一个警告。把速度降下来，安全第一。开车安全。）</p><p><strong>You</strong><strong>：</strong>Thank you, Trooper. Have a good one!</p><p>（谢谢长官。祝您今天愉快！）</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>情境二：市区“尾灯坏了</strong><strong>╱</strong><strong>没开大灯”被市警</strong><strong>拦下</strong></span></h2><p>在普通市区道路上，拦下你的通常是当地的市警（Police Department）或地方警察，称呼用 Officer。</p><p><strong>Officer</strong><strong>：</strong>Hello there. Do you know why I pulled you over tonight?</p><p>（你好。你知道我今晚为什么拦下你吗？）</p><p><strong>You</strong><strong>：</strong>Honestly, I’m not sure, Officer. Is there a problem?</p><p>（老实说我不太确定，长官。有什么问题吗？）</p><p><strong>Officer</strong><strong>：</strong>Your left taillight is out. It’s a safety hazard, especially at night. License, registration and proof of insurance, please.</p><p>（你的左尾灯熄灭了。这很危险，特别是在晚上。请出示驾照、行照和保险证明。）</p><p><strong>You</strong><strong>：</strong>Oh, I had no idea. Thank you for letting me know. Let me get my documents for you. They are in the center console.</p><p>（噢，我完全不知道。谢谢您提醒我。我拿证件给您，都在中央扶手箱里。）</p><p><strong>You</strong><strong>（</strong><strong>递交证件</strong><strong>）</strong><strong>：</strong>Here is my license, registration and the digital insurance on my phone.</p><p>（这是我的驾照、行照，还有手机里的电子保险证明。）</p><p><strong>Officer</strong><strong>：</strong>Thanks. Wait here for a moment.</p><p>（谢谢，在这等一会。）</p><p>（核对完后）</p><p><strong>Officer</strong><strong>：</strong>Everything looks clean. I’m not issuing a ticket, but make sure to get that bulb replaced first thing tomorrow.</p><p>（资料都没问题。我不给你开罚单了，但明天一早务必把那个灯泡换掉。）</p><p><strong>You</strong><strong>：</strong>I will do that first thing in the morning. Thank you, Officer.</p><p>（我明天一早首要任务就是办这件事。谢谢长官。）</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>新移民实用英语小贴士（</strong><strong>Scannable Tips</strong><strong>）</strong></span></h2><p><strong>? Proof of Insurance</strong><strong>（</strong><strong>保险证明</strong><strong>）：</strong>在美国开车，除了License（驾照）和 Registration（行照，通常是一张粉色或白色的纸），警察十之八九还会问你要 Proof of Insurance。现在很多州接受手机里的电子保险卡（Digital Insurance Card）。</p><p><strong>? Yes, Sir / Yes, Ma&#x27;am</strong><strong>：</strong>如果你一时紧张，忘记对方是Trooper还是Deputy，直接用通用尊称 Sir（男警）或Ma&#x27;am（女警），同样显得非常礼貌且专业。</p><p><strong>? Run your info</strong><strong>（</strong><strong>核对资料</strong><strong>）</strong><strong>：</strong>警察拿走证件后，会回警车用电脑查你是否有犯罪记录或未缴的罚单，通常需要5–10分钟，这期间绝对不要下车，老老实实听音乐等待即可。</p><p>遭遇警察Pull Over后的保命“黄金三原则”，你记住了吗？</p><p><strong><em>作者简介：</em></strong><em>老哈利在国内有超过</em><em>15</em><em>年的一线教学经验，曾在新东方英语等一线英语培训机构教授雅思托福等，并创办自己的英语培训学校，所开设之“中老年实用英语班”长期受到学员欢迎和追捧。</em><em>移民美国后特在此开设“新移民实用英语”栏目，希望您在此学到英语教科书上不教的，免费英语班上外教不讲的一些最实用的零基础英语，助您尽快融入北美社区生活。</em></p><p>责任编辑：沈少棋
