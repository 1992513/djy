<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>珀斯高速路超速司机数量暴增逾两倍</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820332-803-220241223-Perth-Smart-Freeway-Wade-_DSC5317-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>最新数据显示，过去一年在珀斯高速公路上被抓到的超速司机数量增加了两倍多。图为珀斯高速公路。（周鑫/大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年07月30日讯】<span lang="zh-TW">（大纪元记者</span><span lang="zh-TW">杨达延</span><span lang="zh-TW">澳洲珀斯编译报导）</span><span lang="zh-TW">最新数据显示，过去一年在珀斯高速公路上被抓到的超速司机数量增加了两倍多。</span></p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">据《西澳人报》报导，西澳交通厅数据显示，</span><span lang="en-US">2025-26</span><span lang="zh-TW">年度共有</span><span lang="en-US">114,483</span><span lang="zh-TW">名司机在高速公路上超过法定限速，较前一年的</span><span lang="en-US">36,232</span><span lang="zh-TW">人增加了</span><span lang="en-US">216%</span><span lang="zh-TW">。</span></p>
<p>最近大珀斯地区开展大规模道路安全整治行动，包括大幅增加固定摄像头数量，新增移动及固定<span lang="en-US">AI</span>摄像头，用于抓拍司机开车时使用手机、未正确系安全带等违规驾驶行为。驾驶时触摸或手持手机的最低罚款为<span lang="en-US">500</span>澳元并扣<span lang="en-US">3</span>分；未系安全带的最低罚款为<span lang="en-US">550</span>澳元并扣<span lang="en-US">4</span>分。</p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">在过去</span><span lang="en-US">12</span><span lang="zh-TW">个月查获的</span><span lang="en-US">114,483</span><span lang="zh-TW">名超速司机中，</span><span lang="en-US">Kwinana</span><span lang="zh-TW">高速公路就抓拍到</span><span lang="en-US">96,544</span><span lang="zh-TW">人，其中</span><span lang="en-US">78,475</span><span lang="zh-TW">人被固定摄像头拍到，</span><span lang="en-US">18,069</span><span lang="zh-TW">人被移动摄像头拍到。相比之下，前一年同期</span><span lang="en-US">Kwinana</span><span lang="zh-TW">高速公路仅抓拍到</span><span lang="en-US">26,355</span><span lang="zh-TW">人（固定摄像头</span><span lang="en-US">10,114</span><span lang="zh-TW">人，移动摄像头</span><span lang="en-US">16,241</span><span lang="zh-TW">人），相当于每月约</span><span lang="en-US">2,600</span><span lang="zh-TW">宗违规。</span></p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">交通厅照片显示，</span><span lang="en-US">6</span><span lang="zh-TW">月</span><span lang="en-US">26</span><span lang="zh-TW">日晚约</span><span lang="en-US">10</span><span lang="zh-TW">时</span><span lang="en-US">40</span><span lang="zh-TW">分，在</span><span lang="en-US">Mill Point Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">出口附近，一辆汽车在限速</span><span lang="en-US">80</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里</span><span lang="en-US">/</span><span lang="zh-TW">小时的路段以</span><span lang="en-US">103</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里</span><span lang="en-US">/</span><span lang="zh-TW">小时的速度行驶。约一小时后，另一辆车被拍到以</span><span lang="en-US">115</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里</span><span lang="en-US">/</span><span lang="zh-TW">小时的速度行驶（超速</span><span lang="en-US">35</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里</span><span lang="en-US">/</span><span lang="zh-TW">小时）。次日早上，第三辆车以</span><span lang="en-US">99</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里</span><span lang="en-US">/</span><span lang="zh-TW">小时的速度被抓拍超速。</span></p>
<p><span lang="en-US">Graham Farmer</span><span lang="zh-TW">高速公路上的移动摄像头在</span><span lang="en-US">2024-25</span><span lang="zh-TW">年度共抓到</span><span lang="en-US">735</span><span lang="zh-TW">名超速司机，</span><span lang="en-US">2025-26</span><span lang="zh-TW">年度则猛增至</span><span lang="en-US">4,575</span><span lang="zh-TW">人，涨幅达</span><span lang="en-US">522%</span><span lang="zh-TW">。</span><span lang="en-US">Mitchell</span><span lang="zh-TW">高速公路的移动和固定摄像头在</span><span lang="en-US">2024-25</span><span lang="zh-TW">年度抓到</span><span lang="en-US">9,142</span><span lang="zh-TW">名超速司机，</span><span lang="en-US">2025-26</span><span lang="zh-TW">年度增至</span><span lang="en-US">13,364</span><span lang="zh-TW">人，上升</span><span lang="en-US">46%</span><span lang="zh-TW">。危险驾驶者中包括一辆在高峰时段于</span><span lang="en-US">Karrinyup Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">附近以</span><span lang="en-US">118</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里</span><span lang="en-US">/</span><span lang="zh-TW">小时的速度行驶于限速</span><span lang="en-US">100</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里</span><span lang="en-US">/</span><span lang="zh-TW">小时路段的车辆。另外还有两辆车在相隔仅一小时内分别被抓到：一辆在</span><span lang="en-US">Karrinyup Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">附近以</span><span lang="en-US">123</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里</span><span lang="en-US">/</span><span lang="zh-TW">小时行驶，另一辆在</span><span lang="en-US">Vincent Street</span><span lang="zh-TW">附近以</span><span lang="en-US">125</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里</span><span lang="en-US">/</span><span lang="zh-TW">小时行驶。</span></p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">西澳道路警务指挥部指挥官彼得（</span><span lang="en-US">Mike Peters</span><span lang="zh-TW">）表示，即使轻微超速也会增加发生严重伤亡事故的风险。“超速仍是西澳道路严重和致命撞车事故的重要原因，”他说，“西澳警方通过公开和隐蔽的执法行动，积极打击包括超速在内的危险驾驶行为，覆盖全州范围，包括珀斯高速公路。”</span></p>
<p>“提醒所有司机务必遵守道路限速标志，并根据道路和交通状况调整驾驶行为。”</p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">目前</span><span lang="en-US">Kwinana</span><span lang="zh-TW">高速公路在</span><span lang="en-US">Gentilli Way</span><span lang="zh-TW">和</span><span lang="en-US">Mill Point Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">附近设有固定摄像头，</span><span lang="en-US">Mitchell</span><span lang="zh-TW">高速公路则在</span><span lang="en-US">Vincent Street</span><span lang="zh-TW">和</span><span lang="en-US">Karrinyup Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">设有固定摄像头。</span><span lang="en-US">Mitchell</span><span lang="zh-TW">高速公路南向车道的固定摄像头于上个月才正式启用，因此</span><span lang="en-US">2025-26</span><span lang="zh-TW">年度的摄像头数量比</span><span lang="en-US">2024-25</span><span lang="zh-TW">年度更多。</span></p>
<p>截至目前，<span lang="en-US">2026</span>年西澳已有<span lang="en-US">120</span>人在交通事故中丧生——比去年同期少<span lang="en-US">1</span>人，但比<span lang="en-US">2024</span>年同期多<span lang="en-US">19</span>人。<span lang="en-US">120</span>名遇难者中，<span lang="en-US">58</span>人在珀斯都市区，<span lang="en-US">52</span>人在偏远地区。</p>
