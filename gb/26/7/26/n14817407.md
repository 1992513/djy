<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>“金嗷讲册”主题书展登场 58册台湾原创作品邀民众共读</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817414-863615-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>云林县政府文化观光处举办“金嗷讲册”阅读推广活动，自7月25日至8月23日在云林记忆Cool（虎尾出张所宿舍）展开，透过主题书展、作家交流及创意竞赛，邀请民众走进阅读世界，认识台湾原创出版的多元魅力。（廖素贞／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年07月26日讯】（大纪元记者 廖素贞台湾云林报导）为推广台湾原创出版及培养全民阅读风气，云林县政府文化观光处举办“金嗷讲册”阅读推广活动，自7月25日至8月23日在云林记忆Cool（虎尾出张所宿舍）展开，透过主题书展、作家交流及创意竞赛，邀请民众走进阅读世界，认识台湾原创出版的多元魅力。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14817408" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817408" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817408-863607.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817408" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817408-863607-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817408" class="wp-caption-text">云林县政府文化观光处举办“金嗷讲册”阅读推广活动，自7月25日至8月23日在云林记忆Cool（虎尾出张所宿舍）展开。开展当天邀请文学作家曾美满(右)、古蒙仁(中)与读者面对面分享创作历程！（廖素贞／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14817410" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817410" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817410-863610.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817410" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817410-863610-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817410" class="wp-caption-text">金绘奖区展示经典绘本创作（廖素贞／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14817412" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817412" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817412-863612.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817412" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817412-863612-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817412" class="wp-caption-text">金漫奖区展出的书籍（廖素贞／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14817413" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817413" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817413-863614.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817413" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817413-863614-600x337.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="337" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817413" class="wp-caption-text">金鼎奖区展出的书籍（廖素贞／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>本次书展精选58册金鼎奖、金漫奖、金绘奖及其他优质台湾原创出版作品，涵盖文学、漫画、绘本等类型，让不同年龄层读者都能感受台湾创作者的丰富能量。</p>
<p>文化观光处长谢明璇表示，阅读是文化扎根的重要基础，“金嗷讲册”除了展出优质作品，也安排作家交流及创意竞赛，希望透过多元方式，让阅读融入生活，深化民众对台湾原创出版及在地文化的认识。</p>
<p>开展茶叙特别邀请文学作家曾美满、古蒙仁与读者面对面分享创作历程，现场并推出结合文学意象的“文学餐桌”限定甜品-古早四果冰，带来融合阅读与生活美学的文化体验。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14817415" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817415" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817415-863616.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817415" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817415-863616-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817415" class="wp-caption-text">开展茶叙特别邀请文学作家曾美满、古蒙仁与读者面对面分享创作历程，现场并推出结合文学意象的“文学餐桌”限定甜品-古早四果冰，带来融合阅读与生活美学的文化体验。（廖素贞／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14817411" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817411" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817411-863611.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817411" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817411-863611-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817411" class="wp-caption-text">展出期间民众可结伴共享阅读乐趣（廖素贞／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14817409" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817409" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817409-863608.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817409" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817409-863608-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817409" class="wp-caption-text">展览期间民众可悠闲阅览展出书籍。（廖素贞／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>展览期间除可自由阅览展出书籍，也可报名参加“金嗷讲册”创意竞赛，以说书、戏剧、音乐、漫画或其他创作形式分享阅读成果，成果发表暨颁奖典礼将于8月23日举行。</p>
<p>文化观光处表示，活动地点“云林记忆Cool”兼具“微册角落”阅读据点及文化部“百大文化基地”特色，长期推动阅读与地方文化，欢迎民众踊跃参与，一同支持台湾原创出版，让阅读成为日常生活的一部分。</p>
