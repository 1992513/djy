<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>三东道主美墨加挺进16强 加拿大力战摩洛哥</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14801239-GettyImages-2284258735.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
