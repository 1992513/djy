(Conversational English)</h3><p>A: Have you seen the news? Tesla just dropped the Model Y L in the US!</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>（你有看到新闻了吗？特斯拉刚刚在美国推出了 Model Y L 车型！）</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>B: Yeah! Phrases)</h3><p>Range：续航里程（电动车单次充满电能跑多远）。</p><p>Curb weight：整车整备质量（车辆空载时的重量）。</p><p>Exterior paint options：外观车漆颜色选择。</p><h3>实用句型(Useful Sentences)</h3><p>谈论续航与加速：</p><p>The car gets 325 miles of range on a single charge, and it goes from 0 to 60 mph in just 4.4 seconds.</p><p>（这辆车单次充电续航可达325英里，零百加速只需4.4秒。）</p><p>谈论车身尺寸（长度）：</p><p>The Model Y L is 196 inches long, which is 7 inches longer than the current smaller Model Y.</p><p>(Model Y L 长度为196英寸，比现款较小的 Model Y 长了7英寸。)</p><p>谈论新车漆：</p><p>Cosmic Silver is a brand new exterior paint color for the US market.</p><p>(宇宙银是美国市场全新推出的外观车漆颜色。)</p><h2>Part 3: Interior Layout &amp; Comfort（内装布局与舒适度）</h2><p>这是 Model Y L 最具突破性的地方：三排六座（Three-row, six-seat layout），配备了第二排独立航空座椅。</p><h3>核心单字与词组 (Key Vocabulary &amp; Phrases)</h3><p>Layout：布局、陈设。</p><p>Captain&#x27;s chairs：独立机长座椅（第二排不连在一起的两个独立座椅）。</p><p>Heated &amp; ventilated seats：加热与通风座椅（在美国，通风座椅非常受欢迎）。</p><p>Powered thigh extenders：电动腿托/大腿支撑延伸。</p><p>Armrests：扶手。</p><p>B-pillar / C-pillar：B柱/C柱（车窗之间的钢柱，新车在这些地方加了出风口）。</p><h3>实用句型(Useful Sentences)</h3><p>谈论第二排和第三排：</p><p>The second row features captain&#x27;s chairs with electric lifting armrests and dedicated air vents in the B-pillar.</p><p>(第二排配备了带电动升降扶手的独立机长座椅，并在B柱设有专用出风口。)</p><p>谈论座椅折叠与空间：</p><p>Both the second and third row seats can fold completely flat, giving you 89 cubic feet of total storage space.</p><p>(第二排和第三排座椅可以完全放平，为你提供89立方英尺的总储物空间。)</p><h2>Part 4: Tech, Charging &amp; Audio（科技、充电与音响）</h2><p>特斯拉的灵魂在于屏幕和充电速度。</p><h3>核心单字与词组 (Key Vocabulary &amp; Phrases)</h3><p>Peak Supercharging speed：最高超级充电速度（这台车支持250 kW）。</p><p>Subwoofer：重低音喇叭（18个扬声器包含1个重低音）。</p><p>Acoustic glass：隔音玻璃（360度全车隔音玻璃）。</p><p>Heat rejection：隔热（镀银玻璃车顶能更好地隔热）。</p><h3>实用句型与造句 (Useful Sentences)</h3><p>谈论车内屏幕：</p><p>It features a massive 16-inch center screen and an 8-inch screen for the second row.</p><p>(它配备了一个超大的16英寸中央屏幕，以及一个为第二排准备的8英寸屏幕。)</p><p>谈论充电：</p><p>It hits a peak Supercharging speed of 250 kW, so you can juice up really fast.</p><p>(它的最高超级充电速度达到250 kW，所以充电非常快。)</p><h2>Summary of Must-Know Words（记住5个高频词）</h2><p>1.
