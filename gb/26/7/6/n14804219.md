<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>嘉义市议会115年捐血周活动 定2500袋为目标</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14804220-859576-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>陈姿妏议长主持今日捐血活动记者会。（李撷璎／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年07月06日讯】（大纪元记者李撷璎台湾嘉义报导）在人造血液尚未问世之前，为了支援重大手术等的救命资源，嘉义市议会今（6）日召开“捐血周活动”记者会。本次公益捐血活动将于7月7日（二）至7月13日（一）期间在嘉义捐血站（博爱路一段488号）及垂杨捐血室（垂杨路132号）举办为期一周的公益捐血活动。并设定“2,500袋热血”的目标，呼吁热血乡亲共同挽袖ヽ响应公益。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14804221" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14804221" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14804221-859577.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14804221" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14804221-859577-600x360.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="360" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14804221" class="wp-caption-text">议长陈姿妏接受媒体联访。（李撷璎／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>议长陈姿妏表示，救人一命，胜造七级浮屠。血液是医疗上最关键且不可或缺的资源。即便现代科技发达，血液至今仍无法经由人工制造，只能仰赖大众卷起袖子捐出爱心，每一袋热血都是急需输血的病患延续生命的及时希望。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14804222" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14804222" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14804222-859578.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14804222" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14804222-859578-600x284.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="284" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14804222" class="wp-caption-text">齐心完成2500袋捐血的决心。（李撷璎／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>近年来受少子女化与高龄化双重影响。捐血人口结构减少，然而医院的用血需求却未曾减低，维持血库的穏定成为大众面临的共同挑战，嘉义市议会期盼透过本次活动，不只是一次性的募集，更希望能鼓励市民朋友将“定期捐血”内化为一种生活习惯，让爱心不间断。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14804223" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14804223" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14804223-859579.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14804223" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14804223-859579-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14804223" class="wp-caption-text">王浩议员是第一个响应捐血的议员。（李撷璎／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>议长陈姿妏又说，为了感谢乡亲们的热血相挺，议会今年也准备了全联礼券ヽ卫生纸及洗碗精等实用生活物资作为感谢礼，奖励挽袖捐血的民众；凡捐血250cc（1袋）可获得200元全联礼券与卫生纸1串，捐血500cc（2袋）者可获得400元礼券ヽ卫生纸1串及洗碗精1瓶，相关内容请详阅活动宣传海报。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14804224" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14804224" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14804224-859580.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14804224" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14804224-859580-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14804224" class="wp-caption-text">捐血宣传照。（李撷璎／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
