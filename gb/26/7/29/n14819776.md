<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>陈锡兴空拍看见古堡与海 记录澎湖西屿西台</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819783-864503-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>古堡炮台上的大炮仍静静朝向海面，厚重钢铁与蓝天形成鲜明对比。（陈锡兴提供）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年07月29日讯】（大纪元记者廖俪芬澎湖报导）摄影家陈锡兴拍摄澎湖西屿西台，先以空拍拉开距离。从海岬上方看，古堡低伏在草坡间，外侧是海岸、潮间带与防波设施，远处连着外埯聚落。西台古堡不像一栋单独的建筑，更像是顺着地势埋进海边的一座防御工事。镜头往下靠近，炮座、城墙、拱门与内部通道一一出现。绿色大炮立在圆形炮座上，炮口仍朝向海面；城墙内侧有一排狭长拱形孔洞，炮位下方则是相连的甬道。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14819786" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819786" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819786-864506.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819786" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819786-864506-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819786" class="wp-caption-text">“西台古堡”石碑立在入口前，指引游客走进澎湖重要的军事古迹。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14819777" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819777" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819777-864497.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819777" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819777-864497-600x338.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819777" class="wp-caption-text">摄影家陈锡兴空拍澎湖西屿西台古堡，古堡坐落海岬高地，俯瞰辽阔海岸与聚落。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14819778" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819778" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819778-864498.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819778" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819778-864498-600x338.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819778" class="wp-caption-text">从空中俯瞰西台古堡完整格局，厚实城墙、内部壕沟与炮座清楚可见。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14819783" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819783" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819783-864503.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819783" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819783-864503-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819783" class="wp-caption-text">古堡炮台上的大炮仍静静朝向海面，厚重钢铁与蓝天形成鲜明对比。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14819782" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819782" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819782-864502.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819782" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819782-864502-600x338.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819782" class="wp-caption-text">镜头俯瞰西台古堡下方的潮间带与海岸防护设施，海水在礁岩间留下深浅不同的色泽。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>清法战争后重建海防西屿西台位于西屿乡外埯村西鼻头前方，面向大海。此地早年已有炮台设施，清法战争后，清廷认为澎湖海防必须强化，于光绪十二年至十四年间重建西屿西台，并配置新式阿姆斯壮后膛炮，成为清末澎湖重要的海防炮台。古堡采内外护墙环绕的配置。炮位设在较高处，官厅、兵房、小弹药库与弹药总库等空间则藏在较低的位置，利用厚墙与土坡遮蔽。从空拍画面看，这种配置尤其清楚，整座炮台不是高筑城楼，而是尽量降低轮廓，减少被海上炮火锁定的机会。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14819779" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819779" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819779-864499.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819779" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819779-864499-600x338.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819779" class="wp-caption-text">西台古堡居高临海，碉堡、丘陵、海湾与远方聚落构成西屿海岸景观。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14819784" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819784" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819784-864504.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819784" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819784-864504-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819784" class="wp-caption-text">穿过古堡拱门，石砌通道层层延伸，阳光照进墙面，也照亮历史留下的痕迹。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14819787" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819787" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819787-864507.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819787" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819787-864507-600x560.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="560" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819787" class="wp-caption-text">西台古堡拱门与通道相互串连，厚实石墙展现百年古堡的防御设计。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>甬道里留下岁月痕迹陈锡兴走进古堡拍摄长甬道，让人看见炮台内部的另一面。光线从射口与门洞透进来，墙面上的修补痕迹、斑驳灰泥与局部露出的红砖，都没有被刻意掩饰。这些通道当年不只是游客步行的路线，而是炮台内部运送物资、调度兵员的重要空间。甬道连接不同房间与炮位，让人员能在较安全的环境下移动。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14819795" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819795" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819795-864515.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819795" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819795-864515-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819795" class="wp-caption-text">城墙内侧一排拱形孔洞，展现古堡细致而坚实的防御构造。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14819797" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819797" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819797-864517.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819797" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819797-864517-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819797" class="wp-caption-text">古堡中央广场、通道与炮位交错，完整呈现要塞空间格局。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14819794" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14819794" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819794-864514.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14819794" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14819794-864514-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14819794" class="wp-caption-text">大炮与后方炮位相互呼应，保留古堡守护澎湖海域的历史印记。（陈锡兴提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>从战地设施成为古迹如今，大炮已不再作战，西屿西台则成为国定古迹。炮座、门洞、城墙与通道仍保存着当年的防御逻辑。近年古堡也曾利用甬道举办音乐会，让原本为战事而建的空间，有了不同用途。不过，回到陈锡兴的空拍照片，最清楚的一点仍是：西台古堡的存在，从来离不开眼前这片海。</p>
