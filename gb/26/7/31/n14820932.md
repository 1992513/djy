<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>85岁范良淮夫妻守住甘露梨、晶圆梨的甜脆</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820946-864833_medium-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>夫妻俩在包装区忙着挑选、分级，满桌都是刚采下的甘露梨。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年07月31日讯】（大纪元记者廖俪芬台湾新竹报导）新竹县新埔乡的甘露梨、晶圆梨进入产季，走进果农范良淮的家，摆着一整排奖状和奖座，冠军、银奖、季军都有。面对这些成绩，范良淮没有多谈，反而带着大家往果园里走，注意的还是树上的果、枝条和梨皮颜色。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14820945" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820945" style="width: 601px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820945-864834_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820945" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820945-864834_medium.jpg" alt="" width="601" b="452" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820945" class="wp-caption-text">新埔水梨果形饱满、表皮细致，是果园用心照顾的成果。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14820948" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820948" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820948-864831_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820948" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820948-864831_medium.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820948" class="wp-caption-text">范良淮夫妇与历年奖项合影，种梨从不是一年的功夫。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>枝条低垂 进梨园得一路弯腰</h2>
<p>梨园里的枝条伸得低，个子高的人得一路弯着腰前进，深怕一抬头就被树枝打到。话才说完，范太太就接着分享，今年过年嫁接水梨时，自己真的被树枝打到，还挂急诊、做了微创手术，伤势不算轻。</p>
<p>范良淮说，嫁接不是把枝条接上去就好，接穗、时间、天气和后续照顾都会影响成功率，任何一个环节没顾好，就可能白忙一场。</p>
<p>从冬天养树、嫁接，到开花后疏果、套袋，再等到夏天采收，夫妻俩几乎都自己来。所以他个人偏向采用农改场基因配对的方式，成功率较高，植株也相对健康。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14820941" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820941" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820941-864838_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820941" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820941-864838_medium.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="799" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820941" class="wp-caption-text">85岁的范良淮走进梨园，逐一拆开纸袋查看甘露梨的成熟情形。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14820950" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820950" style="width: 601px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820950-864829_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820950" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820950-864829_medium.jpg" alt="" width="601" b="451" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820950" class="wp-caption-text">果园里一颗颗套袋的甘露梨，等待采收季到来。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>水梨最怕没水 大水槽不能少</h2>
<p>范太太最常提醒的一件事，就是水不能少。她说，水梨非常怕没水喝，缺水不只会让果实品质变差，严重时还可能影响结果。因此果园里的蓄水槽都做得很大，大到外人看了很难想像。对果农来说，那不是浪费空间，而是夏天照顾水梨的重要准备。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14820940" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820940" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820940-864839_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820940" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820940-864839_medium.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="800" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820940" class="wp-caption-text">范太太打开纸袋准备采收，看到果实饱满，笑得很开心。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14820949" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820949" style="width: 603px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820949-864830_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820949" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820949-864830_medium.jpg" alt="" width="603" b="452" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820949" class="wp-caption-text">梨园架设灌溉管线与棚架，照顾每一棵果树的生长。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>连鸟都会挑又大又甜的梨</h2>
<p>果子慢慢长大后，还得防鸟。范良淮笑说，一大包原本看起来有三颗漂亮水梨，常常就会有一颗被鸟啄过；鸟好像也会挑，专找又大又好吃的果实下手。</p>
<p>果农辛苦套袋、等成熟，最后还得和鸟儿抢收成。但似乎看不到范良淮防鸟措施，他觉得与自然共存，剩下的才是他的。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14820947" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820947" style="width: 449px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820947-864832_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820947" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820947-864832_medium.jpg" alt="" width="449" b="598" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820947" class="wp-caption-text">范良淮仔细拆开保护纸，查看每一颗甘露梨的外观。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14820943" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820943" style="width: 452px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820943-864836_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820943" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820943-864836_medium.jpg" alt="" width="452" b="803" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820943" class="wp-caption-text">即使果实已成熟，外皮被鸟吃了。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>甘露梨：果实大、果心小</h2>
<p>夏天最受欢迎新埔一带栽培的水梨品种不少，其中晶圆梨的正式名称是“台中二号”，由台中区农业改良场以丰水梨和横山梨杂交选育而成。它适合中低海拔地区栽培，果形圆整、果皮褐色，果心小，果肉细致、清脆多汁，也带有淡淡果香。</p>
<p>甘露梨则常称为“宝岛甘露梨”，是台湾农友以晶圆梨、新兴梨等亲本长期选育出来的品种。它的果实较大、果心小、酸味低，吃起来水分多、口感细致，成熟时还带些清甜果香。两种梨的来源不同，但都很适合台湾夏季市场。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14820942" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820942" style="width: 599px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820942-864837_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820942" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820942-864837_medium.jpg" alt="" width="599" b="449" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820942" class="wp-caption-text">现切甘露梨果肉白嫩、清甜多汁，让人一口接一口。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>等果皮转黄 才是范良淮的采收标准</h2>
<p>怎么挑到好吃的水梨？范良淮说得很直接：要看果皮有没有自然转黄，表面要光滑。皮摸起来粗粗的，通常代表还没到最好吃的时候，口感自然会差一些。他坚持等果实自然转色后才采收，消费者买回家冷藏后就能直接吃，不必再等后熟。</p>
<p>甘露梨的甜度大概有12度，一颗削好，都有一盘子大，冰冰凉凉，全家可以开心大口的品尝它。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14820933" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820933" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820933-864827.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820933" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820933-864827.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="800" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820933" class="wp-caption-text">范良淮夫妇逐颗包装甘露梨，从采收、分级到出货都亲力亲为。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14820938" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820938" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820938-864841_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820938" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820938-864841_medium.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820938" class="wp-caption-text">每颗梨都要看大小、外观再分级，品质是留住老客户的关键。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14820951" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820951" style="width: 454px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820951-864828_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820951" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820951-864828_medium.jpg" alt="" width="454" b="604" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820951" class="wp-caption-text">年纪渐长仍坚持自己包装、自己贩售，新埔甘露梨多靠老客户支持。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>老客户吃的 是多年不变的品质</h2>
<p>范良淮夫妻多年来累积不少老客户，靠的不是只把梨种大，而是把每一个环节顾好。</p>
<p>从一座大水槽、一次换植株或嫁接，到采收前对成熟度的判断，都是一颗甘露梨或晶圆梨能不能甜、脆、多汁的关键。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14820944" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14820944" style="width: 451px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820944-864835_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14820944" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14820944-864835_medium.jpg" alt="" width="451" b="801" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14820944" class="wp-caption-text">一颗沉甸甸的水梨拿在手上，更能看出它的份量。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
