<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>黄氏公所颁发奖学金 教导后辈传承家族文化</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817945-DSC05351-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>7月26日，波士顿黄氏宗亲会向优秀子弟颁发奖学金。公所干部及获奖者在祖先画像前合影。（刘景烨/大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年07月27日讯】（大纪元记者刘景烨波士顿报导）7月26日中午，波士顿黄氏宗亲会向其优秀子弟颁发奖学金，并带他们敬拜祖先和了解中国传统的家族文化，期盼年轻人们未来能参与公所的服务。</p>
<p>当天在波士顿华埠的黄氏大楼活动厅，宗亲会干部们向57名优秀黄氏子弟颁发了奖学金。获奖者包括从小学到大学的各个年级学生。而在颁奖仪式之前，黄氏宗长们还教导年轻人向祖先敬香祭拜，以尊重宗族传统。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14817944" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817944" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817944-DSC05348.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817944" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817944-DSC05348-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817944" class="wp-caption-text">波士顿黄氏宗亲会向优秀子弟颁发奖学金。获奖的黄氏子弟敬拜祖先。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14817956" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817956" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817956-IMG_1380.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817956" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817956-IMG_1380-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817956" class="wp-caption-text">总共57名优秀子弟获得奖学金。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>波士顿黄氏宗亲会主席黄鹰立、黄兆祥，美东副总长黄伟健，中文秘书黄纳斯，妇女部主任黄周丽桃等干部皆到场，向优秀黄氏子弟颁发黄氏奖学金和黄国翰奖学金。</p>
<p>黄鹰立提到，黄氏宗亲会明年即将庆祝成立100周年。“我们中国文化，最重要是讲亲情，”他说，“我们公所向上要关怀前辈，向下要带领小辈。很快，你们每个人，在这里都会有机会，为我们公所的不断改善而添砖加瓦。”</p>
<figure id="attachment_14817948" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817948" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817948-DSC05362.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817948" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817948-DSC05362-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817948" class="wp-caption-text">波士顿黄氏宗亲会主席黄鹰立感谢各位干部服务，勉励年轻人参与公所事务。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14817949" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817949" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817949-DSC05388.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817949" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817949-DSC05388-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817949" class="wp-caption-text">家长们在现场祝贺获奖的黄氏子弟。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>黄鹰立回顾道，他从小参加黄氏公所的活动，那时公所还没有这么漂亮的大楼和活动厅，更没有奖学金。在一届又一届会员和干部的努力下，黄氏公所繁荣发展，“一年比一年有进步”。</p>
<p>“我们大家都很为我们这个公所自豪，都尽力去保守我们每一代的进步。希望你们年轻人和学生记住这些话，未来能帮忙，为公所做一些事。”他说。</p>
<p>黄鹰立特别谈到，8月15日，黄氏宗亲会将举办夏季派对，提供诸多美食和体育活动，还有巴士接送参与者，欢迎大家报名。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14817955" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817955" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817955-DSC05499.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817955" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817955-DSC05499-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817955" class="wp-caption-text">黄氏宗亲会妇女部主任黄周丽桃鼓励孩子们继续精进学业。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14817951" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817951" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817951-DSC05396.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817951" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817951-DSC05396-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817951" class="wp-caption-text">黄氏宗亲会主席黄兆祥（左）、黄鹰立（右）向优秀子弟颁发奖学金。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>黄周丽桃也称赞黄氏子弟们取得了优异的学习成绩，并鼓励大家“百尺竿头更进一步，明年成绩更好”。</p>
<p>干部们向每一位获奖者颁发奖金和奖状，并合影留念。随后，黄氏宗亲会还准备了茶点，招待获奖学生和家长。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14817950" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817950" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817950-DSC05389.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817950" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817950-DSC05389-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817950" class="wp-caption-text">黄氏宗亲会中文秘书黄纳斯主持颁奖仪式，介绍获奖学生。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14817954" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14817954" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817954-DSC05398.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14817954" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14817954-DSC05398-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14817954" class="wp-caption-text">美东副总长黄伟健（左）、主席黄鹰立（右）向优秀后辈颁发奖学金。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>据黄兆祥和黄纳斯介绍，公所曾经在每年的春宴期间颁发奖学金，但后来发现，很多孩子即便在领奖之后，也还是不了解黄氏宗亲会的会务，以及会址在哪里。后来大家决定在黄氏大楼举办颁奖仪式，除了发奖学金之外，也教导孩子们敬重祖先和传统。◇</p>
