<h2><span style="color: #0000ff;"><strong>2026年度特色活动与亮点</strong></span></h2>
<div class="post-entry blockquote-style-1">
<div id="penci-post-entry-inner" class="inner-post-entry entry-content">
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-369396 aligncenter" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/742031412_17979735000049377_1310265481511719876_n-819x1024.jpg" alt="" width="819" b="1024" /></p>
<h2><span style="color: #0000ff;"><strong>现场音乐与舞蹈表演</strong></span></h2>
<p>舞台将呈现多种全球音乐流派、传统与现代舞蹈，以及展现加拿大、中东和中亚等多元文化传统的特色表演。</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-369392 aligncenter" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/731111204_17978363598049377_669273615169003502_n-819x1024.jpg" alt="" width="819" b="1024" /></p>
<h2><span style="color: #0000ff;"><strong>互动工坊与文化展示</strong></span></h2>
<p>适合各种技能水平和年龄层的动手创作体验及文化讲故事环节。</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-369395 aligncenter" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/741120566_17978228994049377_77841486462342978_n-819x1024.jpg" alt="" width="819" b="1024" /></p>
<h2><span style="color: #0000ff;"><strong>艺术展览与视觉艺术</strong></span></h2>
<p>专属展区集中展现本土画家、雕塑家及多媒体艺术家的作品，分享丰富的文化遗产。</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-369397 aligncenter" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/735658407_17977948146049377_5925867586456600400_n-819x1024.jpg" alt="" width="819" b="1024" /></p>
<h2><span style="color: #0000ff;"><strong>亲子与儿童专区</strong></span></h2>
<p>为儿童及家庭打造的专属娱乐空间，包含趣味互动游戏与手工作坊。</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-369391 aligncenter" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/747513119_17978973336049377_8033589102391661572_n-819x1024.jpg" alt="" width="819" b="1024" /></p>
<h2><strong><span style="color: #0000ff;">多元文化集市与美食摊位</span></strong></h2>
<p>热闹非凡的特色集市，汇集了本地美食摊位、传统料理、手工艺术品、时尚服饰和生活用品。</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-369394 aligncenter" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/733605963_17978471961049377_5867200490687785929_n-819x1024.jpg" alt="" width="819" b="1024" />想要参与其中？</p>
<p>BaTo组织目前正面向社会招募2026文化节的本土艺术家、美食摊主、手工品牌及志愿者。您可以直接访问<span style="color: #0000ff;"><strong><a style="color: #0000ff;" href="https://www.batocultural.com/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">batocultural.com</a></strong><strong>官网</strong></span>提交申请。</p>
