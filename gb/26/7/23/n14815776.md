<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>115年度苗栗县丰水梨果品评鉴竞赛 夫妻分别获特等、头等奖</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815777-863290-600x400.jpeg" />
<h6>三湾乡农会115年度苗栗县丰水梨果品评鉴竞赛 余春英、李炎干夫妻分别获特等、头等奖。　（许享富／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年07月23日讯】（大纪元记者陈文敏台湾苗栗报导）苗栗县三湾乡农会办理115年度苗栗县丰水梨果品评鉴竞赛，以鼓励农民持续提升品质，并行销苗栗高接梨。23日上午在苗栗县政府第一办公大楼大厅举办颁奖典礼，苗栗县长钟东锦亲自颁发各项奖项，表示希望明年透过参加驻新加坡办事处所举办的“台湾周”活动，将苗栗的优质农产行销到星国，提升农民的收益。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14815778" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14815778" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815778-863291.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14815778" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815778-863291-450x267.jpeg" alt="" width="450" b="267" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14815778" class="wp-caption-text">头等奖。（许享富／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14815779" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14815779" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815779-863292.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14815779" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815779-863292-450x293.jpeg" alt="" width="450" b="293" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14815779" class="wp-caption-text">贰等奖。（许享富／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14815780" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14815780" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815780-863293.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14815780" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815780-863293-450x300.jpeg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14815780" class="wp-caption-text">贰等奖。（许享富／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>今日出席者包括苗栗县长钟东锦、县议会副议长张淑芬、三湾乡长李运光、三湾乡民代表会主席邱腾森及多位代表、苗栗县农会总干事钟金光、三湾乡农会理事长陈光洋、常务监事廖盛荣、总干事谢杏玲、大湖地区农会理事长傅松琳、农粮署北区分署苗栗办事处主任许宏德、苗栗区农业改良场副场长钟国雄及获奖果农。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14815784" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14815784" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815784-863297.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14815784" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815784-863297-450x742.jpeg" alt="" width="450" b="742" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14815784" class="wp-caption-text">钟县长表示，将参加明年举办的“台湾周”活动，县内的水梨、柚子、柑橘、稻米、红枣、东方美人茶、酸柑茶、猫里红茶等农特产推广到星国，并行销苗栗的观光资源。推广到新加坡的原因主要是分散国外市场，有助提升农民的收益。（许享富／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14815783" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14815783" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815783-863296.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14815783" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815783-863296-450x675.jpeg" alt="" width="450" b="675" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14815783" class="wp-caption-text">三湾乡长李运光欣喜乡民余春英丰水梨果夺得特等奖，且今年三湾乡参赛成绩丰硕，诚邀大家来品尝。（许享富／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14815782" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14815782" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815782-863295.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14815782" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815782-863295-450x300.jpeg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14815782" class="wp-caption-text">参等奖。（许享富／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>苗栗县长钟东锦表示，本次比赛由余春英、李炎干夫妻分别获特等、头等奖，相当难得，恭喜他们。现在的水梨栽种方式和以前不同，大部分都用套袋，农药几乎不太使用，水梨除了水分充足、甜度够外，还富含纤维素，在夏天吃上冰过的水梨相当消暑，他个人也很喜欢吃水梨。三湾梨在台湾地区就是很知名的丰水梨品牌，这次比赛也大部分由三湾农民获奖，其余还有大湖、卓兰等地梨农，希望大家能再接再厉。</p>
<p>钟县长指出，县府和驻新加坡办事处约定好参加明年举办的“台湾周”活动，希望能将县内的水梨、柚子、柑橘、稻米、红枣、东方美人茶、酸柑茶、猫里红茶等农特产推广到星国，并行销苗栗的观光资源。推广到新加坡的原因主要是分散国内市场，且当地收入较高、消费能力强，有助提升农民的收益。</p>
<p>钟县长也观察到近年来有部分梨农因年纪大，果园逐渐荒废，相当可惜，希望三湾农会能和县府农业处、改良场研拟对策，鼓励青农回乡务农。农业是苗栗的根本，除了发展观光、科技外，农业也要齐头并进，希望住在苗栗的乡亲都有幸福的感觉。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14815785" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14815785" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815785-863298.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14815785" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815785-863298-450x300.jpeg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14815785" class="wp-caption-text">现场三义乡农会展售农特产品。（许享富／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14815786" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14815786" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815786-863299.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14815786" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815786-863299-450x675.jpeg" alt="" width="450" b="675" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14815786" class="wp-caption-text">大湖地区农会大湖尊梨。（许享富／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14815787" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14815787" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815787-863300.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14815787" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14815787-863300-450x300.jpeg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14815787" class="wp-caption-text">多品种水梨，还多 一种夏季清凉饮品三湾梨玉露。（许享富／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>苗栗县政府农业处表示，苗栗县高接梨栽种面积达1千2百公顷，在梨农的悉心栽培下，除现在行销的丰水梨外，接下来蜜雪梨、新兴梨、甘露梨等品种都会陆续上市，欢迎民众多加选购。</p>
