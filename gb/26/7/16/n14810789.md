<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>伊朗战事再起 欧洲航空业恐面临整并压力</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/06/id14279290-GettyImages-1283335136.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>【大纪元2026年07月16日讯】（大纪元记者吴瑞昌综合报导）随着中东冲突再度升温并推高油价，让许多航空业承受巨大压力。部分航空业投资人与业界高层认为，航空业正<span style="color:#000000">面临洗牌压力，</span>其中财务体质较弱的欧洲航空公司恐面临破产、重组或收购。</p><p class="cjk">目前，拥有<span style="font-family:新细明体, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Calibri, sans-serif">30</span></span></span>年历史的英国廉价航空易捷航空（<span style="font-family:新细明体, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Calibri, sans-serif">easyJet</span></span></span>），将以低于疫情前高峰的估值卖给美国的财团，市场正关注欧洲航空业是否将进入一波重组与整并潮。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p class="cjk">拉脱维亚的波罗的海航空（<span style="font-family:新细明体, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Calibri, sans-serif">airBaltic</span></span></span>）正寻求短期融资以避免违约；波兰航空（<span style="font-family:新细明体, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Calibri, sans-serif">LOT</span></span></span>）多年来一直被视为可能的整并目标；挪威大西洋航空（<span style="font-family:新细明体, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Calibri, sans-serif">Norse Atlantic</span></span></span>）自上市以来股价已暴跌至接近零的水准，当下正在进行战略评估。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p class="cjk">对此，波罗的海航空发言人拒绝发表评论。波兰航空表示，其过去几年的表现证明了该公司商业模式与长期战略的实力；挪威大西洋航空未回应置评请求。</p><h2 class="cjk"><b>专家预测几家航空业者会重组</b></h2><p serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Calibri, sans-serif">EMEA</span></span></span>）地区主管博库姆（<span style="font-family:新细明体, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Calibri, sans-serif">Barema Bocoum</span></span></span>）向路透社透露，“我们正针对重组情况向大约四到五家欧洲大型航空公司推广重组方案。”</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p class="cjk">前英国航空执行长、代表全球<span style="font-family:新细明体, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Calibri, sans-serif">370</span></span></span>多家航空公司、占全球航空客运量约<span style="font-family:新细明体, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Calibri, sans-serif">85%</span></span></span>的国际航空运输协会（<span style="font-family:新细明体, serif"><span lang="en-US"><span style="font-family:Calibri, Air</span></span></span>）强调自身流动性充足，但有分析师警告该公司资产负债表体质相对脆弱，可能使其成为航空业的整并目标。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex
