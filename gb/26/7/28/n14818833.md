<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>南竿津沙聚落 石屋、沙滩与在地海味</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818907-864142_medium-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>修复后的闽东式石屋融入山林，保留石墙、木窗与瓦顶等传统元素。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年07月28日讯】（大纪元记者廖俪芬马祖报导）夏天到马祖旅行，南竿津沙聚落是一个适合慢慢停留的地方。沿着山路进入聚落，眼前是一栋栋依着坡地而建的石头屋，巷道不宽，转过弯又是另一幅景色。第一次来的游客看到眼前的聚落与海湾，忍不住直呼：“好美哦！”</p>
<p>津沙原名“金沙”，因沙滩在阳光下闪闪发亮而得名。这里早年曾是南竿第二大村，居民多以捕鱼为生；后来渔源减少、人口外移，反而让不少老屋与渔村样貌保留下来。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14818903" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14818903" style="width: 601px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818903-864146_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14818903" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818903-864146_medium.jpg" alt="" width="601" b="451" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14818903" class="wp-caption-text">从聚落望向海湾，红瓦、彩绘墙与蓝色海面构成津沙的日常风景。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14818897" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14818897" style="width: 454px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818897-864158_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14818897" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818897-864158_medium.jpg" alt="" width="454" b="605" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14818897" class="wp-caption-text">夏天到马祖旅行，南竿津沙聚落是一个适合慢慢停留的地方。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14818896" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14818896" style="width: 453px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818896-864165_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14818896" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818896-864165_medium.jpg" alt="" width="453" b="684" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14818896" class="wp-caption-text">津沙聚落的窄巷穿梭于石屋之间，老屋、木窗与生活盆栽，保留渔村日常风景。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>走进坑道 再看依山而建的石头屋</h2>
<p>来到津沙，可以先走进坑道看看。外面虽是夏天，进入坑道后立刻感觉凉爽，与户外形成明显对比。坑道留下马祖过去的战地痕迹，走出坑道，再回头看聚落里的花岗岩石屋，更能感受到这座岛屿不同时期留下来的生活记忆。</p>
<p>有些石屋经过整理，成为民宿、餐馆及特产店；有些仍保留岁月留下的斑驳痕迹。沿着巷子散步，不需要赶着找景点，看看老屋、屋瓦与石墙，就能消磨不少时间。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14818905" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14818905" style="width: 451px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818905-864144_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14818905" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818905-864144_medium.jpg" alt="" width="451" b="801" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14818905" class="wp-caption-text">津沙聚落的石屋沿着山坡分布，红瓦与石墙藏在大片绿意之中。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14818901" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14818901" style="width: 452px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818901-864147_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14818901" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818901-864147_medium.jpg" alt="" width="452" b="602" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14818901" class="wp-caption-text">石屋与石阶顺着坡地而建，是津沙聚落最具特色的建筑景观。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>傍晚来到沙滩 有人玩SUP、有人晒太阳</h2>
<p>津沙沙滩到了傍晚最热闹。有人带着SUP下水，在平静的海面上慢慢划行；有人躺在沙滩上晒日光浴，也有人三五成群坐着聊天。没有太多吵杂的游乐设施，只有海浪、风声与游客的笑声。</p>
<p>马祖夏天阳光虽强，津沙靠海，傍晚吹起风来十分凉快。坐在岸边看着天色慢慢改变，就算什么也不做，也不觉得浪费时间。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14818902" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14818902" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818902-864148_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14818902" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818902-864148_medium.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="600" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14818902" class="wp-caption-text">傍晚的津沙沙滩凉风徐徐，游客坐在岸边聊天、戏水，享受慢步调。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14818906" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14818906" style="width: 451px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818906-864143_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14818906" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818906-864143_medium.jpg" alt="" width="451" b="801" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14818906" class="wp-caption-text">赤脚站在津沙沙滩，吹着海风，安静欣赏傍晚海景。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14818895" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14818895" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818895-864166_medium.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14818895" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14818895-864166_medium.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="774" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14818895" class="wp-caption-text">穿过聚落堤坊便是津沙沙滩，海湾近在眼前，散步看海十分方便。（廖俪芬／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>淡菜、鱼面、红糟炒饭 吃一顿马祖晚餐</h2>
<p>玩过沙滩，晚餐可以到津沙小馆。当季淡菜带着海味，简单料理就很好吃；红糟炒饭则有马祖常见的红糟香气，米饭炒得均匀，颜色也很抢眼。走了一下午，几道热菜上桌，很快就被大家分食一空。</p>
<p>离开前，再到于妈妈特产店逛逛。架上有不同种类的鱼干及石花菜，既是马祖人熟悉的海味，也适合带回家慢慢品尝。从坑道、石头屋走到沙滩，再以淡菜和红糟炒饭收尾，津沙的行程不复杂，却很有马祖味。</p>

<h2>骑车下坡要小心</h2>
<p>前往津沙的道路坡度很陡，也有急弯。骑机车下坡时不要催油门，应放慢速度，以前后刹车平稳控制车速，切勿突然急煞。第一次到南竿、对路况没有把握的游客，搭公车或计程车会更轻松。</p>
