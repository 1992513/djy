<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>吃出好气血：不是贪吃！肚子饿多是假象！</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811231-7919eb9bc6e00f86826617ab566f42f6-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>分清真假食欲，顺应脾胃的生理规律，不仅能帮你轻松维持体重，更是通往“气血和调、百病不生”的健康捷径。（大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年07月17日讯】在现代生活里，我们常常刚吃完饭没多久，嘴巴又馋了；或者明明不饿，看到零食就忍不住伸手。很多人以为这是自己“意志力薄弱”或“贪吃”，但在传统中医的眼中，<strong>这种“明明肚子不空，却总想吃东西”的感觉，大部分都是“假性食欲”！</strong></p>
<p><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811181-4efdb023dd5c4ce3b9566d21157abf28.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811181 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811181-4efdb023dd5c4ce3b9566d21157abf28.jpg" alt="" width="359" b="448" /></a></p>
<p>中医讲求“脾胃为后天之本”，胃主受纳（接收食物），脾主运化（消化并转化营养）。当这套消化系统的平衡被打破时，身体就会发出错误的“饥饿讯号”。</p>
<p><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811182-70fca1022047fe9029f7ed01e966b504.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811182 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811182-70fca1022047fe9029f7ed01e966b504.png" alt="" width="367" b="458" /></a></p>
<p>老中医常说：<strong>“胃不和，则食不知节；脾不健，则假饿频生。”</strong>究竟是哪些体质问题在背后操纵你的假性食欲？我们从传统中医的视角来一探究竟。<ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811186-807993a691b039f7ca45d86029a3a5ea.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811186 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811186-807993a691b039f7ca45d86029a3a5ea.jpg" alt="" width="380" b="475" /></a></p>
<h2><strong>一、为什么会产生“假性食欲”？老中医剖析三种常见体质</strong></h2>
<p><strong>1.胃火炽盛——“胃热杀谷”，吃再多也像没吃</strong></p>
<p>《黄帝内经》提到：“胃热则消谷善饥。”这里的“消谷善饥”，指的就是胃里的火气太旺，把进来的食物过快地“烧”掉了，导致人极易感到饥饿。</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>成因：</strong>长期熬夜、喜食辣味、煎炸高热量食物，或是情绪压抑化火。</li>
<li><strong>假饿表现：</strong>吃完正餐没多久又饿了，特别想吃冰镇饮品或重口味食物。同时常伴随<strong>口臭、口干舌燥、牙龈肿痛、大便干结</strong>等症状。</li>
</ul>
<p><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811222-4521a0b9f23808a1435b69ac1d93a126.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811222 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811222-4521a0b9f23808a1435b69ac1d93a126.png" alt="" width="380" b="475" /></a></p>
<p><strong>2.</strong><strong>脾虚湿阻</strong><strong>——</strong><strong>“脾不运化”，大脑误以为营养不够</strong></p>
<p>中医认为，脾气虚弱时，运化功能减退，吃进去的食物无法转化为气血精微（营养），反而聚湿生痰。此时身体的细胞其实“缺营养”，大脑接收到信号，就会不断发出“继续吃！”的指令。</p>
<li><strong>成因：</strong>饮食不节制、常吃生冷寒凉、久坐少动伤脾。</li>
<li><strong>假饿表现：</strong>嘴里总是淡淡无味，特别想吃甜食或高碳水化合物。伴随<strong>吃一点就胀气、身体沉重易累、大便黏马桶、舌头边缘有齿痕</strong>。</li>
<p><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811223-fd8ab57d502fa48da364e503d7fc17fc.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811223 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811223-fd8ab57d502fa48da364e503d7fc17fc.png" alt="" width="380" b="475" /></a></p>
<p><strong>3.</strong><strong>肝郁气滞</strong><strong>——</strong><strong>“情志不遂”，借吃解忧的“肝胃不和”</strong></p>
<p>中医认为“肝主疏泄”，调控着我们的精神情绪。如果长期压力大、焦虑、生气，肝气郁结就会“木克土”，横逆犯胃。这时的“饿”，其实不是胃饿，而是“心饿”，也就是现代医学说的情绪性饮食（EmotionalEating）。</p>
<li><strong>成因：</strong>工作压力大、情绪长期压抑或波动大。</li>
<li><strong>假饿表现：</strong>一烦躁、加班或受气就想吃东西（尤其是高油高糖的垃圾食物），吃完又充满罪恶感。常伴随<strong>胸闷、喜叹气、两胁（肋骨两侧）胀痛</strong>。</li>
<h2><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811224-fb6a573811f70326a7183e4a3c69fc11.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811224 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811224-fb6a573811f70326a7183e4a3c69fc11.png" alt="" width="380" b="475" /></a></h2>
<h2><strong>二、老中医传授：</strong><strong>3</strong><strong>招教你破解“假性食欲”</strong></h2>
<li><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811216-baf1f0780bfa10cc3dc9c7c933e6c1b6.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811216 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811216-baf1f0780bfa10cc3dc9c7c933e6c1b6.jpg" alt="" width="380" b="475" /></a></li>
<p>当你下次又感到“嘴馋/假饿”时，不妨试试以下中医养生小妙招，帮你的脾胃按下一键重置：</p>
<p><strong>1.先喝杯“温水”，给胃降降火</strong></p>
<p><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811200-40331d2b03c192ba13167c215f337dde.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811200 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811200-40331d2b03c192ba13167c215f337dde.png" alt="" width="380" b="475" /></a></p>
<p>假性食欲往往是因为“胃热”或身体轻度缺水。感觉饿时，先慢慢饮用200ml的温开水（切忌冰水，冰水伤脾气）。如果喝完水15分钟后饥饿感消失，说明刚才只是“假饿”。</p>
<p><strong>2.</strong><strong>按压穴位，精准抑制假食欲</strong></p>
<li><strong>中脘穴（胃的保健大穴）：</strong>位于肚脐直上4寸（约胸骨下端与肚脐连线的中点）。用指腹顺时针揉按2-3分钟，能和胃健脾，平抑胃火。</li>
<li><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811217-b001ba64c5b9038efb3ed1522c1c1ac6.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811217 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811217-b001ba64c5b9038efb3ed1522c1c1ac6.jpg" alt="" width="380" b="475" /></a></li>
<li><strong>内关穴（疏肝理气、抚平情绪）：</strong>位于手腕横纹向上2寸（约三指宽）两条筋之间。按压至有酸胀感，能有效缓解因压力引起的“情绪性假饿”。</li>
<li><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811218-034e8dfa3f2346617107af9c698036d0.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811218 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811218-034e8dfa3f2346617107af9c698036d0.jpg" alt="" width="381" b="476" /></a></li>
<li><strong>足三里（调理脾胃第一穴）：</strong>位于外膝眼下3寸（四指宽）。常按能补益脾胃，改善脾虚引起的异常食欲。</li>
<li><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811219-56f155f2f611360a6d90d2417b8276ae.png"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811219 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811219-56f155f2f611360a6d90d2417b8276ae.png" alt="" width="380" b="475" /></a></li>
<p><strong>3.</strong><strong>养生茶饮调理</strong></p>
<p><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811209-35739bb119e15e3e16db728c3ffffd56.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811209 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811209-35739bb119e15e3e16db728c3ffffd56.jpg" alt="" width="395" b="494" /></a></p>
<li><strong>针对“胃火过旺型”（总是想吃重口味）：</strong>
<li><strong>麦冬石斛茶：</strong>麦冬6g、石斛6g、养阴生津、清胃泻火。<ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811206-1c38a4e75964b9201d375924e0e3f4fd.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811206 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811206-1c38a4e75964b9201d375924e0e3f4fd.jpg" alt="" width="387" b="483" /></a><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811208-0768fbb9805a255c6e63e0aaad2bf9a7.jpg"><br />
</a></li>
</li>
<li><strong>针对“脾虚湿重型”（总是想吃甜食</strong><strong>/</strong><strong>碳水）：</strong>
<li><strong>陈皮茯苓茶：</strong>陈皮3g、茯苓6g、炒白术6g，干山楂2片，煮水饮用。能健脾祛湿、理气和中。</li>
<li><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811207-652ea66bd9ea86db59f708dab29faee7.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811207 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811207-652ea66bd9ea86db59f708dab29faee7.jpg" alt="" width="381" b="476" /></a></li>
<li><strong>针对“肝郁压抑型”（一烦躁就想暴饮暴食）：</strong>
<li><strong>玫瑰薄荷茶：</strong>干玫瑰花5朵、薄荷叶3片，枸杞5粒，温水冲泡。能疏肝解郁、理气和胃。</li>
<li><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811208-0768fbb9805a255c6e63e0aaad2bf9a7.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-14811208 aligncenter" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14811208-0768fbb9805a255c6e63e0aaad2bf9a7.jpg" alt="" width="383" b="479" /></a></li>
<h2><strong>结语</strong></h2>
<p>中医常讲：<strong>“饮食有节，起居有常。”</strong></p>
<p>下次再感到饥饿时，不妨先停下来听听身体的声音：这是胃真的空了，还是你的“脾”在呼救、“肝”在发泄？分清真假食欲，顺应脾胃的生理规律，不仅能帮你轻松维持体重，更是通往“气血和调、百病不生”的健康捷径。</p>
