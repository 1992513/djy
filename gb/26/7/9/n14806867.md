<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>美FDA近期为何召回15项食品 一文看懂</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14621766-EpochImages-5992458442-1080x720-1.webp" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>【大纪元2026年07月10日讯】（大纪元记者李芳编译报导）美国食品药物管理局（FDA）自今年6月以来陆续公布多起食品召回事件，受影响产品涵盖零食、乳制品、冷冻水果、植物性食品、婴儿配方奶粉及保健食品等。综观6月以来的召回案例，细菌污染与未标示过敏原仍是两大主要原因，另外也有产品因可能混入塑胶碎片而下架。</p><h2>沙门氏菌污染牵连多项食品</h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>今年5月底至6月初，多家公司因使用可能受污染的California Dairies Inc.奶粉原料，陆续宣布召回产品。FDA并为此设立“重大产品召回<ahref="https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/major-product-recalls" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="health-article-hover-class">Major Product Recalls</a>）”专页，持续更新受影响商品。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:423px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14801839-Utz-image-Zapps-and-Dirty.jpeg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14801839-Utz-image-Zapps-and-Dirty.jpeg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14801839-Utz-image-Zapps-and-Dirty.jpeg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14801839-Utz-image-Zapps-and-Dirty.jpeg" alt="部分召回的Zapp&#x27;s和Dirty品牌薯片示意图。（图片来源：FDA网站 大纪元制图）" width="423" b="282"/></picture></a><figcaption>部分召回的Zapp's和Dirty品牌薯片示意图。（图片来源：FDA网站 大纪元制图）</figcaption></figure></div><p>其中包括：<br/>? 五起司面包<ahref="https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/champion-foods-recalls-some-batches-motor-city-pizza-co-5-cheese-bread-because-possible-health-risk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="health-article-hover-class">Motor City Pizza Co. 5 Cheese Bread</a>），因可能受到沙门氏菌（Salmonella）污染而于6月1日发布召回。<br/>? Utz旗下<ahref="https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/utz-quality-foods-llc-issues-voluntary-recall-certain-limited-varieties-zapps-and-dirty-potato-chips" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="health-article-hover-class">Zapp&#x27;s及Dirty</a>品牌部分洋芋片，也因相同原因于5月发布召回，相关产品持续列入FDA重大召回专页。<br/>? TNVitamins于6月26日召回<ahref="https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/total-nutrition-inc-recalls-organic-moringa-products-following-supplier-initiated-recall-due" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="health-article-hover-class">100% Organic Moringa Capsules and Powder</a>，同样因可能遭沙门氏菌污染。</p><div><figure style="max-width:485px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14806879-tn_organic_moringa_powder_side_-_lot_2782_exp_5-2028-djy.jpeg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14806879-tn_organic_moringa_powder_side_-_lot_2782_exp_5-2028-djy.jpeg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14806879-tn_organic_moringa_powder_side_-_lot_2782_exp_5-2028-djy.jpeg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14806879-tn_organic_moringa_powder_side_-_lot_2782_exp_5-2028-djy.jpeg" alt="TNVitamins于6月26日召回100% Organic Moringa Capsules and Powder。（图片来源：FDA网站 大纪元制图）" width="485" b="328"/></picture></a><figcaption>TNVitamins于6月26日召回100% Organic Moringa Capsules and Powder。（图片来源：FDA网站 大纪元制图）</figcaption></figure></div><p>沙门氏菌可能导致腹泻、发烧及腹部绞痛，幼童、老年人及免疫力较弱者感染后可能出现较严重并发症。</p><h2>李斯特菌、大肠杆菌污染持续出现</h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>除沙门氏菌外，FDA近期也发布多起因其它病原菌污染而召回的食品。</p><p>6月26日，La Ceiba Foods Latin Market Inc.召回<ahref="https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/la-ceiba-foods-latin-market-inc-recalls-cottage-cheese-products-because-possible-health-risk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="health-article-hover-class">La Colonia、Selectos Latinos及Clover Hill</a>品牌多款Requesón（软质乳酪），原因是可能受到李斯特菌（Listeria monocytogenes）污染。李斯特菌对孕妇、老年人及免疫功能低下者尤其危险。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>7月6日，FDA公布GreenWise有机冷冻蓝莓<ahref="https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/frutas-y-hortalizas-del-sur-sa-initiates-recall-frozen-greenwise-organic-iqf-blueberries-due" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="health-article-hover-class">GreenWise Organic IQF Frozen Blueberries</a>）召回公告，原因为可能受到大肠杆菌O145污染，产品销往美国东南部八州。</p><div><figure style="max-width:385px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14806884-Frozen-GreenWise-Organic-IQF-Blueberries.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="374"/></picture></a><figcaption>7月6日，FDA公布GreenWise Organic IQF Frozen Blueberries。（图片来源：FDA网站）</figcaption></figure></div><p>此外，Nara Organics也于6月13日召回全部批次婴儿配方奶粉，原因为可能受到“肉毒杆菌<ahref="https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/nara-organics-recalls-all-lots-nara-infant-formula-because-possible-health-risk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="health-article-hover-class">Clostridium botulinum</a>）”污染。由于肉毒杆菌可能造成严重神经系统疾病，此案受到高度关注。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><h2>未标示过敏原仍是最常见召回原因之一</h2><p>除了微生物污染，未标示过敏原（Undeclared Allergens）仍是FDA近期最常见的召回原因之一。</p><p>自6月底以来，FDA陆续公布多起因标示问题而召回的食品，包括：<br/>? High Valley Orchard巧克力葡萄干（未标示花生）<br/>? Bakr Cookie Dough（未标示大豆）<br/>? Fusia Kimchi &amp; Tofu Kimbap（未标示鲔鱼）<br/>? 部分韩式生鱼片产品（未标示小麦、大豆及芝麻）<br/>? Ola-Ola Pounded Yam（未标示牛奶）</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>FDA指出，对特定食物过敏者而言，即使摄取少量未标示的过敏原，也可能引发严重甚至危及生命的过敏反应。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><h2>异物混入也是重要风险</h2><p>除污染与标示问题外，食品中混入异物也是近期召回原因之一。</p><p>MorningStar Farms于6月22日主动召回两款植物性食品，包括Buffalo Chik&#x27;n Nuggets及Hot &amp; Spicy Sausage Patties，原因是产品可能含有塑胶碎片。</p><h2>FDA今年6月以来食品召回重点</h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><div class="table-responsive"><table style="height:417px" width="741" cellSpacing="0" cellPadding="0"><tbody><tr><td class="td1" valign="middle">
