<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>围棋：人类再胜AI！申真谞成功复仇KataGo</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14812770-532653work-20260719-003-vv.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
