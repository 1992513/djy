<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>中国大陆三周发生81次地震 5级以上4次</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/07/id14812113-ca5837b24fb78f8ebf8750b8809ea79b.jpeg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
