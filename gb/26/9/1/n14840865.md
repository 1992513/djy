<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>【厨娘香Q秀】中式海鲜浓汤和酥炸季节时蔬</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14840871-2018-06-19-5b287c60194e7-780x438-169.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>FDA优良厨师金帽奖杨俊民，采用本土南瓜的甜度加上栗子南瓜的厚度，分享中式黄金海鲜浓汤，口感鲜美还带着淡淡Q软南瓜香味。而酥炸季节时蔬搭柠檬红茶酱，杨俊民老师特别提到柠檬红茶酱，要加一点盐，并放入冷藏冰镇后再加蜂蜜。葱油可使用葱白或红葱头。另外调酥炸面糊时，里面可加些沙拉油，以保持食物炸后的酥脆感。</p><h2>第一道~中式黄金海鲜浓汤</h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><h3>食材：</h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>南瓜800g、洋葱丁60g、培根丁30g、杏鲍菇丁100g 、虾仁 100g、蟹管肉60g、大骨蔬菜高汤1000g</p><p>A.调味料：无盐奶油2匙、黑胡椒粒少许</p><p>B.调味料：盐巴2T、白糖4T、奶水80cc、自制葱油适量、太白粉2T</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><h3>作法：</h3><p>1.本土南瓜及栗子南瓜洗净去籽切片(不去皮)入蒸笼蒸软再压制成泥状。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>2.烫杏鲍菇及蟹管肉、虾仁。</p><p>3.用无盐奶油炒香培根丁及洋葱丁，再加些黑胡椒粒，加入大骨蔬菜高汤。再加入南瓜泥煮沸。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p>4.浓汤加少许盐拌匀后、确定南瓜泥打散了，加入奶水。并以太白粉勾芡，起锅前滴入少许葱油提味，盛入盅内备用。</p><p>5.所有配料川烫至熟，放在浓汤上面即可。</p><h2>第二道~酥炸季节时蔬搭柠檬红茶酱</h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><h3>食材：</h3><p>鲜香菇2朵、红黄甜椒30g、茄子30g、南瓜30g、玉米笋20g、面线少许</p><p>A.调味料：酥炸粉6T、沙拉油1T、水适量</p><p>B.调味料：红茶茶包1包、糖3T、盐巴少许、新鲜柠檬汁60cc、蜂蜜(最后放) 2T、开水250cc、太白粉水3T</p><div
