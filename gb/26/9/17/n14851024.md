<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>国象奥赛首轮：印度名将埃里盖西爆冷落败</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851030-55532122411_527f22a98c_k.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
