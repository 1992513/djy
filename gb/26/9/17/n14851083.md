<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>读者调查：数据中心发展与电力成本问题</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851094-2026-09-11-epoch-poll-outside.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
