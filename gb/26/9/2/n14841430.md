<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>福建点头镇洪水 “白茶第一街”被淹（视频）</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14841481-639701d392a917e557ed3388e4bcf930.png" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
