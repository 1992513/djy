<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>国民党六立委访法拉盛与台侨交流</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853012-194501-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>国民党立委与纽约台侨合影。（黄小堂／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年09月20日讯】（大纪元记者黄小堂纽约报导）中国国民党六位立法委员徐巧芯、罗智强、洪孟楷、罗廷玮、黄健豪及葛如钧，9月19日晚间出席在纽约市法拉盛御庭酒楼举行的晚宴。活动由中国国民党驻美东支部主办，现场席开逾20桌，吸引纽约及长岛地区侨胞与各界人士参与。</p>
<figure id="14853020" aria-describedby="caption-14853020" style="width: 601px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><ahref=" https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853020-194509-450x300.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <img decoding="async" class="" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853020-194509-450x300.jpg" alt="国民党驻长岛分部在法拉盛欢迎6位立委来访。" width="601" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-14853020" class="wp-caption-text">国民党驻长岛分部在法拉盛欢迎6位立委来访。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="14853019" aria-describedby="caption-14853019" style="width: 601px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><ahref=" https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853019-194508-450x300.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <img decoding="async" class="" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853019-194508-450x300.jpg" alt="国民党驻长岛分部在法拉盛欢迎6位立委来访。" width="601" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-14853019" class="wp-caption-text">国民党驻长岛分部在法拉盛欢迎6位立委来访。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="14853016" aria-describedby="caption-14853016" style="width: 599px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><ahref=" https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853016-194505-450x300.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <img decoding="async" class="" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853016-194505-450x300.jpg" alt="国民党立委与驻长岛分部成员合影。" width="599" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-14853016" class="wp-caption-text">国民党立委与驻长岛分部成员合影。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>晚宴以“传递立法院立场?共创台湾新未来”为主题。六位立委进入会场时，受到现场侨胞欢迎，随后安排唱国歌、贵宾致词及立委问政分享等环节，并就台美关系、台湾政局及2026年地方选举展望与侨胞交流。</p>
<p>中国国民党驻美东支部常委孙运悌致欢迎词时表示，六位立委在台湾立法院的表现有目共睹，并向访美团表达欢迎。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14853009" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14853009" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853009-194499.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14853009" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853009-194499-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14853009" class="wp-caption-text">国民党立委徐巧芯（右五）与中国国民党驻美东支部常委孙运悌（右四）互赠纪念礼品。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>谈及年底地方选举，孙运悌指出，部分县市选情面临挑战，呼吁国民党支持者返台助选。她强调，期盼海内外凝聚力量，共同关心台湾未来发展。、</p>
<p>活动现场也介绍多位侨界代表及嘉宾，包括前纽约市议员顾雅明，以及中国国民党驻纽约分部与驻长岛分部、纽约大陈同乡会等来自纽约的侨团与同乡会人士。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14853018" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14853018" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853018-194507.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14853018" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853018-194507-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14853018" class="wp-caption-text">纽约大陈同乡会在法拉盛欢迎国民党6位立委来访。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14853017" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14853017" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853017-194506.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14853017" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853017-194506-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14853017" class="wp-caption-text">国民党立委与纽约大陈同乡会成员合影。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>六位立委分别发表谈话后，主办单位安排晚宴及逐桌交流，让侨胞有机会与立委近距离合影。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14853015" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14853015" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853015-194504.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14853015" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853015-194504-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14853015" class="wp-caption-text">国民党立委与纽约台侨合影。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14853014" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14853014" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853014-194503.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14853014" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853014-194503-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14853014" class="wp-caption-text">国民党立委与纽约台侨合影。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14853013" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14853013" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853013-194502.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14853013" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853013-194502-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14853013" class="wp-caption-text">国民党立委与纽约台侨合影。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>

<p>晚宴另安排抽奖活动，奖品包括六位立委签名的纽约纪念品、立法院纪念礼品，以及附有多位国民党政治人物签名的纪念品，为当晚侨胞交流增添互动气氛。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14853008" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14853008" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853008-194498.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14853008" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853008-194498-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14853008" class="wp-caption-text">台侨获赠有国民党立委签名的纪念品。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14853007" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14853007" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853007-194497.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14853007" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14853007-194497-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14853007" class="wp-caption-text">国民党立委致赠台侨立法院纪念礼品。（黄小堂／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>此次国民党立委访美行程自9月9日展开，先后走访旧金山、洛杉矶、华盛顿及纽约，透过拜会美方人士及侨界交流，传达国民党在国会与政策议题上的立场。</p>
