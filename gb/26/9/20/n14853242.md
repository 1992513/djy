<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>只需一种蔬菜！就能轻松做出开胃小菜</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2017/12/1606131656272483.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr><span style="font-weight:400"><strong>编者按：</strong>只要准备一种蔬菜，不用复杂的步骤，花费几分钟就能轻松做出美味又开胃的小菜。</span></p><h2><span style="color:#008000">一种蔬菜就能做一道小菜</span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><h3><span style="color:#0000ff">叶菜类</span></h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2020/04/shutterstock_1329517565.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="200"/></picture></a><figcaption>小松菜。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><p><span style="color:#000000"><strong>凉拌青菜（2人份）</strong></span></p><p>锅中放入水100?、1/2大匙酒、1/2小匙日式高汤粉、味醂及酱油各25?后开火煮滚，做成凉拌酱汁。用另一个锅子将水煮滚，加少许盐后放入一把叶菜（菠菜、小松菜等）烫熟。青菜冲冷水后将水捏干 ，切成约5㎝长。汁与青菜装进大碗中拌匀，放30分钟以上使青菜入味。</p><p><strong>胡麻酱拌青菜（2人份）</strong></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>用锅子烧开加了少许盐的水，烫熟一把青菜。青菜冲冷水后将水捏干，切成约 5㎝长。在大碗中混合无调味的胡麻酱及砂糖各2大匙、酱油1小匙、适量白芝麻，再加入青菜拌匀。</p><h3><span style="color:#0000ff">大白菜</span></h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2020/04/shutterstock_475799716.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="200"/></picture></a><figcaption>大白菜。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>西餐风炖菜（2人份）</strong></p><p>锅中放入1/8颗大白菜，再加水300?与一块高汤块，用小于锅面的盖子或铝箔纸直接盖在大白菜上煮约12分钟，然后关火放凉。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p><strong>韩式凉拌大白菜（2人份）</strong></p><p>将三片大白菜切成1㎝宽，用微波炉以500w加热3分钟。放凉后用手挤掉水分。在大碗中混合约1㎝的蒜泥（市售蒜泥酱）、少许盐、1/2小匙鸡汤粉、1 大匙麻油、适量白芝麻，再放入大白菜拌匀。</p><h3><span style="color:#0000ff">洋葱</span></h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>洋葱一颗去皮后切掉根部 。在小锅中装入水500?、2大匙鲣鱼酱油露（3倍浓缩）、1小匙日式高汤粉，并放入洋葱。用小于锅面的盖子或铝箔纸直接盖在洋葱上，以小火煮约30分钟后关火放凉。也适合冷冻。</p><p><strong>味噌洋葱（2人份）</strong></p><p>洋葱一颗去皮后切薄 。在食物保鲜袋中装入味噌100g、洋葱并仔细搓揉。于冰箱中冷藏一晚，让味噌入味后即可使用。适合冷冻，是搭配即食味噌汤或炒菜时的好帮手。</p><h3><span style="color:#0000ff">胡萝卜</span></h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div 长的胡萝卜丝。锅中放沙拉油加热，然后翻炒1/2条辣椒。辣椒变色后放入胡萝卜，以中火炒约2分钟。加2大匙酒、1 /2大匙砂糖，拌炒约1分钟。最后加1/2小匙酱油，炒至汤汁收干便完成。</p><h3><span style="color:#0000ff">马铃薯</span></h3><div><figure style="max-width:300px"
