<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>习访美 法轮功吁停止迫害 举办烛光夜悼</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14857135-EpochImages-8965338603.jpeg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />

class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px"
