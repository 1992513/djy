<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>枪支安全讲座聚焦存放 专家解析自卫法规</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859436-3-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>经济发展委员会主席David Peters（左）和活动主持人Jonathan Shih合影。（陆希／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年09月29日讯】（大纪元记者陆希休斯顿报导）由西南管理区（SWMD）主办的“第四届年度枪支安全讲座暨实务工作坊”，9月26日下午1点至4点在Shriner Center举行，邀请检察官、警员及律师从枪械安全、家庭防护及德州自卫法律等面向，提醒枪支拥有者建立正确安全观念与法律意识。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859432" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859432" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859432-01.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859432" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859432-01-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859432" class="wp-caption-text">休斯顿警察局汽车窃盗组警官Tracy Hicks主讲枪械保管。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>中国城治安委员会主席Jonathan Shih主持活动并介绍讲者。主讲人包括哈里斯县地检署保护令首席检察官Vanessa Goosen、休斯顿警察局汽车窃盗组Tracy Hicks警官，以及Armed Attorneys律师Edwin Walker；西南管理区公共安全委员会主席Peter Aquero、经济发展委员会主席David Peters、公共安全主任Victor Beserra、汽车盗窃部门主管Nhatthien Nguyen等亦出席。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859438" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859438" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859438-5.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859438" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859438-5-600x462.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="462" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859438" class="wp-caption-text">Jonathan强调需要持续训练，手指离开扳机、安全习惯。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>讲座首先强调“负责任持枪”并非取得枪械即可，而是需要持续训练、安全习惯与法律知识。Jonathan Shih介绍五项基本安全原则，包括把每把枪视为上膛、枪口保持安全方向、非准备射击时手指离开扳机、确认目标及周遭环境，以及切勿试图接住掉落的枪械。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859434" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859434" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859434-2.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859434" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859434-2-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859434" class="wp-caption-text">汽车盗窃部门主管Nhatthien Nguyen（左）、Tracy Hicks警官合影。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>在枪械保管方面，Tracy Hicks建议使用可固定于墙面或地面的保险箱，尤其家中有儿童或访客时，更应防止未经授权接触。Tracy Hicks指出，枪械不宜长期存放车内；若确有需要，应放置于固定式车用保险箱。讲者并提醒，休斯顿仍有大量枪械从车辆遭窃，车主应避免使用容易被辨识的枪械标志或贴纸，以降低成为窃贼目标的风险。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859437" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859437" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859437-4.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859437" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859437-4-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859437" class="wp-caption-text">哈里斯县地检署保护令首席检察官Vanessa Goosen。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>家庭暴力与枪械安全也是讲座重点。Vanessa Goosen说明，保护令可由执法机关执行，适用于家庭暴力、跟踪、性侵、人口贩运及住宅入室窃盗等情况。哈里斯县居民可免费申请，申请不以收入或移民身份为条件；保护令生效期间，受命人不得持有枪械，相关枪械须依规定交由当局保管。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859440" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859440" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859440-6.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859440" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859440-6-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859440" class="wp-caption-text">Armed Attorneys律师Edwin Walker。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Edwin Walker则解析德州自卫法，包括“城堡原则”及“坚守阵地”等规定。他提醒，是否使用武力仍须依具体情境及当时合理信念判断；路怒事件尤其应避免升高冲突，能离开现场、保持距离并报警时，应优先采取这些方式。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859441" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859441" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859441-7.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859441" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859441-7-600x450.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859441" class="wp-caption-text">活动结束民众排队依票券，领取枪支安全保险盒。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Jonathan Shih最后强调，枪械安全不应止于一次讲座，持枪者仍应接受专业训练，熟悉枪械操作、故障处理及德州相关法律；准确使用枪械不仅是安全问题，也是使用者必须承担的法律与道德责任。</p>
