<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>休斯顿FASCA携手TCML欢庆台湾中秋节</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859383-02-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>休斯顿FASCA与中华文化学院（ICC）台湾华语文学习中心（TCML）合影。（陆希／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年09月29日讯】（大纪元记者陆希休斯顿报导）休斯顿FASCA（海外青年文化志工）与中华文化学院（ICC）台湾华语文学习中心（TCML）共同主办的“台湾中秋节”活动，9月26日中午在侨教中心大礼堂举行，透过表演、文化体验、游戏及美食，带领不同族裔民众认识台湾中秋节传统，吸引许多家庭一起参加，现场气氛热闹。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859382" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859382" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859382-01.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859382" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859382-01-600x439.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="439" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859382" class="wp-caption-text">高家富（右）和休斯顿FASCA舞龙队合影。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>侨教中心主任高家富及ICC执行董事林政宪偕同教职员出席。活动由FASCA休斯顿会长Quenton Lee主持，在老师戴珮青、邱佩冠等带领下，青年志工共同规划各项节目、文化展示及互动游戏。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859384" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859384" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859384-03.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859384" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859384-03-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859384" class="wp-caption-text">学员的舞蹈表演。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>高家富致词表示，中秋节是台湾重要的传统节庆之一，象征家庭团聚。台湾人过中秋节除了分享月饼、赏月，也常与亲友一起烤肉；对身处海外的华人而言，仰望同一轮明月，也象征彼此因共同文化而连结。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859385" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859385" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859385-04.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859385" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859385-04-600x409.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="409" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859385" class="wp-caption-text">现场设置中文书法体验区，供民众挥毫。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>林政宪介绍，ICC长期致力于华语文教育与文化传承，TCML于2026年1月开始授课，希望将华语文及文化学习推广至更广泛的在地社区，不仅服务台湾后裔，也让不同背景的民众有机会认识华语文与台湾文化。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859386" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859386" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859386-05.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859386" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859386-05-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859386" class="wp-caption-text">台式卤味吸引民众购买。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>此次活动以“探索、创造、发现新奇中认识台湾中秋文化”为主轴，设计“文化护照”闯关活动，参加者走访各个站点、完成体验后盖章，从游戏中认识台湾月亮节传统。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859387" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859387" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859387-06.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859387" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859387-06-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859387" class="wp-caption-text">高家富和民众在台下欣赏学员的表演。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>现场设置中文书法、天灯笼制作、（不可食用）月饼工艺、台湾茶文化“台湾好茶趣”、中秋故事、台湾话游戏及机器人体验等摊位；“玩转宝岛”则安排扯铃、踢毽子等传统游戏，让大小朋友在动手做、动手玩的过程中接触台湾文化。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859388" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859388" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859388-07.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859388" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859388-07-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859388" class="wp-caption-text">小朋友展示完成的天灯，开心且小有成就感。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>节目同样精彩，舞龙队率先登场，并有扯铃表演及台湾文化舞蹈（TCDC）呈献的《秋夜之下》等演出，为节庆增添欢乐气氛。现场并有台式卤味、米粉、面、春卷及饮料等小吃，让参与者在品尝台湾味的同时，体验中秋节的节庆氛围。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14859389" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14859389" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859389-08.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14859389" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14859389-08-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14859389" class="wp-caption-text">制作工艺月饼，体验中秋节的节庆氛围。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>主办单位表示，希望通过青年志工与社区教育的力量，让更多族裔家庭从“庆祝、学习、体验”中认识台湾，也让台湾传统文化在休斯顿社区持续交流与传承。</p>
