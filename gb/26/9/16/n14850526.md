<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>桃园中坜交通事故 转弯车应礼让直行车</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14850527-873756-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>桃园市中坜警分局中坜交通中队分队长姚憬恒指出，王男驾车左转时，与对向直行，庄男所驾车辆发生交通事故，致使双方乘客受伤。经测试双方无酒驾情事，详细肇事尚待厘清。（徐乃义／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年09月16日讯】（大纪元记者徐乃义台湾桃园报导）中坜警分局中坜交通中队于2026年9月16日7时许获报，在中运路与后寮路口有交通事故，立即派员到场。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14850530" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14850530" style="width: 599px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14850530-873757_medium.webp"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14850530" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14850530-873757_medium.webp" alt="" width="599" b="449" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14850530" class="wp-caption-text">9月16日7时许获报，在中运路与后寮路口有交通事故，立即派员到场。（桃园市中坜警分局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>经了解，51岁王男驾驶自用小客车沿后寮路行驶左转往中运路时，与对向沿后寮路直行，21岁庄男驾驶之自用小客车发生交通事故，致使王男乘客14岁王女及庄男乘客19岁沈女受有多处擦挫伤送医。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14850531" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14850531" style="width: 601px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14850531-873758_medium.webp"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14850531" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14850531-873758_medium.webp" alt="" width="601" b="451" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14850531" class="wp-caption-text">两部自用小客车发生交通事故，车损不轻。（桃园市中坜警分局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>中坜警分局中坜交通中队经测试双方均无酒精反应，详细肇事尚待厘清。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14850532" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14850532" style="width: 601px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14850532-873759_medium.webp"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14850532" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14850532-873759_medium.webp" alt="" width="601" b="451" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14850532" class="wp-caption-text">两部自用小客车发生交通事故，车损不轻。（桃园市中坜警分局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>中坜警分局长呼吁，驾驶人行经路口应减速慢行，注意号志及周边车流，转弯时确认对向来车动态，转弯车应礼让直行车，保持安全距离，共同维护用路安全。◇</p>
