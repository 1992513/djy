<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>桃园特宠认养9/20登场 鹦鹉、鬃狮蜥、蜜袋鼯找新家</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851772-874176-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>透过认养活动寻找合适家庭，也提醒民众应先了解物种特性及饲养需求，避免冲动饲养。（桃园市政府动保处提供）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年09月18日讯】（大纪元记者徐乃义台湾桃园报导）桃园动保处长王得吉表示，近年民众饲养宠物日益多元，动保处也陆续收容弃养或走失的非犬猫宠物，对于公告后无人领回者，透过认养活动寻找合适家庭，也提醒民众应先了解物种特性及饲养需求，避免冲动饲养。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14851768" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14851768" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851768-874172.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14851768" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851768-874172-450x256.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="256" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14851768" class="wp-caption-text">桃园特宠认养9/20登场。（桃园市政府动保处提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>开放认养动物包括鬃狮蜥、鹌鹑、黄金鼠、蜜袋鼯、小太阳鹦鹉、虎皮鹦鹉、玄凤鹦鹉、吸蜜鹦鹉、红腹侧颈龟、王蛇、球蟒、苏卡达象龟、锦龟及亚马逊鹦鹉等，名单将依动物状况滚动更新，并公告于动保处官网及脸书。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14851769" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14851769" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851769-874173.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14851769" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851769-874173-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14851769" class="wp-caption-text">苏卡达象龟。（桃园市政府动保处提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>其中苏卡达象龟、亚马逊鹦鹉属指定认养物种，须另经书面审核及家访。认养人须年满18岁、设籍桃园市，并携带身份证及适合的动物运输笼。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14851770" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14851770" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851770-874174.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14851770" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851770-874174-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14851770" class="wp-caption-text">爬虫及小型哺乳类动物认养。（桃园市政府动保处提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>桃园动保处提醒，特殊宠物在饮食、环境及照护上各有需求，认养前应审慎评估自身饲养能力，切勿因一时兴起饲养；所有认养人均须现场参加生命教育课程，若有退养纪录，将不得再参与本活动。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14851771" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14851771" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851771-874175.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14851771" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14851771-874175-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14851771" class="wp-caption-text">亚马逊鹦鹉属指定认养物种。（桃园市政府动保处提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>活动地点为桃园市南昌动物保护教育园区（桃园市桃园区向善街120-1号），相关认养规定及最新动物名单可至桃园动保处官方网站(https://animal.tycg.gov.tw/)、动物保护资讯网(https://animal.moa.gov.tw/)或脸书粉丝专页查询。◇</p>
