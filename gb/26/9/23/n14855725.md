<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>中秋感恩 大陆民众向李大师送上节日祝福</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14855849-2026-9-20-260916rktdo_01.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
