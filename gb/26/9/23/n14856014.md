<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>联合国峰会期间 法轮功第六年举办车游</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14856015-1.png" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
