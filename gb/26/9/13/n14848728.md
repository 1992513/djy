<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>“走后门”的英文真的就叫走后门 还有更地道的</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14848860-shutterstock_619461617.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>同样一个职位，别人规规矩矩投简历、排队面试；他呢，托当官的舅舅打了个电话，事就成了，这在中文里叫“走后门”。</p><p>前几次介绍的“辛苦了”“加油”，英文都找不出一句对应的话。这回可不一样，英文那头还真也有一扇“后门”。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>先说那扇“后门”</strong></span></h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>英文里确实有现成的说法：get in through the back door（从后门进去）、a back-door deal（私下交易）。He got the job through the back door.（他这工作是走后门弄来的。）意思、画面都跟中文对得上，老外一听就懂。</p><p>不过真要地道，英文还有几句更传神的。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>pull strings</strong><strong>（幕后拉线的那只手）</strong></span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>最常用、最生动的是pull strings。例：She pulled some strings to get her son into that school.（她走后门把儿子弄进那所学校。）</p><p>这说法源自提线木偶，台上的木偶会动、会演，全靠幕后那人拉动一根根丝线。19世纪英国人把这幅画面搬到了人情世故上，凡是有人在幕后暗中运作、悄悄使劲，就叫pull strings。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>2019 年，美国爆发轰动全球的大学招生贿赂丑闻事件（俗称 Varsity Blues 行动），当时最受注目的一起案件是，有一名中国学生的家庭透过中间人（William “Rick” Singer）向史丹佛大学的帆船队教练行贿高达 650万美元，把原本不符合入学条件的女学生包装成顶尖帆船运动员，保送进史丹佛大学。事发后，这名学生被开除，教练认罪，涉案家长也遭到起诉。</p><p>当然，像这种透过金钱贿赂、伪造履历来操纵入学的名额，已远非普通的pull strings（靠人脉、走后门）范畴，而是非法的行贿与欺诈犯罪（bribery and fraud）。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p>例：She pulled strings illegally by paying massive bribes to secure her child&#x27;s admission.（她透过钜额贿赂非法走后门，以获取孩子入学资格。）</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>know someone</strong><strong>（有关系、</strong><strong>有门路</strong><strong>）</strong></span></h2><p>走后门的底层逻辑，英文一句谚语点得最透：It’s not what you know, it’s who you know.（要紧的不是你会什么，而是你认识谁。）平时想说“有关系、有门路”，就用have connections、know the right people、know someone。例如：He knows someone at city hall.（他在市政府有人。）</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>更露骨的说法：</strong><strong>grease someone</strong><strong>’</strong><strong>s palm</strong><strong>（行贿）</strong></span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>还有个词得跟“走后门”分清楚：grease someone’s palm，字面上是“往人手心抹油”，意指塞钱、行贿，这比走后门更露骨，已经是拿钱开路了。若只是靠人情关系，用pull strings就好。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>避雷小结</strong></span></h2><p>go through the back door老外能懂，但口语里pull strings、have connections更顺、更地道。</p><p>别把“有关系”说成have relationship，那多半会被理解成“有（男女）关系”。要说“有门路”是have connections。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>补充例句</strong></span></h2><p>He didn’t apply like everyone else—his dad pulled some strings.<br/>（他没像大家一样走流程，他爸走了后门。）</p><p>In this town, it’s not what you know, it’s who you know.<br/>（在这地方，靠的不是本事，是关系。）</p><p>She got the internship through connections, not on merit.<br/>（她那实习机会是靠关系弄来的，不是凭真本事。）</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>English Corner</strong></span></h2><p><strong>get in through the back door：</strong>走后门、从后门进去</p><p><strong>pull strings：</strong>动用关系、幕后运作</p><p><strong>have some pull：</strong>有门路、吃得开</p><p><strong>have connections / know the right people：</strong>有关系、有人脉</p><p><strong>It’s not what you know, it’s who you know.：</strong>靠的不是本事，是人脉。</p><p><strong>nepotism：</strong>裙带关系、任人唯亲（源自教宗封赏“侄子”）</p><p><strong>cronyism：</strong>朋党作风、任用亲信</p><p><strong>grease someone’s palm：</strong>塞钱、行贿
