<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>“拍马屁”英文怎么说？有难听的也有文雅的</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14849469-shutterstock_2752567753.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>开会时总有那么一位：老板话还没说完，他先点头如捣蒜；老板讲个冷笑话，他笑得最大声；老板说东，他绝不说西。这一套，中文有个词叫“拍马屁”。</p><p>换成英文该怎么说？今天就为读者备齐一整套，从客气的到难听的，分场合用。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>先说“拍马屁”这三个字的来历，相传它出自北方游牧的旧俗：牵着马相遇，顺手拍拍对方马的屁股，赞一句“好马”，主人听了受用，拍的是马屁股，中的是主人的意。记住这匹马，等一下有个巧合等着你。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>日常口语（轻一点的说法）</strong></span></h2><p><strong>suck up to someone</strong><strong>：</strong>巴结、讨好某人，最常用的一个。例：He’s always sucking up to the boss.（他老是拍老板马屁。）</p><p><strong>butter someone up</strong><strong>：</strong>字面上是“往人身上抹奶油”，把人哄得油光水滑、心花怒放，好开口求事。例：I buttered him up before asking for a day off.（我先把他哄高兴，才开口请假。）</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p><strong>kiss up to someone</strong><strong>：</strong>意思和suck up差不多，美式口语常用。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>难听一点的（带点火气）</strong></span></h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p><strong>brown-nose</strong><strong>：</strong>这个词画面不太雅（你自己琢磨琢磨：鼻子怎会变棕的？），可美国人用得极多，指极尽谄媚之能事。名词brown-noser就是“马屁精”。语气不客气，熟人之间打趣可以，正式场合少用。</p><p>还有更粗的kiss-ass，字面不必翻了，属脏话一路，认得就好，自己别轻易出口。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p>除了brown-noser，英文形容这种人还有一长串：</p><p><strong>a yes-man</strong><strong>：</strong>应声虫，老板说啥都说yes的那种。</p><p><strong>a bootlicker</strong><strong>：</strong>“舔靴子的人”，卑躬屈膝地谄媚。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p><strong>an apple-polisher</strong><strong>：</strong>“擦苹果的人”，早年美国学生爱给老师送个擦得锃亮的苹果讨好，这词就这么来的。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p><strong>a toady</strong><strong>：</strong>最有画面的一个，早年江湖郎中身边有个托儿，当众吞下据说有毒的蟾蜍（toad），再由郎中“妙手回春”把他救活，好兜售药品。这个吃蟾蜍的跟班叫toad-eater，缩成toady，后来专指低三下四的马屁精。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>文雅书面一点的</strong></span></h2><p>要说最文雅的一个，是curry favor（讨好、逢迎）。例：He joined the club just to curry favor with the manager.（他入会纯粹是为了讨好经理。）</p><p>妙就妙在它的来历，还记得开头那匹马吗？这里的curry不是咖喱，而是“给马梳毛、刷洗”。它出自14世纪初的法国讽刺长诗《福维尔传奇》（Roman de Fauvel）：一匹叫福维尔（Fauvel）的栗色马，阴险狡诈，却爬到了主人头上，教会与朝廷的权贵都争着巴结它。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><p>“给福维尔刷毛”（curry Fauvel）于是成了“奉承小人、以谋私利”的代名词。后来福维尔这名字被人淡忘，读音一讹，curry Fauvel就成了今天的curry favor。</p><p>你看，中文拍的是马的屁股，英文刷的是马的鬃毛。东方西方骂起马屁精，竟不约而同都牵出了一匹马。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>避雷小结</strong></span></h2><p>别把“拍马屁”直译成pat the horse’s bottom，老外只会一头雾水。</p><p>flatter（奉承）这个词是有的，但偏中性、偏书面；想说得地道、带点揶揄，还得用suck up、butter up、brown-nose这些。</p><p>后面教的那一串词全是贬义，是背后吐槽用的。当面千万别对人说You’re such a brown-noser，那是要撕破脸的。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>补充例句</strong></span></h2><p>Stop sucking up to the teacher—it won&#x27;t change your grade.<br/>（别再拍老师马屁了，成绩不会因此改变。）</p><p>He buttered up his wife before mentioning the fishing trip.<br/>（他先把老婆哄高兴，才提钓鱼的事。）</p><p>Everyone in that office is a yes-man; nobody dares to disagree.<br/>（那办公室全是应声虫，没人敢唱反调。）</p><h2><strong><span style="color:#008000">English Corner</span> 谄媚</p><p><strong>brown-noser</strong><strong>：</strong>[名] 马屁精</p><p><strong>bootlicker</strong><strong>：</strong>谄媚拍马之人</p><p><strong>yes-man</strong><strong>：</strong>应声虫</p><p><strong>apple-polisher</strong><strong>：</strong>拍马屁的人</p><p><strong>toady</strong><strong>：</strong>低三下四的马屁精</p><p><strong>curry
