<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>61届广播金钟奖入围名单 教育广播电台16项领跑</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845778-260909064339100613.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
