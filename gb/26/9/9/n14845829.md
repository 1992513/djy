<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>北美华人排球邀请赛在蒙特利尔举行 中华会馆：致敬先辈</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845839-20260905_Montreal_volleyball7-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>第81届北美华人邀请赛（NACIVT）开幕式现场。（满地可中华会馆提供）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年09月08日讯】来自美加两国的数千名排球运动员、家人和朋友，于劳工节周末齐聚蒙特利尔，参加第81届北美华人排球邀请赛（NACIVT）。9月5日周六下午，在蒙特利尔会展中心（Palais des congrès de Montréal）举行了隆重而热闹的开幕式。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14845845" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14845845" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845845-20260905_Montreal_volleyball3.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14845845" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845845-20260905_Montreal_volleyball3.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14845845" class="wp-caption-text">从右至左）本届赛事承办方满地可中华会馆主席黄蔚岳，驻蒙特娄台北经济文化办事处主任陈淑容，开幕式上分别演唱美国和加拿大国歌的Alicia Reid和Louese Hu，赛事承办方CRARR执行总监Fo Niemi，蒙特利尔SPVM警官Abdullah Emran在开幕式上。（满地可中华会馆提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14845841" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14845841" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845841-20260905_Montreal_volleyball2.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14845841" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845841-20260905_Montreal_volleyball2.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14845841" class="wp-caption-text">第81届北美华人邀请赛（NACIVT）开幕式现场。（满地可中华会馆提供）</figcaption></figure>

<figure id="attachment_14845847" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14845847" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845847-20260905_Montreal_volleyball6.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14845847" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845847-20260905_Montreal_volleyball6.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14845847" class="wp-caption-text">满地可中华会馆副主席张广存（左二）与谭超鸿（右一）获得NACIVT颁发的感谢状。（满地可中华会馆提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>开幕式上，加美两国国歌演唱和大型舞狮表演拉开了本届赛事帷幕。主办方之一、满地可中华会馆副主席张广存（Bryant Chang）在致辞中欢迎来自加美两国从东岸到西岸的运动员，并向当年在波士顿唐人街发起这项年度赛事的台山籍先辈致敬。他表示，这项年度排球赛事是华社继承先辈遗志、铭记先辈功绩的见证。</p>
<p>“无论我们的先辈原本是移民到加拿大或美国，我们都拥有共同的历史。那些主要来自广东省各地村庄的勇敢年轻人，为了建造这两个国家的铁路而远赴北美。他们怀抱着对‘金山’的憧憬，只为给后人创造更美好、更富裕的生活。我们正是先辈们数十年间所承受的辛劳、磨难与牺牲的结晶。”张广存说。</p>
<p>蒙特利尔市议员、警界人士、以及外交人员出席了开幕式并致词。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14845848" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14845848" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845848-20260905_Montreal_volleyball4.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14845848" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845848-20260905_Montreal_volleyball4.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="401" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14845848" class="wp-caption-text">北美华人邀请赛采用男子9人制和女子6人制。图为比赛现场。（满地可中华会馆提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14845850" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14845850" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845850-20260905_Montreal_volleyball5.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14845850" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14845850-20260905_Montreal_volleyball5.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14845850" class="wp-caption-text">北美华人邀请赛采用男子9人制和女子6人制。图为比赛现场。（满地可中华会馆提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>北美华人排球邀请赛在北美各大城市轮流举行。今年蒙特利尔的赛事由满地可中华会馆、CRARR等组织共同举办。本届赛事规模为历届最大，包括纽约、多伦多、旧金山等地队伍在内的来自美国和加拿大200多支球队参赛，共有4,000名注册球员（包括2,400名美国参赛者）以及1,000名亲朋好友共襄盛举。</p>
