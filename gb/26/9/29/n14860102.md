<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>休士顿荣光会完成交接 禹道庆接任新会长</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860112-05-600x400.jpeg" />
<h6>休士顿荣光会新旧会长交接大会合影。（陆希／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年09月30日讯】（大纪元记者陆希休斯顿报导）休士顿荣光会新旧会长交接大会于9月27日中午在侨教中心展览室举行，侨教中心主任高家富应邀出席观礼。大会依传统仪程进行，全体肃立唱国歌，向国旗及国父遗像行三鞠躬礼，并由卸任会长左大为恭读国父遗嘱，展现荣光会对历史传承与服务精神的重视。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14860109" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14860109" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860109-02.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14860109" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860109-02-600x422.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="422" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14860109" class="wp-caption-text">大会依传统仪程进行，展现荣光会对历史传承与服务精神的重视。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>左大为在会务报告中回顾四年任期工作，感谢理监事及会员的支持，并透过照片回顾各项活动，包括元旦及双十国庆升旗护旗、参与美国退伍军人大会、参观德州水产养殖场、举办防诈骗讲座及元宵节活动等。他表示，荣光会每年参与元旦及双十升旗护旗任务，会员均引以为荣。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14860110" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14860110" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860110-03.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14860110" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860110-03-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14860110" class="wp-caption-text">左大为赠送礼物感谢张连祐（右）的无私贡献。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>在抗战老兵纪念方面，荣光会除协助申请及颁发抗战老兵纪念章，也持续探望老兵；禹道庆提及老兵过世后参与告别式，以敬礼、覆盖国旗及扶棺等回忆，一度哽咽，并感慨持续关怀是唯一能表达敬意的方式。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14860115" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14860115" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860115-08.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14860115" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860115-08-600x450.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14860115" class="wp-caption-text">左大为回顾四年来的精彩活动。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>左大为特别回顾2024年在德州埃尔帕索军人公墓举行的致敬活动，纪念52位中华民国空军烈士。当时各地荣光会代表逾百人参与，美国海军航空队康大为逐一向52座墓碑敬礼，令人动容。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14860108" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14860108" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860108-01.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14860108" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860108-01-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14860108" class="wp-caption-text">在主任高家富（左）监交下，禹道庆（右）在左大为手中接下象征传承军刀完成新旧会长交接。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>会中亦提到荣光会面临会员年龄老化及年轻会员不足等挑战，期盼未来吸引曾服役的年轻世代及眷属加入，为组织注入新活力。同时，荣光会持续推动国际亲善大使计划，协助青年参与国际交流。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14860111" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14860111" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860111-04.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14860111" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860111-04-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14860111" class="wp-caption-text">新任会长禹道庆致词。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>大会通过新任理事名单后，新任理事召开会议推举干部，禹道庆当选会长，李明勋任副会长，丁方印任监事长，董元庆、左大为任监事。随后在高家富主任监交下完成新旧会长交接。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14860113" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14860113" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860113-06.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14860113" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860113-06-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14860113" class="wp-caption-text">荣光会新任理监事团队和当月生日寿星合影。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>禹道庆致词时感谢左大为及历任干部奠定的基础，并表示自己自2015年经前会长高华鹏引荐加入理事会，未来将持续与会员交流、服务会务。他也关注泰北孤军后代在身份及赴台求学方面所面临的问题，期盼相关单位持续关注协助。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14860114" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14860114" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860114-07.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14860114" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860114-07-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14860114" class="wp-caption-text">贵宾康大为和孙铁汉（右一、二）出席并分享空军军旅经历。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>活动最后在慷慨激昂高唱陆军、海军及空军军歌声中圆满结束。</p>
