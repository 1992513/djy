<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>德甲第4轮：多特开局四连胜 力压拜仁领跑</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14852859-GettyImages-2295488039.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
