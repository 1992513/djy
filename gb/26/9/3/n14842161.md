<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>总理承诺西澳GST分成将保持不变</h2>
<h6></h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年09月03日讯】<span lang="zh-TW">（大纪元记者</span><span lang="zh-TW">杨达延</span><span lang="zh-TW">澳洲珀斯编译报导）</span><span lang="en-AU">8</span><span lang="zh-TW">月</span><span lang="en-AU">24</span><span lang="zh-TW">日（</span><span lang="zh-TW">周一</span><span lang="zh-TW">）</span><span lang="zh-TW">，澳洲总理阿尔巴尼斯在珀斯南部</span><span lang="en-AU">Henderson</span><span lang="zh-TW">区举行的记者会上承诺，在他担任总理期间，</span><span lang="en-AU">2018</span><span lang="zh-TW">年达成的</span><span lang="en-AU">GST</span><span lang="zh-TW">协议中有关西澳</span><span lang="en-AU">GST</span><span lang="zh-TW">分成的安排将保持不变。</span></p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">据《今日珀斯》报</span><span lang="zh-TW">导</span><span lang="zh-TW">，</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">8</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">月</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">14</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">日，生产力委员会（</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">The</span> <span lang="en-AU">Productivity</span> <span lang="en-AU">Commission</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">）发布了关于</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2018</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">年</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">GST</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">分配改革审查的中期报告。该委员会认为，</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2018</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">年莫里森政府与西澳达成的</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">GST</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">协议是一项代价高昂的政策失误，该协议使税收体制的公平性下降、复杂度增加，且几乎只让西澳受益。</span></p>
<p>根据该协议，从<span lang="en-AU">2022</span>至<span lang="en-AU">2023</span>财年开始，联邦政府在西澳收取的每一澳元商品及服务税（<span lang="en-AU">GST</span>）中，将返还西澳政府<span lang="en-AU">70</span>澳分，然后在<span lang="en-AU">2024</span>至<span lang="en-AU">2025</span>财年，分成方案为每一澳元的<span lang="en-AU">GST</span>返还西澳<span lang="en-AU">75</span>澳分。这使西澳成为最大受益者，所得远超其它州和领地，西澳每年能多拿<span lang="en-AU">60</span>亿澳元。</p>
<p>该协议还包括“不受损”（<span lang="en-AU">no-worse-off</span>）补助，以确保其它州和领地不会因这些调整而蒙受收入损失。</p>
<p>工党政府还在<span lang="en-AU">2024</span>年延长了莫里森时期的“不受损”补助，将其原定的到期时间从<span lang="en-AU">2026</span>至<span lang="en-AU">2027</span>财年延长至<span lang="en-AU">2029</span>至<span lang="en-AU">2030</span>财年。</p>
<p>然而，生产力委员会表示，这些补助目前每年给纳税人造成约<span lang="en-AU">60</span>亿澳元的负担。仅在<span lang="en-AU">2024</span>至<span lang="en-AU">2025</span>财年支付的<span lang="en-AU">64</span>亿澳元，本可以为每位纳税人提供超过<span lang="en-AU">450</span>澳元的减税。委员会还警告称，如果铁矿石价格或产量上升，这一成本每年可能飙升至<span lang="en-AU">120</span>亿澳元。</p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">生产力委员会须在</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2026</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">年</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">12</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">月</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">31</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">日前向联邦政府提交最终报告，目前正在征求更多的意见书。</span></p>
<p>自从中期报告公布后，西澳工党政府、行业团体、企业界以及西澳社区一直施压，要求总理阿尔巴尼斯明确表态支持现行安排。此前他公开表态时仅承诺西澳将获得其“公平份额”。</p>
<p><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">8</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">月</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">24</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">日（</span><span lang="zh-TW">周一</span><span lang="zh-TW">），</span><span lang="zh-TW">他告诉记者：“我的立场非常明确：在我担任总理期间，西澳的</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">GST</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">安排不会有任何改变。这就是我的观点，也是我一贯的观点。”</span></p>
<p>责任编辑：杨新云</p>
