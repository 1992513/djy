<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>【车评】蛮力提升 声浪更震撼 2026 Dodge Charger R/T Plus</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843215-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_01-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>2026 Dodge Charger R/T Plus。（李奥／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年09月05日讯】Dodge品牌车都是以动力取胜，尤其是轿车型的Charger，更是受人喜爱，而且也获得不少地方警察局的青睐，购入作为警车追逐贼人，成为高性能轿车的代表作。2026年款Dodge Charger是车系的第八代产品，外形更有动感。</p>
<p><a loading="lazy" title="YouTube video player" data2-src=""></a><a href="https://d21y52w6f35pla.cloudfront.net/U2kSVfBviItr"><img width="600" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/1992513/djy/master/gb/300/djtsp.jpg" title="进入原文观看"  alt="进入原文观看"></a><h3 align=center>(视频.国内建议下载<a href="https://github.com/1992513/www/blob/master/README.md#8">翻墙软件</a>进入原文观看)</h3><a src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/Q_27EfreROI?si=6F2iNIcQNMceGDVT" width="560" b="315" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></a></p>
<p>进入第八代车型，Charge的外形设计可谓回归原点，宽阔而长的车身，再加上扁平的一线车灯，如向第一代Charge致敬，当然，车灯使用了既省电又光亮的LED灯，车前及车尾中间都挂上了三角箭形徽章，称为“Fratzog”，同时也在四个车轮中心可看到，成为了新一代Charger的特点。微胀的引擎盖使Charge看起来更粗壮，而引擎盖中间有黑色饰板，有 “SIXPACK”字体，正好代表了车内的SIXPACK引擎。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14843180" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14843180" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843180-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_07.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14843180" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843180-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_07-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14843180" class="wp-caption-text">2026 Dodge Charger R/T Plus。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14843182" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14843182" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843182-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_11.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14843182" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843182-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_11-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14843182" class="wp-caption-text">光亮的LED灯。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>最为特别的是，新Charger不是四门轿车，而是一辆五门揭背车，Dodge称之为“隐藏揭背（hidden-hatch）”，在外观上不太看得出来，既可令Charger更为流线，又可大大方便了装载东西，当后座放平后的容量达到37.4立方英尺。另外，新Charger分为三门版及五门版两种。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14843184" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14843184" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843184-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_21.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14843184" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843184-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_21-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14843184" class="wp-caption-text">新Charger是五门揭背车。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>第八代Charger的动力分为SIXPACK引擎版及全电力版两种，而SIXPACK引擎又细分为SO及HO版。测试的R/T型号配备了Standard Output（S.O.）的3升直六双涡轮增压引擎，输出420匹马力及468磅英尺扭力，比旧有5.7升HEMI引擎多出来50匹马力及73磅英尺扭力，0-60英里/时加速为4.6秒。而Scat Pack版配备的High Output（H.O.）引擎，输出更强劲，高达550匹马力。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14843187" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14843187" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843187-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_22.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14843187" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843187-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_22-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14843187" class="wp-caption-text">3升直六双涡轮增压引擎。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>那么，SIXPACK引擎有何特别呢？它使用了低惯性涡轮增压器，除了可更快增压外，又有近乎零迟滞的推进力；反向旋转的涡轮组件则有助于减少涡轮迟滞并平衡气流。高达22psi的压力，在2,500转时即可全数释放扭力，让驾驶者只要轻踩加速踏板，就能获得毫不费力的低转速冲劲。最高规格的Daytona Scat Pack版，则是全电力动力系统，配备输出670匹的电动马达，令Charger在3.3秒就可亮丽完成0-60英里/时的加速，可谓十分惊人！</p>
<figure id="attachment_14843189" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14843189" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843189-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_13.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14843189" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843189-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_13-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14843189" class="wp-caption-text">引擎盖上的SIXPACK。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>车内是以黑色为主，再配上各式红色线作点缀，增加跑味。异形方向盘的顶部及底部，都改为扁平，使用也更方便，最好是Dodge将Drive Mode按钮放在方向盘下方位置，动一动手指就可改变行车模式。10.25英寸的数位仪表与12.3英寸中控萤幕，将Charger带入了新世纪，大部分的设置也可在两个萤幕内更改，内置了Uconnect 5系统，反应很快。环绕式风格的座舱则拥有可切换64种颜色的气氛灯，由前台伸延到车门，再延续到后门，相当震撼！变速杆的形状独特，但仍算方便，毕竟只需开车或倒车时才用到。然而，引擎启动键设在变速杆后方，也是合理的设计，但有别于其他汽车藏在方向盘后方，所以有时也有错乱找不到启动键的时候。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14843191" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14843191" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843191-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_39.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14843191" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843191-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_39-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14843191" class="wp-caption-text">车内采用黑红双色。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>虽然Charger的内部宽阔，无论乘坐或脚部空间也很大，然而由于车身矮，所以头部容易碰到车顶，尤其后座更甚，出入要份外小心！同样由于Charger车体宽大，所以在驾驶时，尤其在转弯时更容易碰到行车线边。Charge的加速很快，最喜欢它加速时的沉厚声音，再加上源源不绝的动力，虽然声音不及以往V8时的雄厚，但也是非常悦耳！</p>
<figure id="attachment_14843194" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14843194" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843194-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_24.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14843194" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843194-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_24-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14843194" class="wp-caption-text">10.25英寸的数位仪表。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14843196" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14843196" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843196-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_25.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14843196" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843196-2026_Dodge_Charger_RT_25-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14843196" class="wp-caption-text">12.3英寸中控萤幕。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>测试的Charger只是入门版R/T Plus，实力也不是盖的，相信Scat Pack版更有趣。至于Daytona Scat Pack纯电版，马力与加速虽然更强，但缺乏了迷人的引擎声，百货应百客，也会有消费者喜欢的！Charger R/T的起跳价为$49,995，5万元以下的价格就可购入美式肌肉车，也是值得的！◇</p>
