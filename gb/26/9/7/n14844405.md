<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>中秋烤肉抗烤焦、解油腻 营养师传授对策</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/02/id14444326-6_shutterstock_418451995-e1740370406247.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>亲友团聚欢庆中秋节，免不了会举办烤肉大餐。万一肉烤焦了，该如何处理？你是否担心摄取太多烧烤食品对健康不利？营养师提供对策让你开心享受又安心。</p><p>烤肉一不小心就烤焦，你是不是默默安慰自己：没事啦！把烧焦的部分剥掉就可以吃。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>但……真的没事吗？烤焦的肉，到底能不能食用？对健康有威胁吗？</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>其实高温碳烤含油脂或淀粉类食材时，很容易产生PAH（多环芳香族碳氢化合物）的物质，<ahref="https://www.fda.gov.tw/tc/siteContent.aspx?sid=13714" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="health-article-hover-class">其中以苯(a)骈芘(benzo(a)pyrene, 简称BaP)的致癌性最强，国际癌症研究中心（International Agency for Research on Cancer, IARC）将其列为一级致癌物。</a></p><p>台湾针对部分食品中的（PAHs）设有含量标准，例如烧烤肉及其制品中品苯骈[a]芘（BaP）的上限为5 ppb。若是长期摄取超标的烧烤肉品，恐提升罹患部分癌症的风险。</p><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14844406-1d146eb9656ec25dd3f7482612116ba9.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14844406-1d146eb9656ec25dd3f7482612116ba9.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14844406-1d146eb9656ec25dd3f7482612116ba9.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14844406-1d146eb9656ec25dd3f7482612116ba9.jpg" alt="（高敏敏营养师提供）" width="300" b="373"/></picture></a><figcaption>（高敏敏营养师提供）</figcaption></figure></div><h2><span style="color:#3e7100">肉烤焦了，该怎么办？</span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p><strong>情况1.小部分微微焦黑</strong></p><p>对策：把焦黑的部分剥掉，一般健康人食用是没问题的。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p><strong>情况2.整片烤得一片焦黑</strong></p><p>根据<ahref="https://www.fda.gov.tw/tc/newsContent.aspx?cid=4&amp;id=t622147" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="health-article-hover-class">台湾食品药物管理署</a>，研究结果显示，相较于正常烧烤，食材烤焦后，多环芳香族碳氢化合物的含量会显著增加。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p>对策：如果你身体健康、没有特殊病史，剥除焦黑处后即可食用（注意：只是偶尔吃可以）；若是你的肠胃敏感、免疫力较差、或家族有癌症病史，直接丢掉比较安全。</p><p>人体本身具有清除部分异常细胞的能力，但如果长期暴露于致癌物，仍可能提高罹癌风险。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000">运用两招避开食物烤焦陷阱</span></h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>怎么做才能将食物烤得香又不至于焦黑？</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p><strong>1.食材太厚</strong></p><p>对策：烤肉前，先将食材切成薄片或小块状。</p><p><strong>2.难熟透的食材</strong></p><p>对策：先汆烫、蒸熟或微波预热，这样做可缩短直火炭烤时间，比较不会因烤焦而产生致癌物。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#3e7100">烤肉搭配五种蔬果 解油腻、助代谢</span></h2><p>烤肉时，别只顾着享用肉品，搭配这五种蔬果一起食用，不只能补充营养，还能帮助身体代谢、解油腻。</p><p><strong>1.洋葱</strong></p><div><figure style="max-width:408px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14843771-onion1_1084823900.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source alt="洋葱。(Shutterstock)" width="408" b="272"/></picture></a><figcaption>洋葱。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><p>享用烤肉时多夹一点洋葱，不只香气加分，还可提升保护力。洋葱含有硫化物，是身体天然的解毒小帮手，有助于分解体内的有害物质，像是烤肉过程产生的毒素（如PAHs）。</p><p><strong>2.芭乐</strong></p><div><figure b="324"/></picture></a><figcaption>莴苣。（图：shutterstock）</figcaption></figure></div><p>用莴苣包肉吃，让美味加分，增加补充膳食纤维。莴苣富含水分和膳食纤维，可促进肠胃蠕动，不仅能帮身体顺利排出多余废物，又能舒缓大啖烤肉后的肠胃负担。</p><p><strong>4.奇异果</strong></p><div><figure
