<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>【视频】台湾万鹭朝凤奇景 中秋迎最佳赏鸟期</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14844788-2609080204532378.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
