<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>没能到热带岛屿度假？跟我一起做打发柠檬水</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14844381-entree-lemonade-20260817-vs-1080x720-1.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>【大纪元2026年09月08日讯】（TheKitchn.com网站Patty Catalano撰文／大纪元记者徐海韵编译）我多么渴望能徜徉在热带岛屿的沙滩，双脚踩着细沙，聆听阵阵海浪拍岸声，享受着凉爽的海风。遗憾的是，今年夏天我没能去度假，这就是为什么最近频繁制作这款新式打发柠檬水（whipped lemonade）的原因。加入菠萝（凤梨）和椰子不仅赋予它热带风情，还让口感更加美味清爽。这款夏日冰饮既像柠檬水又像奶昔，风味绝佳，我相信全家人都会喜欢。</p><h3>为什么你会爱上它</h3><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>只需三种食材，耗时不到五分钟。用搅拌机将新鲜柠檬汁、椰浆（cream of coconut）和冷冻菠萝混合搅打，即可制成这款清凉又充满热带风情的冰饮。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>它是消暑佳品。炎炎夏日，无论大人还是孩子，都能在享用这款冰沙饮品时感受到清凉舒爽。</p><h3>主要食材</h3><p><strong>柠檬</strong>：新鲜柠檬最好！需要1/4杯柠檬汁，约取自两个多汁的柠檬。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p><strong>冷冻菠萝块</strong>：使用冷冻水果能赋予饮品甜美的热带风味，同时减少了为获得冰凉口感所需的冰块用量。</p><p><strong>椰浆</strong>（Cream of Coconut）：注意不要将其与椰子奶油（coconut cream）或椰奶（coconut milk）混淆！这种质地浓郁、口感细腻的配料有助于平衡新鲜柠檬汁的酸味。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><h2>热带风打发柠檬水（Tropical Whipped Lemonade）</h2><p>总时间：4分钟<br/>操作时间：3分钟<br/>搅打时间：1分钟</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p>1人份</p><h3>【材料】</h3><p>半杯椰浆<br/>1/4杯鲜榨柠檬汁（约取自2个中号柠檬）<br/>2杯冷冻菠萝块（约9盎司）<br/>半杯冰块（约2.5盎司）<br/>打发奶油（whipped cream），用于顶部装饰（可省略）<br/>柠檬片，用于装饰（可省略）</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><h3>【作法】</h3><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>1. 将椰浆、柠檬汁、冷冻菠萝块和冰块放入搅拌机中（最好是高速搅拌机）。如有需要，可使用搅拌棒辅助，搅打至质地顺滑，约需1分钟。</p><p>2. 倒入杯中，可根据喜好饰以打发奶油和柠檬片。</p><p><strong>食谱说明</strong></p><p>可以用打发的1/3杯无糖椰浆加1/4杯甜炼乳来代替椰浆。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div
