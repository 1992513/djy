<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>2026多伦多秋季单车特卖会仅一天 先到先得！</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/09/id14860194-BICYCLE-FALL-BLOWOUT-SALE-2026-2-600x400.jpg" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年09月23日讯】安省规模最大的单日单车清仓特卖会将于2026年秋季回归！</p>
<div class="post-entry blockquote-style-1">
<div id="penci-post-entry-inner" class="inner-post-entry entry-content">
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-374110" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/111-819x1024.jpg" alt="" width="819" b="1024" /></p>
<p>活动汇聚顶尖单车零售商、制造商与分销商，针对知名品牌单车、电动单车、骑行服饰、零组件及配件提供季度末超值折扣。</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-374109" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/112-1024x768.jpg" alt="" width="1024" b="768" /></p>
<h2>2026亮点特色与活动区域</h2>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-374112" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/截图-2026-09-25-上午7.52.27-1024x621.png" alt="" width="1024" b="621" /></p>
<p><strong>电动单车与微型出行扩展专区（Expanded E-Bike &amp; Micro-Mobility Zone）：</strong></p>
<p>专为电动公路车、越野车及通勤车打造的扩建专区，并邀请电池技术专家现场提供咨询与服务。</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-374111" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/截图-2026-09-25-上午7.53.21-1024x665.png" alt="" width="1024" b="665" /><strong>室内试骑轨道扩建（Expanded Test Ride Track）：</strong></p>
<p>规模更大的室内试骑场地，让买家在购买前可于现场亲自试驾公路车、越野车及电动单车。</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-374113" src="https://ntdca.com/cn/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/截图-2026-09-25-上午7.51.47-1024x594.png" alt="" width="1024" b="594" /><strong>季度末库存大清仓（End-of-Season Inventory Clearouts）：</strong></p>
<p>大多伦多地区各大单车店直营清仓，展示车、库存设备及专业骑行服饰皆享惊爆优惠价。</p>

<p><strong>技术与维修中心（Tech &amp; Maintenance Hub）：</strong></p>
