<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>白梅低语的地方 摄影家在老聚落停了下来</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683758-826161-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>枝头花开，日常如常。（林桔春提供）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年01月25日讯】（大纪元记者廖俪芬台湾新竹报导）走进这个地方，位于新竹北埔老街旁国恩家庆天水堂，及金广福国定古迹百年古厝的百年老梅树，梅花其实没有特别的目的。不是为了追花期，也不是为了打卡，只是循着熟悉的小路，一步步走进老聚落的边缘。围墙不高，老屋不新，远远就看见几株梅树低低地展开枝枒，像是早就知道会有人来，却又不急着迎接。林桔春摄影家的镜头，就是在这样的状态下被举起来的。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14683749" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14683749" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683749-826152.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14683749" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683749-826152-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14683749" class="wp-caption-text">百年老梅枝桠横展，白梅点点盛开，吸引赏花与摄影的民众在树下缓步停留，古厝成了最温柔的背景。（林桔春提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14683748" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14683748" style="width: 451px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683748-826151.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14683748" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683748-826151-600x900.jpg" alt="" width="451" b="677" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14683748" class="wp-caption-text">从侧面望去，天水堂的屋脊线条层层展开，红砖墙与灰石基座相映，展现百年聚落的稳定与从容。（林桔春提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14683747" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14683747" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683747-826150.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14683747" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683747-826150-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14683747" class="wp-caption-text">百年门楼静立岁月中。（林桔春提供）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>?老屋、围墙与刚刚好的梅花</h2>
<p>这里的梅花不算盛开，白色的花朵零零散散地分布在枝头，枝干弯曲、粗糙，带着明显的岁月痕迹。花没有压过背景，反而让红砖老屋、石墙与围篱都自然地进入画面里。</p>
<p>镜头里的梅花，并不是主角，而是陪着老建筑一起存在的角色。有些画面，梅枝越过围墙，刚好遮住屋瓦的一角；有些角度，庙宇屋脊藏在花影后方，只露出熟悉的线条。这些构图，都不是刻意安排，而是站定之后，慢慢等画面自己成形。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14683750" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14683750" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683750-826153.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14683750" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683750-826153-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14683750" class="wp-caption-text">老梅树前方，金广福公馆屋脊若隐若现，梅花与古迹相互衬托，构成北埔冬日限定的风景。（林桔春提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14683751" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14683751" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683751-826154.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14683751" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683751-826154-600x900.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="900" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14683751" class="wp-caption-text">老梅枝干盘曲、花朵疏落，背景是天水堂与金广福公馆静静伫立，北埔老街在冬日清晨，展现最温柔的历史风景。（林桔春提供）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>在枝枒之间? 看见时间</h2>
<p>拍摄时，最花时间的不是按快门，而是选择站在哪里。稍微往左，枝条太密；往右一步，又会遮住后方的老屋。林桔春摄影家常常停下来，看很久，才移动半步。这样的拍摄方式，让画面里多了一种“不急着完成”的感觉。</p>
<p>梅树的枝干向外延展，弯曲却稳定，像是长年在这里承受风雨的结果。花开得不多，却刚好，让人注意到树本身的姿态。镜头没有拉近特写，而是保留空间，让枝枒、天空与后方的聚落一起呼吸。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14683752" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14683752" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683752-826155.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14683752" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683752-826155-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14683752" class="wp-caption-text">红砖老屋静静伫立，白梅在枝头开花，冬末的气息在墙与树之间慢慢流动。 （林桔春提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14683753" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14683753" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683753-826156.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14683753" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683753-826156-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14683753" class="wp-caption-text">枝桠低垂，白梅穿过视线，后方庙宇屋顶若隐若现，岁月与季节在此交会。 （林桔春提供）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>池水，是另一个画面</h2>
<p>聚落一侧的小池，成了拍摄后段的重心。池水不深，水面微微混浊，却因为风小，映照得出梅树完整的倒影。石头围出的池岸线条自然，没有刻意修饰，反而让倒影显得安稳。</p>
<p>有几个瞬间，站在池边看着水面，很难分清哪一边才是真正的树。枝枒在水里延伸，云影慢慢移动，偶尔一片落花掉进水面，画面就轻轻变了。这样的景，不需要快门连拍，只要等。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14683755" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14683755" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683755-826158.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14683755" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683755-826158-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14683755" class="wp-caption-text">两株梅树相对而立，水面映出倒影，构成一幅属于冬末的平衡画面。（林桔春提供）</figcaption></figure>
<h2>不是景点? 是一段被留下的日常</h2>
<p>如果要说这里是一个“景点”，其实并不准确。它没有醒目的入口，也没有介绍牌，只是老社区里，一块仍然保留着自然与时间痕迹的角落。梅花每年都会开，池水每天都在，但不是每一次都会被看见。</p>
<p>林桔春摄影家的照片，留下的不是花开最盛的瞬间，而是一种刚刚好的状态，花未满、叶未生、人不多，时间慢慢走。离开时，回头再看一眼，那些梅树依然低低地站着。不说话，也不挽留。只是静静地，等下一次有人愿意停下来。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14683754" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14683754" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683754-826157.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14683754" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14683754-826157-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14683754" class="wp-caption-text">池水如镜，倒映树影与天空，花不在水中，却留在心里。（林桔春提供）</figcaption></figure>
