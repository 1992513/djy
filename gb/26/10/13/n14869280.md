<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>休斯顿双十茶会 逾450人同欢展现侨界团结活力</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869294-02-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>主任高家富、处长萧伊芳与主委洪良冰、副主委林焌轩（前排左4至左7）及与会来宾共同切蛋糕，祝贺中华民国生日快乐。（陆希／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年10月14日讯】（大纪元记者陆希休斯顿报导）“中华民国115年双十国庆茶会”10月10日上午11时于休士顿侨教中心大礼堂举行，由休士顿双十国庆筹备会主办。驻休经文处处长萧伊芳、侨教中心主任高家富、国策顾问吴光宜，以及多位侨务荣誉职人员、社团领袖与大休斯顿地区逾450位侨胞热情参与，现场座无虚席，气氛热烈。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14869295" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14869295" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869295-03.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14869295" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869295-03-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14869295" class="wp-caption-text">北一女仪队整齐俐落的演出。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>活动由陈莹、张雪宁联袂主持，以去年“期待今年再见”的结语开场，感谢大家如期相聚，为茶会揭开序幕。庆筹会主委洪良冰致词感谢表演者、总监何真、主持人及工作团队的付出，安排当天丰富精彩节目，并邀请大家欣赏演出。</p>
<p>萧伊芳致词回顾自9月中旬启动的一系列国庆活动，表示升旗典礼与茶会是系列庆祝活动的重要环节，并预告隔日晚间将举行国庆晚会。她特别感谢洪良冰及筹备团队的辛劳，祝福中华民国国运昌隆，也祝侨胞生意兴隆、身体健康、家庭美满、学业进步。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14869296" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14869296" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869296-04.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14869296" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869296-04-600x450.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14869296" class="wp-caption-text">侨教中心主任高家富台上致词。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>高家富表示，派驻休斯顿三个月来，深感当地侨界凝聚力强，国庆活动在洪良冰带领下有声有色。他指出，侨教中心将于年底展开整体修缮工程，以提升服务品质，期盼侨胞持续支持侨务工作。</p>
<p>随后，处长萧伊芳、主任高家富与主委洪良冰、副主委林焌轩及与会来宾共同切蛋糕、齐唱生日歌，高呼“中华民国生日快乐！”FASCA志工也将蛋糕分送至观众席，让大家一边欣赏节目、一边分享国庆喜悦。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14869297" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14869297" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869297-05.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14869297" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869297-05-600x430.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="430" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14869297" class="wp-caption-text">林天娜与章翠英歌唱表演。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14869298" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14869298" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869298-06.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14869298" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869298-06-600x433.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="433" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14869298" class="wp-caption-text">“天下第一班”融合民族舞蹈及健身操的表演。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>当天节目多元，包括“北一女仪队”整齐俐落的演出，展现多年来持续参与双十及元旦升旗活动的爱国热忱。部分团员即使已是祖母年纪，仍不畏日晒、挥汗练习，以行动传承对台湾的情感，赢得全场掌声与赞赏。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14869299" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14869299" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869299-07.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14869299" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869299-07-600x482.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="482" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14869299" class="wp-caption-text">王珞桦精彩扯铃表演。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14869300" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14869300" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869300-08.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14869300" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869300-08-600x447.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="447" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14869300" class="wp-caption-text">中华养生德艺队则演出32式太极剑。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14869301" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14869301" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869301-09.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14869301" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869301-09-600x365.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="365" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14869301" class="wp-caption-text">德州元极舞带来融合太极、传统舞蹈与功夫元素的《乾坤如意扇》。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>此外，德州元极舞带来融合太极、传统舞蹈与功夫元素的《乾坤如意扇》；林天娜与章翠英以歌声传递追逐梦想、勇敢向前的正能量；扯铃队队长王珞桦带来精彩扯铃，展现民俗技艺的活力。“天下第一班”以太极扇、民族舞蹈及健身操呈现多元风貌，中华养生德艺队则演出32式太极剑与太极拳，展现身法与剑法合一的功底。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14869293" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14869293" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869293-01.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14869293" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14869293-01-600x437.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="437" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14869293" class="wp-caption-text">115年双十国庆茶会活动最后全场共同合唱《明天会更好》。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>活动最后由处长萧伊芳领唱《明天会更好》，全场共同合唱，为茶会画下温馨句点。主办单位并准备台湾家乡风味点心餐盒及双十纪念小物，与侨胞分享节庆喜悦，凝聚海外台湾人的情谊。</p>
