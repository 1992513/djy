<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>西雅图侨界庆祝双十国庆升旗典礼</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868818-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-1-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>10月10日，西雅图侨学各界在中国城庆喜公园举行双十国庆升旗典礼，与会人士见证中华民国国旗升起。（舜华/大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年10月13日讯】（记者马明报导）10月10日，西雅图侨学各界在中国城庆喜公园举行双十国庆升旗典礼，庆祝中华民国115年国庆。中华会馆与侨界社团共同筹办活动，侨界人士、社团代表及义工齐聚一堂，唱国歌、见证国旗冉冉升起，并祝福中华民国国运昌隆。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14868819" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14868819" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868819-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-9.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14868819" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868819-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-9-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14868819" class="wp-caption-text">西雅图双十国庆升旗典礼现场，驻西雅图台北经济文化办事处处长林美呈致词。（舜华/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>驻西雅图台北经济文化办事处处长林美呈在致词时，感谢中华会馆干部、各侨团与义工共同促成活动。林处长表示，现场布置与各项筹备工作并非一人之功，而是众人共同努力的成果，并邀请在场人士以掌声向筹办团队致意。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14868824" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14868824" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868824-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-3.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14868824" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868824-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-3-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14868824" class="wp-caption-text">青年们共同托起巨幅中华民国国旗，在西雅图中国城参加双十国庆游行。（舜华/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>谈及台湾的民主发展，林处长指出，台湾于30年前首次举行总统直选，是民主发展的重要里程碑。此后，台湾的民主持续深化，自由、民主与人权已成为民众坚持的价值，也是台湾与世界民主伙伴共同维护的理念。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14868825" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14868825" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868825-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-4.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14868825" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868825-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-4-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14868825" class="wp-caption-text">侨界人士手持中华民国与美国国旗，在西雅图中国城游行，共庆双十国庆。（舜华/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>林处长并表示，台美长期维持密切的经贸关系。随着人工智能（AI）时代到来，台湾凭借精密制造能力，与美国建立更紧密的合作关系。未来双方将持续深化科技合作，强化彼此的伙伴关系。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14868826" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14868826" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868826-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-7.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14868826" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868826-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-7-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14868826" class="wp-caption-text">与会人士在庆喜公园与舞龙舞狮团队合影，彩狮展示“风调雨顺”“国泰民安”等祝福条幅。（舜华/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>面对快速变化的国际情势，林处长感谢海外侨胞长期给予中华民国台湾支持与鼓励，期盼乡亲继续与办事处携手，深化台美关系。她也以英语向现场来宾致意，重申民主、自由与人权等共同价值是台美关系的重要基础，并祝福大家国庆快乐。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14868827" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14868827" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868827-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-8.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14868827" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868827-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-8-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14868827" class="wp-caption-text">双十国庆升旗典礼上，年老一代将中华民国国旗交接到年轻一代手上。（舜华/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>西雅图华侨文教服务中心主任钟佩珍在致词时表示，在西雅图唱自己国家的国歌、看见国旗冉冉升起，内心十分感动。钟主任感谢中华会馆及筹办团队，并向侨务委员会FASCA海外青年文化大使与在场社团致谢，感谢大家共同庆祝中华民国115年国庆。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14868822" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14868822" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868822-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-10.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14868822" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868822-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-10-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14868822" class="wp-caption-text">西雅图华侨文教服务中心主任钟佩珍致词。（舜华/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>西雅图中华会馆副主席邱美瑞致词时也提到，活动前一天阴雨不断，原本担心当天仍会下雨，所幸典礼迎来好天气。她感谢义工提早策划、到场布置，以及办事处与侨教中心同仁提供各方面的支持，并向参与的侨团和乡亲致谢，表示正因大家投入，才有当天热闹的场面。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14868823" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14868823" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868823-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-12-s.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14868823" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868823-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-12-s-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14868823" class="wp-caption-text">西雅图中华会馆副主席邱美瑞致词。（舜华/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>升旗典礼前，侨胞们在西雅图中国城游行，共同庆祝双十国庆。各侨团与乡亲沿街前行，为中国城增添热闹的节庆气氛，也展现侨界的凝聚力。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14868829" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14868829" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868829-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-6.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14868829" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868829-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-6-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14868829" class="wp-caption-text">美国西北地区台湾大学校友在庆喜公园的双十国庆拱门下合影。（舜华/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14868830" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14868830" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868830-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-11.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14868830" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14868830-21-TaiwanCelebratesNationalDay-11-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14868830" class="wp-caption-text">FASCA海外青年文化大使协会成员身穿黄色上衣，在双十国庆活动现场合影。（舜华/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
