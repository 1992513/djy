<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>组图：直击许家印在伦敦的荒置豪宅</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14866423-1jpg.jpeg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
