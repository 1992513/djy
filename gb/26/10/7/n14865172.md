<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>组图：好莱坞传奇女星爱娃·玛丽·森特去世</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14865188-GettyImages-103641575.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>【大纪元2026年10月07日讯】（大纪元记者许家琳综合报导）曾出演《码头风云》并荣获奥斯卡奖的好莱坞传奇女星爱娃·玛丽·森特（Eva Marie Saint）于10月6日去世，享年102岁。她是好莱坞黄金时代最后仅存的巨星之一。</p><p>爱娃·玛丽·森特曾与加里·格兰特（Cary Grant）和马龙·白兰度（Marlon Brando）等巨星搭档并有出色表现。森特的公关人员杰夫·桑德森（Jeff Sanderson）在一份书面声明中表示，森特于周二（10月6日）在洛杉矶家中去世，去世时家人和亲友陪伴在侧，死因为自然原因。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>1954年，她在伊利亚·卡赞（Elia Kazan）执导、传奇影星马龙·白兰度主演的《码头风云》中，出色演绎了伊迪·道尔（Edie Doyle）一角，从而家喻户晓。凭借这一银幕处女作中的精彩表现，她荣获奥斯卡最佳女配角奖。当时她即将临盆，在欣喜领奖时还打趣道：“我可能就在这儿把孩子生下来了！”</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:450px" class="alignnone"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14865191-GettyImages-103641579.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14865191-GettyImages-103641579.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14865191-GettyImages-103641579.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14865191-GettyImages-103641579.jpg" alt="&lt;span class=&quot;HwtZe&quot;
