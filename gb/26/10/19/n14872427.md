<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>战争风险扩大下应否捍卫盟友 美国读者这样看</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14872429-2026-10-16-epoch-poll-outside.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>

print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div
