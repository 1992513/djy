<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>欧冠第2轮：大巴黎大胜曼城 王马逆转罗马</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14870076-GettyImages-2300222065.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
