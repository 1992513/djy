<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>【车评】探索野外的好伙伴 2026 Honda Passport TrailSport Elite</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867266-IMG_1026-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>2026 Honda Passport TrailSport Elite。（李奥／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年10月10日讯】步入2026年，Honda Passport有了全新设计的外貌，这辆属于Passport车系的第四代产品，新的外形设计更为有型，更为粗犷。Passport有三种主要车型，分别为RTL、TrailSport和TrailSport Elite，这周测试的是2026年款Honda TrailSport Elite。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14867222" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14867222" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867222-IMG_1045.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14867222" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867222-IMG_1045-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14867222" class="wp-caption-text">2026 Honda Passport TrailSport Elite。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Honda Passport TrailSport Elite前方底部装上了两个红色挂勾，牵引高达5,000磅，可用于拖拉船只、露营车等。鲜艳橙色的车身，是Honda少有的选择，在阳光下也会闪闪发亮，头灯组由橙色LED灯围绕，份外好看。再加上前方作为保护的钢制护板，再加上Continental CrossContact ATR全地形轮胎，增强越野能力。橙色的“TrailSport”徽章份外显眼，与同为橙色的车身，份外搭配。电动折叠后视镜，以及加热的挡风玻璃雨刷，在寒冷天气下也不会被卡住。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14867225" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14867225" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867225-IMG_1030.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14867225" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867225-IMG_1030-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14867225" class="wp-caption-text">外形更粗犷。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Passport搭载了在林肯厂制造的3.5升DOHC V6引擎，并搭配10速自动变速箱，提供285匹马力和262 磅英尺扭矩，比上一代增加了5匹。Passport采用Honda轻型卡车架构，该平台首次应用于全新Honda Pilot，具备极高刚性，专为越野能力设计。测试时，3.5升引擎的动力充沛，城市及高速公路都难不到它，此外，Passport的强大动力又可应付各种越野路段。Passport提供七种驾驶模式，包括雪地、运动、沙地等模式。Sport模式下的加速表现迅猛，动力输出顺畅，Normal模式则提供更平顺、舒适的驾驶体验。车内的变速控制是由Acura移值过来的变速按键，由上至下为P、R、N及D／S，按键大小各有不同。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14867226" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14867226" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867226-IMG_1048.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14867226" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867226-IMG_1048-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14867226" class="wp-caption-text">3.5升DOHC V6引擎。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>配置了i-VTM4矩矢量全轮驱动及其四模式智能牵引力管理系统，i-VTM4使用主动扭矩矢量将高达70％的发动机扭矩发送到后轴，并将100％的扭矩发送到左后轮或右后轮。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14867227" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14867227" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867227-IMG_1037.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14867227" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867227-IMG_1037-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14867227" class="wp-caption-text">后灯组。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>进入车内，2026年Passport TrailSport的内饰设计以实用性和舒适性为核心，同时融入现代科技。标配12.3英寸触控萤幕，支援Google Built-In、Amazon Alexa以及无线Apple CarPlay和Android Auto，操作直观且反应灵敏。TrailSport车型采用条纹座椅面料，搭配类似粗麻布的仪表板和车门饰板，增添运动与户外风格。TrailSport Elite则提供更高级的皮革座椅和座椅通风功能，兼顾豪华感。后排腿部和头部空间宽敞，后货舱提供44立方英尺的储物空间，折叠第二排后可扩展至84立方英尺，足以应对家庭出游或户外活动的需求。每款车型均标配备用轮胎，且可选配全尺寸备胎，放置于货舱内，体现Honda对实用性的重视。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14867230" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14867230" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867230-IMG_1049.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14867230" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867230-IMG_1049-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14867230" class="wp-caption-text">内饰以实用和舒适为主。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>安全方面，Passport TrailSport搭载Honda Sensing系统，包括碰撞缓解制动、车道保持辅助、交通标志识别等功能，确保日常驾驶的安全性。还有360度的全景摄象机，把周围环境完全显现，在越野时更为有用，初时找不到摄象机按钮，后来在右边的雨刷杆末端找到，用起来更为方便。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14867231" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14867231" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867231-IMG_1061.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14867231" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867231-IMG_1061-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14867231" class="wp-caption-text">双色座椅。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>尽管Passport TrailSport主打越野，但其在日常驾驶中的表现同样令人满意。悬挂系统平衡了舒适性与稳定性，车内隔音效果出色，即使搭载全地形轮胎，胎噪也控制得宜。与Toyota 4Runner和Ford Bronco等车身框架式SUV相比，Passport TrailSport的单体式车身设计在越野能力上稍逊，但在公路驾驶中提供更舒适的体验。而相较于自家的CR-V TrailSport，Passport提供更高阶的越野装备，如护板和专属悬挂调校，适合更严苛的地形。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14867232" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14867232" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867232-IMG_1054.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14867232" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867232-IMG_1054-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14867232" class="wp-caption-text">仪表板。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14867233" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14867233" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867233-IMG_1053.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14867233" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867233-IMG_1053-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14867233" class="wp-caption-text">前台萤幕。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>凭借其强大的V6引擎、专为越野设计的装备以及宽敞的内部空间，使其在竞争激烈的市场中脱颖而出，对于寻求冒险与家庭用车平衡的消费者而言，Passport TrailSport无疑是一款值得考虑的选择。其价格合理、可靠性高，且 Honda的工程品质为其加分不少。无论是穿越城市街道还是探索乡野小径，2026年Passport TrailSport都能成为您的可靠伙伴。◇</p>
<figure id="attachment_14867236" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14867236" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867236-IMG_1057.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14867236" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14867236-IMG_1057-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14867236" class="wp-caption-text">变速按钮。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
