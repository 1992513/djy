<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>守护亚裔投票权 德州民权团体与族裔媒体研讨</h2>
<h6></h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年10月27日讯】（大纪元记者陆希休斯顿报导）随着11月3日德州中期选举投票日临近，美国社区媒体（American Community Media）与亚裔法律联盟（Asian Law Caucus）于10月22日共同举办线上专题讲座，主题为“AAPI德州人投票2026：族裔媒体需要提前知道的事”。会议由Sandy Close主持，邀请多位亚太裔（AAPI）倡议人士与投票权律师，深入解析选民暂缓名单（voter suspense list）、资格审查及投票所障碍等议题，期盼透过少数族裔媒体扩大传播，保障选民权益。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14877193" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14877193" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877193-01.jpg"><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14877193" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877193-01-600x393.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="393" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14877193" class="wp-caption-text">AAPI德州人投票2026线上专题讲座。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14877194" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14877194" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877194-02.png"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14877194" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877194-02-600x344.png" alt="" width="600" b="344" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14877194" class="wp-caption-text">亚洲法律联盟民主与全国倡议律师Kimberly Leung。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>针对选举当天的突发状况，亚裔法律联盟律师Kimberly Leung指出，若民众在投票现场遭遇恐吓或违规行为应主动举报。选举保护热线团队将第一时间与选务人员协调，必要时由律师团队向法院申请紧急司法救济。热线提供英语、亚裔多语种、西班牙语、阿拉伯语及手语支援。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14877195" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14877195" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877195-03.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14877195" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877195-03-600x351.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="351" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14877195" class="wp-caption-text">德州民权专案投票权专员律师Nina Oishi。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>德州民权计划（TCRP）律师Nina Oishi补充说明，德州法律明文规定行动不便者及长者享有优先排队与要求座椅的权利，各投票所须张贴相关告示。此外，德州部分寺庙与清真寺亦设置为合法投票所，便利多元社群就近投票。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14877196" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14877196" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877196-04.png"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14877196" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877196-04-600x349.png" alt="" width="600" b="349" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14877196" class="wp-caption-text">亚裔德州正义组织执行董事Nabila Mansoor。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>面对生成式AI带来的假资讯挑战，Nina Oishi建议媒体与选民遵循“举报但不转发”原则，避免二次扩散。她呼吁大家参考《德州选举法规》、各县选举官网、州务卿（SOS）网站及TCRP提供的单页指南。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14877197" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14877197" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877197-05.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14877197" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877197-05-600x336.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="336" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14877197" class="wp-caption-text">SAAVETX（南亚裔美国选民）创办人兼主席/德州赋权教育基金Chanda Parbhoo。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>研究显示，超过三分之一的AAPI社群成员在明知资讯可能不实的情况下仍会转发，显见族裔媒体在查证与警示上的职责至关重要。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14877198" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14877198" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877198-06.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14877198" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14877198-06-600x359.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="359" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14877198" class="wp-caption-text">选举保护热线电话总整理。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>德州亚裔正义组织（ATJ）执行长Nabila Mansoor与德州南亚裔选民赋权教育基金会（SAAVETX）创办人Chanda Parbhoo强调，破除“投票无用论”是当务之急。在竞争激烈的基层选战中，少数族裔的关键选票能发挥决定性作用。民权团体正推动全年无休的社群动员，透过包车接送学生与选民、提供多语种宣导，全力提升早期投票（Early Voting）参与率。</p>
