<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>【车评】锐利动感 操控佳 2026 Lexus IS 350 F Sport AWD</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880327-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_01-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>2026 Lexus IS 350 F Sport AWD。（李奥／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年10月31日讯】迈入2026年，Lexus IS车系的第三代产品自2013年推出市场，至今已有十多年历史，经历了2017及2020年的中期改款（Facelift），到了2025年，IS进行了第三次变身，主要是外型上的改变，以及内部大更新，而今周测试的是自IS 350 F Sport AWD。</p>
<p><a loading="lazy" title="YouTube video player" data2-src=""></a><a href="https://d1tvvb2j7fpfrx.cloudfront.net/B8pBVjoWrnhp"><img width="600" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/1992513/djy/master/gb/300/djtsp.jpg" title="进入原文观看"  alt="进入原文观看"></a><h3 align=center>(视频.国内建议下载<a href="https://github.com/1992513/www/blob/master/README.md#8">翻墙软件</a>进入原文观看)</h3><a src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/QFQnVSIcMaA?si=ph2ya5T5UJP2t0-h" width="560" b="315" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></a></p>
<p>2026年Lexus IS 350沿用了2025年Facelift后的外形，头灯组是保留了上一次改款的锐利设计，钩形的LED日间行车灯在大灯上方，而大灯由三个方形的LED组成。主要改动是中间巨形Spindle进风口，上半部分缩小了，再加上一个新的鼻尖，看起来有如全新设计一样；头灯下方的入风口形状也改动了，并配上新的裙脚，更有动感。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14880307" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14880307" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880307-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_09.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14880307" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880307-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_09-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14880307" class="wp-caption-text">Spindle进风口。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>车尾的“一眉”设计可算保留了，改动很少，新加的尾翼加强了动感，实际功能不大。此外，车尾下方装上了黑色塑料板，有三块导风板。测试的IS 350轮圈轮胎是19吋，正幅式双条设计轮圈，轮胎都是235／40R19（前）及265 ／35R19（后），后轮比前轮更宽，既有好看的效果又有高操控的性能。刹车卡钳采用红色涂装，再一次加强动感，前13.1英寸透气刹车碟配四活塞卡钳，后11.7英寸透气刹车碟配两活塞卡钳，刹车能力超强！</p>
<figure id="attachment_14880309" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14880309" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880309-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_06.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14880309" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880309-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_06-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14880309" class="wp-caption-text">2026 Lexus IS 350 F Sport AWD。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14880310" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14880310" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880310-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_12.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14880310" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880310-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_12-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14880310" class="wp-caption-text">235／40R19（前）及265 ／35R19（后）。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>为了增强车身刚度，Lexus对悬架设置进行了多次改良。新IS装有的螺旋弹簧，其重量比即旧有款式轻了20％。新型悬挂A臂，是以铝合金材料锻造，从而减轻了18％重量。悬架防滚杆用上了新材料，重量也减轻17％。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14880311" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14880311" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880311-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_20.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14880311" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880311-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_20-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14880311" class="wp-caption-text">车尾底部扰流器。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>2026年款的IS 350，引擎采用排汽量3.5公升V6排列，虽不及之前测试的IS 500的5公升V8引擎，但最大马力也有311匹，扭矩也有280磅呎，压缩比为11.8：1，0-60英里／时的加速时间为6秒，极速锁定在143英里／时。变速箱是8速自动变速箱。测试车是AWD，即四轮驱动，操控极佳，只是加速柔和一点，但在Sport及Sport＋模式时，引擎声响更响亮，驾驶起来更有乐趣。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14880312" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14880312" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880312-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_24.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14880312" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880312-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_24-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14880312" class="wp-caption-text">3.5公升V6排列引擎。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>IS 350外装是低调的灰色，厂方称为“WIND”，配以红黑双色内装，装有电热三幅式方向盘、变档拨片、八方向电动前排座位并配有三段式电热及降温等，可惜IS并没有其他Lexus车系的自动调温座椅。仪表板是一个大萤幕，可显示不同汽车资讯，如轮胎气压、行车里数、行车模式及档位等资讯，并且是“扇形”设计，左上及右上角便不会被方向盘阻挡。中控台萤幕为12.3英寸，采用触碰控制，反应灵敏。中控台也经重新设计，以往的CD音响退役了，Remote Touchpad操控板也取消了，恒温系统移到萤幕下方，大按钮容易使用。再往下是两个USB Type C插口，最底部是手机无线充电板。车后玻璃也装有太阳挡，可在驾驶座“遥控”开关。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14880314" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14880314" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880314-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_25.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14880314" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880314-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_25-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14880314" class="wp-caption-text">大萤幕仪表板。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14880315" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14880315" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880315-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_26.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14880315" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880315-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_26-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14880315" class="wp-caption-text">中控台萤幕为12.3英寸。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>IS 350的方向盘与变速杆操作上都很顺畅很轻松，变速杆也备有+/-手动变速功能，要专注驾驶，双手都要握住方向盘，用不了+／-功能？那么也可使用在方向盘后方的Paddle Shift。用作改变行车模式的银色小圆盘，在变速杆右方，IS 350有“Eco”、“Normal”、“Sport”、“Sport+”及“Custom”五种，Normal及Custom是按下去选择，Sport及Sport＋是右转，而Eco是左转，小圆盘在转动后会回弹中间。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14880316" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14880316" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880316-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_28.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14880316" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880316-2026_Lexus_IS_350_AWD_28-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14880316" class="wp-caption-text">自动变速杆。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>今年，Lexus提供的IS系列，只剩下350了，还好有AWD及RWD的选择，再加上不同的等级有不同的设备，也可迎合不同需要的消费者。然而，Sedan的选择也明显日渐减少，除了IS之外，Lexus也只有ES了！◇</p>
