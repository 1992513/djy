<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>中国制造业再陷收缩 出口难填内需缺口</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/10/id14880561-768454.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>【大纪元2026年10月31日讯】（大纪元记者赵彬报导）中国10月官方制造业PMI降至49.3，跌破荣枯线，生产、新订单与出口订单同步转弱。北京将回落归于假期与外部不确定性，但多项数据显示，所谓经济复苏可能更多依赖出口与国家支出，而非可持续的内需。</p><h2><strong>官方说法</strong><strong>受到质疑</strong></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>中共国家统计局10月31日<ahref="https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202610/t20261031_1965719.md#1" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">公布</a>，10月制造业采购经理人指数（PMI）为49.3，较9月的50.1下降0.8个百分点，<ahref="https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinas-factory-activity-seen-flat-october-2026-10-30/" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">低于市场预期</a>的50.0，也是近四个月内第三次处于收缩区间。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>官方将回落解释为“十一”假期前部分需求提前释放，以及国际环境复杂多变所致。国家统计局首席统计师<ahref="https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202610/t20261031_1965719.md#1" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">霍丽慧称</a>，制造业供需两端均有放缓，生产指数降至49.8，新订单指数降至49.4。</p><p>但这一解释难以掩盖更广泛的疲态。<ahref="https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202610/t20261031_1965718.md#1" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">10月非制造业商务活动指数</a>降至49.7，综合PMI产出指数也降至49.7，制造业、服务业与建筑业同时失去动能。《<ahref="https://www.wsj.com/world/asia/china-economic-activity-loses-steam-sharply-in-october-5990ffe0" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">华尔街日报》指出</a>，这是制造业、服务业和建筑业指标同时转弱的罕见组合。</p><h2><strong>出口引擎正在失速</strong></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>最值得警惕的是新出口订单指数。<ahref="https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202610/t20261031_1965718.md#1" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">该指数</a>从9月的50.0降至10月的48.7，重新跌回收缩区间，显示此前可能因圣诞订单提前出货而推高的出口动能，正在迅速消退。</p><p><ahref="https://apnews.com/article/china-manufacturing-economy-pmi-exports-f1e0e24004da425a1b3c8935b16a245c" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">美联社报导称</a>，中国企业在国内需求疲弱下，长期以扩大对外出口寻找买家；但随着贸易顺差扩大，多个贸易伙伴正要求中国重新平衡贸易。日本《<ahref="https://www.sankei.com/article/20261031-ZSXE6WFFC5KLDKE4FDN5PV6QNE/?outputType=asynd" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">产经新闻</a>》也指出，中国过剩生产与贸易顺差引发的摩擦升温，正在压低企业信心。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p><ahref="https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/china/chinas-economic-growth-picks-up-third-quarter-beating-expectations-2026-10-19/" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">路透社的分析则更直接</a>：中国第三季经济增长主要由出口和国家部门带动，国内需求尚未自我维持，房地产市场也未见底。<ahref="https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinas-factory-activity-seen-flat-october-2026-10-30/" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">换言之，</a>9月PMI短暂站上50，未必代表经济真正回暖，而更可能是出口前置、政策托底与统计节奏共同造成的表象。</p><h2><strong>内需疲弱未被修复</strong></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p>官方强调高技术制造业与装备制造业PMI仍分别为51.3和50.6，试图以此证明“新动能”仍在扩张。但消费品行业PMI降至48.8，高耗能行业更降至47.3，显示与普通家庭、就业和传统产业相关的经济活动明显更弱。</p><p><ahref="https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-economic-growth-picks-up-third-quarter-beating-expectations-2026-10-19/" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">路透指出，</a>这种分化暴露了北京增长模式的结构性问题：资源持续流向高端制造、基建与国家支持的产业，而居民消费、民间投资与房地产仍处于低迷。路透社此前报导，零售销售持续落后于工业产出，房地产下行已连续多年侵蚀家庭财富。</p><p><ahref="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-31/china-factory-activity-manufacturing-purchasing-managers-index-services" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">彭博社则认为</a>，10月数据凸显政策制定者正面临更大压力：既要维持全年增长目标，又必须应对经济复苏失去动能的风险。<ahref="https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-economic-growth-picks-up-third-quarter-beating-expectations-2026-10-19/" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">路透进一步指出</a>，若政策仍主要依靠政府投资、信贷投放与产业补贴，而非提高家庭收入和修复住房市场信心，其效果能否持续，仍存在很大疑问。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><h2><strong>官方叙事与现实落差</strong></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>中共官方将10月回落定性为假期错位和外部冲击，并强调企业生产经营预期指数仍达53.4，试图维持“经济长期向好”的叙事。但生产、新订单、采购、出口订单及非制造业活动<ahref="https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202610/t20261031_1965718.md#1" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">同时回落</a>，显示企业对未来的信心未必能转化为实际投资、招聘和消费。</p><p><ahref="https://apnews.com/article/china-manufacturing-economy-pmi-exports-f1e0e24004da425a1b3c8935b16a245c" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">美联社表示，</a>对北京而言，真正的问题不在于10月PMI是否受假期影响，而在于出口、政府支出与国家产业政策能否继续掩盖内需不足。<ahref="https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/china/chinas-economic-growth-picks-up-third-quarter-beating-expectations-2026-10-19/" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">当出口订单回落</a>、贸易摩擦上升、房地产仍无起色时，官方所描绘的复苏图景，正面临越来越多的现实检验。</p><p>责任编辑：孙芸#
