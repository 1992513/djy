<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>华林功夫馆庆20周年 创始人余翠梅谈习武心路</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828025-DSC06515-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>8月9日，麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。现场演出以战鼓拉开序幕。（刘景烨/大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年08月12日讯】（大纪元记者刘景烨昆士市报导）20头彩狮摇头摆尾，2条舞龙上下翻飞，师徒数十人展示拳脚兵器，锣鼓和喝采声音不绝于耳。</p>
<p>8月9日傍晚在昆士市（Quincy）龙凤酒楼，麻州“华林功夫太极馆”（Wah Lum Kung Fu &amp; Tai Chi Academy）庆祝20周年。其创始人余翠梅（Mai Du）表示，对这段习武、授徒和服务公益的历程充满感恩。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14828027" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828027" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828027-DSC06542.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828027" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828027-DSC06542-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828027" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。20头彩狮齐齐献瑞。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14828028" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828028" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828028-DSC06561.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828028" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828028-DSC06561-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828028" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。20头彩狮齐齐献瑞。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>华林功夫太极馆由余翠梅创办于麻州摩顿市（Malden），目前在昆士市和波士顿市西罗克斯伯里（West Roxbury）皆有分校。据武馆网站介绍，余翠梅是华林北派探腿螳螂拳的第8代传人，师承新英格兰地区华林派国术总会首席教官罗森（Bob Rosen），曾在中国的多次武术比赛中获得金、银、铜等不同奖项。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14828026" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828026" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828026-DSC06529.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828026" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828026-DSC06529-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828026" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。嘉宾颁赠褒奖。左起：摩顿市市议员Stephen Winslow、武馆创始人余翠梅、摩顿市市长Gary Christenson。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14828032" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828032" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828032-DSC06605.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828032" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828032-DSC06605-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828032" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。华林派传人合影，左起：Amy Tran、余翠梅、Bob Rosen、Andrea So。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>“过去20年，华林功夫太极馆让我的生活更充实、更温暖、更有意义。我们有宏大而清晰的目标，就是培养下一代的身心健康，让他们彼此相连，融入社会，像家人一样互相陪伴，庆祝我们的文化和传统，同时也让他们懂得努力工作、自律、自尊、尊重他人，连接超越世代的血脉和传承。”余翠梅说。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14828031" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828031" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828031-DSC06588.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828031" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828031-DSC06588-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828031" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。学员们表演刀盾。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14828033" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828033" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828033-DSC06614.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828033" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828033-DSC06614-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828033" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆庆祝20周年。2条彩龙齐舞，象征20年精彩历程。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>她说，武馆的教官和学员们不仅训练武艺、参加比赛、从事表演，还投身公益服务，比如倡导反对歧视亚裔，在社区活动中教导防身术，为节日庆典表演舞龙舞狮等等。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14828034" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828034" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828034-DSC06627.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828034" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828034-DSC06627-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828034" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。学员们表演彩带舞。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14828035" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828035" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828035-DSC06637.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828035" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828035-DSC06637-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828035" class="wp-caption-text">华林功夫太极馆庆祝20周年。武术老师Mimi Chan表演旗舞。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>余翠梅还说，看到在台上表演的年轻人如此充满能量，希望他们秉持初心，精诚团结，继续为社会带来正面力量。“我希望看到这家武馆发展得更壮大、更坚实，而不是大而不稳。”她说。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14828036" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828036" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828036-DSC06639.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828036" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828036-DSC06639-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828036" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。学员们表演拳脚功夫。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14828040" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828040" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828040-DSC06654.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828040" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828040-DSC06654-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828040" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆庆祝20周年。约500来宾参加晚宴，观赏演出。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>今年50岁、习武38年的余翠梅感慨“饮水思源”，特别感谢老师罗森和师公陈培，因为他们不仅传授武术，更推动华林派在全美各地繁茂成长。她也感谢各位教官，以及200多名学员对武馆的长期支持。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14828037" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828037" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828037-DSC06645.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828037" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828037-DSC06645-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828037" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。武馆教官Amy Tran和Andrea So表演兵器对抗。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h2><strong>嘉宾褒奖 宗族道贺</strong></h2>
<p>20周年庆典当天，约500位嘉宾齐聚昆士市龙凤酒楼，支持华林功夫太极馆的事业，欣赏教官和学员们的演出。</p>
<p>摩顿市长柯廷森（Gary Christenson），昆士市长柯奇（Thomas Koch），麻州众议员黄子安（Donald Wong）等嘉宾，皆到场向余翠梅颁赠褒奖。麻州州长希莉（Maura Healey），波士顿市长吴弭（Michelle Wu）等多位政要，也寄来奖状，祝贺华林派取得成就。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14828023" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828023" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828023-DSC06468.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828023" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828023-DSC06468-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828023" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。为彩狮点睛的嘉宾合影，右1为昆士市市长Thomas Koch。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14828024" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828024" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828024-DSC06487.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828024" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828024-DSC06487-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828024" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆庆祝20周年。点睛之后，2头新彩狮起舞。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>来宾们首先在酒楼室外的广场上聚集，为2头新彩狮点睛，并欣赏华林师生的战鼓表演。随后在酒楼内，华林成员们舞动20头彩狮和2条彩龙，象征武馆20年来的精彩历程。</p>
<p>武馆的幼年、青少年和成年学员，以及表演队带来了一系列功夫表演，包括小洪拳、太极拳、七星拳螳螂拳等等。华林功夫太极馆的2位教官，Amy Tran和Andrea So亦表演了兵器攻防。创始人余翠梅压轴登台，表演拳术和鞭法，随后与全体师生共同向来宾致谢。现场欢呼与掌声不绝，非常热闹。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14828041" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828041" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828041-DSC06658.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828041" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828041-DSC06658-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828041" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆在昆士市龙凤酒楼庆祝20周年。余翠梅表演鞭法。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14828042" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828042" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828042-DSC06676.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828042" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828042-DSC06676-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828042" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆庆祝20周年。参与表演的武馆师生合影。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>当天，来自全美多个州的华林派国术总会分支成员，皆到场参与庆典。代表余氏宗族的纽英崙余风采堂，也有约20位宗亲参与庆祝，包括元老余超群、主席余立昌等等。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14828039" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14828039" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828039-DSC06648.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14828039" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14828039-DSC06648-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14828039" class="wp-caption-text">麻州华林功夫太极馆庆祝20周年。余翠梅师傅向来宾们致意。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
