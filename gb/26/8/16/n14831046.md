<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>太麻里金针山花开 千鹤亭八成金黄</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831048-867807-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>山坡上的金针花慢慢铺开，远处山景与蜿蜒山路相映。（翁丽吟提供）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年08月16日讯】（大纪元记者廖俪芬台湾台东报导）台东太麻里金针山的花开了。县议员翁丽吟近日上山查看花况，沿路拍下山坡花田、凉亭步道、农家晒金针及山区景色。橙黄色的金针花已开始覆在绿色坡地，有的沿着道路开，有的在步道旁一整片延伸，金针山的花季已经启动。翁丽吟表示，目前千鹤亭一带花况较好，约开八成；双乳峰约四成。千鹤亭周边的花开得较集中，从凉亭往下看，山坡已见一片金黄；双乳峰还有不少花苞，整体花况正在往上走。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14831054" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14831054" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831054-867813.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14831054" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831054-867813-600x800.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="600" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14831054" class="wp-caption-text">金针花近拍，橙黄色花瓣带着红色线条，是花季里最亮眼的颜色。（翁丽吟提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14831053" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14831053" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831053-867812.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14831053" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831053-867812-600x1067.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="800" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14831053" class="wp-caption-text">蓝天下，金针花开始染黄整片坡地。（翁丽吟提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14831050" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14831050" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831050-867809.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14831050" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831050-867809-600x800.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="600" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14831050" class="wp-caption-text">金针花开满坡地，游客走进花海，也能眺望太麻里山区景致。（翁丽吟提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>花还没全开　山坡已有看头不是每一块坡地都同样满开，有些地方的花刚开，有些仍以花苞为主。不过，已有游客沿着步道慢慢往上走，在凉亭前、山坡边拍照，也有人停下来看山谷景色。金针山的花季，每年花况都会随海拔、日照和天气不同而有差别。今年先开的是千鹤亭一带，双乳峰则还要再等一等。翁丽吟说，想抢先赏花的民众已可上山，但若想看更完整的花海，后续花况还会持续增加。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14831052" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14831052" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831052-867811.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14831052" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831052-867811-600x800.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="600" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14831052" class="wp-caption-text">金针花旁坐落山头，游客可沿步道上行，欣赏山谷景色。（翁丽吟提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>花季也是农家忙的时候除了花海，翁丽吟也拍到农家把采下的金针铺在竹筛上日晒。一排排金针在阳光下干燥，是山上花季里另一种常见画面。金针不只是观光花，也是山区农家的重要作物。从采收、日晒到加工，都得赶着天气处理。山上石头屋也有冷压初榨苦茶油等农产品，游客赏花时，也可以认识当地的农产和生活。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14831058" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14831058" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831058-867817.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14831058" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831058-867817-600x1067.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="800" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14831058" class="wp-caption-text">金针山花坡一路延伸到山头凉亭，登上步道可看见大片花海。（翁丽吟提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14831049" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14831049" style="width: 447px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831049-867808.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14831049" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831049-867808-600x800.jpg" alt="" width="447" b="596" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14831049" class="wp-caption-text">农家采下金针后铺在竹筛上日晒，准备制成干金针。（翁丽吟）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14831057" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14831057" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831057-867816.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14831057" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831057-867816-600x800.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="600" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14831057" class="wp-caption-text">房子周边被金针花包围，游客沿阶梯走进山区花田。（翁丽吟提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14831051" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14831051" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831051-867810.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14831051" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831051-867810-600x1067.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="800" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14831051" class="wp-caption-text">太麻里金针山农民以自然农法。制作的冷压初榨苦茶油。（翁丽吟提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>去年台风影响　山路仍单线管制不过，今年上金针山要先留意路况。翁丽吟说，受去年台风影响，部分道路目前仍不理想，上山路段采单线通车，由现场管制人员持无线电协调车辆轮流前进；机车通行较不受影响。她提醒，山区交通预计要到八月底才会较顺畅。开车游客上山前要预留等候时间，遇到管制不要抢快，依现场人员指挥通行。花已经开了，路还在整理，放慢脚步上山，才能好好看这一季的金针花。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14831059" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14831059" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831059-867818.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14831059" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831059-867818-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14831059" class="wp-caption-text">台东县议员翁丽吟表示，骑机车上金针山，目前交通不受影响。（翁丽吟提供）</figcaption></figure>
