<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>玩桌游培养情绪力 桃园八德国小SEL夏令营打造家庭互动力</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830780-867721-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>SEL夏令营开幕合照。（桃园市府教育局提供）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年08月16日讯】（大纪元记者徐乃义台湾桃园报导）暑假不只是孩子放松休闲的时光，更是亲子共同成长的好机会！桃园市教育局主办“SEL亲子夏令营－家庭桌游玩出好心情”，由八德国小承办活动，邀请桃园市各国小的升二、升三年级学生及家长们共同参与。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14830781" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14830781" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830781-867722.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14830781" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830781-867722-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14830781" class="wp-caption-text">亲子共同进行SEL桌游体验。（桃园市府教育局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>活动邀请长期投入社会情绪学习推广的苏吉禾老师及陈宝美老师担任讲师，透过桌游、情绪探索及亲子互动，引导家庭在欢笑中学习社会情绪能力（SEL），建立更紧密、更有温度的亲子关系。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14830782" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14830782" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830782-867723.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14830782" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830782-867723-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14830782" class="wp-caption-text">讲师引导亲子讨论情绪与表达。（桃园市府教育局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>夏令营以桌游作为学习媒介，在讲师的带领下，透过合作挑战、情绪探索、静心瓶DIY及亲子互动等多元课程，引导家长与孩子练习倾听、合作、表达感受及理解彼此需求，并在游戏与分享中学习以正向方式面对情绪与解决问题，让SEL自然融入生活情境。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14830783" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14830783" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830783-867724.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14830783" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830783-867724-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14830783" class="wp-caption-text">亲子合作完成桌游挑战。（桃园市府教育局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>活动现场笑声不断，亲子携手完成桌游任务、分享游戏心得。不少家长表示，平时难得有机会放下手机与工作，全心陪伴孩子一起游戏，透过活动重新认识孩子的想法，也学习用更多理解与鼓励取代责备。孩子则在游戏中学会等待、合作与表达情绪，展现良好的学习成果。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14830784" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14830784" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830784-867725.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14830784" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830784-867725-450x600.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="600" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14830784" class="wp-caption-text">静心瓶DIY体验活动。（桃园市府教育局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>近年来，社会情绪学习（Social Emotional Learning, SEL）已成为国际教育的重要趋势，强调培养孩子自我觉察、自我管理、社会觉察、人际互动及负责任的决策等五大核心能力。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14830785" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14830785" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830785-867726.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14830785" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830785-867726-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14830785" class="wp-caption-text">静心瓶观察。（桃园市府教育局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>八德国小持续深耕SEL教育，规划教师增能、学生课程、亲职教育及家庭共学活动，希望让SEL从校园延伸至家庭，陪伴孩子培养情绪调节能力与正向人际互动。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14830786" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14830786" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830786-867727.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14830786" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830786-867727-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14830786" class="wp-caption-text">情绪东南西北。（桃园市府教育局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>八德国小辅导主任杨绣如表示，家庭是孩子第一个学习情绪与人际互动的重要场域，期待透过SEL夏令营，让家长认识社会情绪学习的重要性，并将活动经验带回家庭，让亲子在日常生活中持续练习倾听、同理与沟通，共同营造温暖支持的家庭氛围。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14830788" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14830788" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830788-867728.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14830788" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830788-867728-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14830788" class="wp-caption-text">亲子互动交流欢乐时光。（桃园市府教育局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14830789" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14830789" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830789-867729.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-14830789" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830789-867729-450x320.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="320" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14830789" class="wp-caption-text">颁发亲子结业证书。（桃园市府教育局提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>八德国小谢雅莉校长表示，教育不只是知识的传授，更重视品格与情绪能力的培养。未来八德国小将持续整合校内外资源，推动SEL教育，透过课程、亲职教育及多元体验活动，携手家庭共同陪伴孩子建立幸福力、韧性力与良好的人际能力，让社会情绪学习真正落实于校园与家庭，陪伴孩子自信迎向未来。◇</p>
