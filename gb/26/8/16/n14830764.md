<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>新唐人武术大赛落幕 评委：传统武术在复兴</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830649-LD103503.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
