<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>迪士尼发布新动画《Clay》 金球影帝基伦克金配音</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830898-20260816-mark-disney13.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>

Culkin）将为主角配音。</p><p>迪士尼原创动画电影《Clay》（暂名）在周五D23博览会上首度亮相<ahref="https://www.instagram.com/reels/DcDC7rBCALd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"
