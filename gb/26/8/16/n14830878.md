<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>台打歌节目周日开播 千田爱纱与洪暐哲搭档主持</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830881-eff8fd2807289aa0990c684443ac9dc9.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>【大纪元2026年08月16日讯】（大纪元记者马维芬报导）年代MUCH全新音乐节目《我就是要-打歌舞台》将于本周日（16日）晚间8点首播。由千田爱纱与洪暐哲领军主持，首集邀请台湾男团U:NUS与FEniX上节目竞技，并开放粉丝进棚，近距离为偶像应援。</p><p>记者会一开场，两位主持人便以限定团名“纱瓦组合”向大家打招呼。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>节目规则需要来宾透过游戏闯关争取打歌机会。本以为两团因此会爆发浓烈火药味，没想到两团私下革命情感浓厚，问答环节互相接哏默契十足。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>反倒是FEniX成员夏浦洋因电影拍摄缺席，陈峻廷竟戏瘾上身，语重心长说“尊重他的决定”，让主持人爱纱哭笑不得，直呼：“原来是团体内部才有火药味啊！”</p><div><figure style="max-width:596px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14830882-ba26fe929de9c123ca415723e61ad2b8.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px"
