<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>美国买卖房子必备英文 何谓Listing agent？</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/01/id14679886-f6296c4b9413de3bdfc85cc81cb9d405.jpeg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>在美国买房卖房，有一个很重要的角色，就是房屋经纪人，学名是Realtor，一般都称为Agent。两方经纪人合作，把交易撮合成功，然后按成交价分佣金。</p><p>绝大部分的买卖都会有两个Agent，一个是代表卖家、为卖家服务的Seller’s Agent，一个是代表买家、为买家服务的Buyer’s Agent。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>卖方的挂牌经纪人又叫Listing agent。在美国郊区常见到待售房子门口插着一个牌子，上面写着：</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>“For Sale – Call Jane Doe, Listing Agent”</p><p>这表示Jane Doe就是这套房子的卖方代理人。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>Listing agent</strong><strong>的</strong><strong>职责</strong></span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>Listing agent的核心工作是帮卖家把房子推向市场、定价、宣传、谈判并完成交易。他是卖方的“代言人”，和买方经纪人一起把房屋交易撮合完成。</p><p>举个例子，张先生想卖掉在纽约的一套公寓，他请了一位listing agent。这位经纪人帮他定价$850,000，在房源共用网络（MLS）上发布，配上专业照片；举办了两个周末的open house（开放参观屋）。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>最终引来三位买家竞价，最后卖到$890,000。Closing交割后，张先生付给listing agent佣金（commission），通常2.5%到3%。</p><p>Listing company是挂牌经纪人所属的房地产经纪公司（brokerage）。有时listing agent也会兼任买方经纪人，这时就叫“双重代理”（dual agency）。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>List</strong><strong>的其它用法</strong></span></h2><h3><strong>? 公司上市、挂牌</strong></h3><p>Listed还可以指公司股票在交易所挂牌交易的行为或状态。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>例：Apple is listed on NASDAQ.（苹果公司在纳斯达克上市。）</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>所以，Listed company / Public company就是指已经在交易所上市的公司。</p><p>例：Microsoft is a listed company.（微软是一家上市公司。）</p><p>例：A listed company must follow strict reporting rules.（上市公司必须遵守严格的披露规定。）</p><p>Stock exchange list / Listing是交易所的“股票名单”，即交易所允许交易的公司股票的集合。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><h3><strong>? 歌曲上榜</strong></h3><p>这里的listed?指的是列入官方榜单，强调“榜上有名”。</p><p>例：The new single is listed on the top 10 of Billboard this week.</p><p>（这首新单曲本周挤进Billboard前十榜单。）</p><h3><strong>? 列为世界遗产</strong></h3><p>例：The site is listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.</p><p>（联合国教科文组织将该遗址列为世界遗产。）</p><h3><strong>? 列入金氏世界纪录</strong></h3><p>英语通常说“be listed in the Guinness World Records”或“be recognized by the Guinness World Records”。</p><p>例：The largest pizza was listed in the Guinness World Records.</p><p>（这个超大披萨创下金氏世界纪录。）</p><p>例：He was recognized by the Guinness World Records for the longest fingernails.</p><p>（他的超长指甲获金氏世界纪录认证。）</p><h3><strong>? 列为处方药</strong></h3><p>例：The drug is listed as preaion-only.（这是处方药。）@*</p><p>责任编辑：沈少棋
