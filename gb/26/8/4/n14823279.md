<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>第二届波士顿华人排球赛 35队竞技联谊</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823261-DSC05783-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>8月1日和2日，第二届波士顿华人排球邀请赛在麻州埃弗雷特市（Everett）举办。年轻人们在赛场上激烈竞技。（刘景烨/大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年08月04日讯】（大纪元记者刘景烨埃弗雷特市报导）8月1日和2日，麻州埃弗雷特市（Everett）联合纽英崙中华公所，举办第二届“波士顿华人排球邀请赛”，迎来波士顿地区及纽约的总计35支队伍。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14823272" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823272" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823272-DSC05895.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823272" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823272-DSC05895-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823272" class="wp-caption-text">8月1日和2日，第二届波士顿华人排球邀请赛在埃弗雷特市（Everett）举办。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14823270" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823270" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823270-DSC05873.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823270" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823270-DSC05873-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823270" class="wp-caption-text">8月1日和2日，第二届波士顿华人排球邀请赛在麻州埃弗雷特市（Everett）举办。市长Robert J. Van Campen与“波士顿涨潮队”合影。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>为期两天的9人制排球赛，在埃弗雷特市（Everett）的绿河公园（Rivergreen Park）举办。今年的场地和主办方与去年第一届赛事相同，但参赛团队数量比去年多7队。所有队伍中，有7队来自纽约，有22个男子球队，以及13个女子球队。虽然天气炎热，大家挥汗如雨，但现场气氛热烈，欢呼与喝采不绝于耳。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14823263" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823263" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823263-DSC05808.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823263" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823263-DSC05808-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823263" class="wp-caption-text">男子球员赛场开球。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14823258" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823258" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823258-DSC05762.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823258" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823258-DSC05762-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823258" class="wp-caption-text">男子队伍在赛场上竞技，如火如荼。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14823262" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823262" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823262-DSC05789.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823262" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823262-DSC05789-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823262" class="wp-caption-text">男子队伍在赛场上竞技，如火如荼。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>最终，“波士顿涨潮队”（Boston Rising Tide）获得男子组冠军，成功卫冕；女子组冠军，由“波士顿飓风队”（Boston Hurricanes）摘得。A组优胜者是“波士顿飓风绿队”（Boston Hurricane Green），少年组优胜为“波士顿剑虹女子队”（Boston Lady Knight Red）。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14823264" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823264" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823264-DSC05810.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823264" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823264-DSC05810-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823264" class="wp-caption-text">男子队伍在赛场上竞技，如火如荼。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14823265" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823265" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823265-DSC05826.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823265" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823265-DSC05826-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823265" class="wp-caption-text">男子队伍在赛场上竞技，如火如荼。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14823260" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823260" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823260-DSC05776.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823260" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823260-DSC05776-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823260" class="wp-caption-text">一场比赛结束后，双方运动员握手。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>除了运动和竞技之外，参赛者表示，这场比赛最有意义的地方，就是增进彼此的友谊。至孝笃亲公所女子排球队的队长Fanny Tsoi说：“看到大家这两年来加深友谊，看到这样的社区连结，活动每年在扩大，这非常好。我们玩这种运动一段时间了，很多朋友在排球场之外都看不到，所以很高兴在场上重逢，以及结交新朋友。”</p>
<p>她还说，她明年还会来参加这个比赛，也期待看到更多队伍加入，一起享受户外排球运动。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14823266" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823266" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823266-DSC05841.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823266" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823266-DSC05841-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823266" class="wp-caption-text">女子排球队成员们聚精会神，竞技赛场。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14823268" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823268" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823268-DSC05852.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823268" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823268-DSC05852-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823268" class="wp-caption-text">女子排球队成员们聚精会神，竞技赛场。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14823269" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823269" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823269-DSC05855.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823269" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823269-DSC05855-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823269" class="wp-caption-text">女子排球队成员们聚精会神，竞技赛场。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>埃弗雷特市市长范坎彭（Robert J. Van Campen），中华公所主席雷国辉、英文书记阮鸿灿，至孝笃亲公所主席陈秀珍等嘉宾，都于8月2日上午到场观赛，与球员们交流及合影。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14823259" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823259" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823259-DSC05770.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823259" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823259-DSC05770-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823259" class="wp-caption-text">球员们在比赛之余，还与观众的宠物互动。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14823271" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823271" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823271-DSC05888.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823271" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823271-DSC05888-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823271" class="wp-caption-text">嘉宾左起：中华公所主席雷国辉、义工Martin Chan、英文书记阮鸿灿、埃弗雷特市长Robert J. Van Campen、幕僚长Chris Connolly、波士顿涨潮队教练Kevin Luke。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14823273" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14823273" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823273-DSC05898.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14823273" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823273-DSC05898-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14823273" class="wp-caption-text">波士顿剑虹队教练Bobby Goon（左）和至孝笃亲公所主席陈秀珍合影。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>范坎彭说，埃弗雷特拥有卓越的城市设施，可以为这样的盛大活动提供妥善的条件。他还说，华人排球赛的成功举办，体现了埃弗雷特市对于华人文化，以及其它多元文化的支持。</p>
<p>“我希望给大家留下这样的印象，埃弗雷特市是一个热情好客的社区，对所有人敞开大门。”范坎彭说。</p>
<p>深度参与赛事筹备工作的阮鸿灿说，看到参赛者们玩得这么开心，交到这么多朋友，这令他非常高兴。◇</p>
