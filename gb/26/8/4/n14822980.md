<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>洋葱怎么料理最健康？</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/04/id14492881-decayed-tooth_1524042713.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>我曾经在演讲中提到，洋葱含有槲皮素，对身体有很多健康效益。台下马上有人问我：“那洋葱到底要怎么料理，才最健康呢？”</p><p>这个问题其实问得非常好。因为洋葱不只是厨房里的配角，也是一个兼具营养与疗愈潜力的食材。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#3e7100"><strong>洋葱的营养价值</strong></span></h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>洋葱里面最重要的成分之一，就是槲皮素（Quercetin），这是一种强效的抗氧化物，具有抗发炎、保护心血管的作用。</p><p>另外，它还含有：<br/><strong>? 硫化物：</strong>帮助解毒、抗菌、保护血管。<br/><strong>? 益生质（寡糖）：</strong>养肠道好菌。<br/><strong>? 维生素<strong>C<strong>、钾：</strong></strong></strong>提升免疫力、调节血压。</p><p>从功能医学角度来看，洋葱对“抗发炎、肠道健康、心血管保护”都有加分效果。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>切洋葱会流眼泪，是因为洋葱在切开时会释放“挥发性硫化物”，刺激眼睛产生泪水。有几个简单的改善方法：<br/><strong>? </strong>先冷藏再切<br/><strong>? </strong>用锋利的刀<br/><strong>? </strong>靠近水源切（开水龙头）<br/><strong>? </strong>戴眼镜或护目镜</p><h2><span style="color:#3e7100"><strong>洋葱生吃好还是煮熟好？</strong></span></h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>答案是：各有优点，最好搭配吃。</p><p><strong>生洋葱：</strong>保留较多槲皮素与硫化物，所以抗氧化效果较强。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p><strong>熟洋葱：</strong>刺激性降低，较温和、好消化、甜味增加、容易入口。</p><p>肠胃较敏感的人，可以多用熟洋葱。想强化抗氧化，可以适量吃生洋葱。</p><h2><span style="color:#3e7100"><strong>不同颜色的洋葱有差吗？</strong></span></h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p><strong>紫洋葱（红洋葱）：</strong>含花青素，抗氧化力较强，味道较辛辣。适合生吃。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p><strong>白洋葱：</strong>味道较甜、温和，适合炒菜、炖煮。</p><p>所以，想保健就多吃紫洋葱；想好吃就吃白洋葱。</p><div style="margin-left:0;margin-right:0;max-width:1200px"><figure
