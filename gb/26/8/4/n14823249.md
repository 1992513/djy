<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>衣服手洗比洗衣机洗好？专家这样说</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823969-P.143-2.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr><strong>编者按：</strong>衣服天天都要洗，洗剂和洗衣机大家都在用，但你真的洗对了吗？<span style="font-weight:400">专家解答洗衣机问题，一次弄懂！</span></p><p><strong>Q1.是不是手洗就一定比洗衣机机洗好呢？</strong></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823974-P.141-2.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823974-P.141-2.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823974-P.141-2.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823974-P.141-2.jpg" alt="（苹果屋提供）" width="300" b="374"/></picture></a><figcaption>（苹果屋提供）</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>其实草率的手洗也会有让脏污残留衣物的问题，机洗反而能全面、大量地清洗污垢。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>强调手洗是因为有些衣物材质较轻柔，为了不伤衣料无法机洗。但现在洗衣机已增进许多功能，分为：波轮型、搅拌型、滚筒式洗净等，多能达到一般的需求，容量更弹性。</p><p>此外，洗剂也进展到酵素洗衣粉、强效洗衣精、特定污渍或各材质专用的洗剂等，甚至还有“有机洗涤剂”。</p><p>简而言之，搭配正确的预处理去污方法后再简单机洗，是最环保又洁净的洗衣方法。只要注意洗衣机的使用方法，不要太常塞入过多衣服，正确使用洗衣网、洗衣球等应用道具，选择适合的洗衣剂种类，就可以摆脱一味要求手洗的迷思。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p><strong>Q2.新闻说洗衣机比马桶脏，贴身衣物丢洗衣机好吗？</strong></p><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823975-P.142-1.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823975-P.142-1.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823975-P.142-1.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823975-P.142-1.jpg" alt="（苹果屋提供）" width="300" b="251"/></picture></a><figcaption>（苹果屋提供）</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>洗衣机一个月到一个半月定期保养就没问题，重点是如何保护！</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>贴身衣物，像是内衣或弹性纱、运动型衣服都建议包网袋清洗。贴身内衣可放入球状塑胶网，钢圈比较不容易变形。</p><p>注意不要用热水洗涤，热水不会洗得比较干净，反而会让衣物弹性疲乏，导致衣物损耗率高。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p><strong>Q3.机洗前，要怎样判断一次最多能洗多少衣服？</strong></p><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>先估算待洗衣物的重量，再放入单次机洗容量8成衣物量，效果最好！</p><p>若是市售洗衣机的机洗量，可先看洗衣容量公斤数的标示（如7、10、13公斤）。然后计算待洗衣物的重量（需留意衣物的干湿状态），评估是否为最适合的衣物量。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>而为了预留转洗空间，一次建议放入8成衣物就好，如：原可洗7公斤，1件100克的T恤（干燥状态）可洗70件，但为预留转洗空间，建议只放8成的衣物量（56件）。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div
