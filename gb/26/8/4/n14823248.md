<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>衣服天天都要洗 但你真的用对洗剂了吗？</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2023/05/id14006166-shutterstock_793737499.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr><strong>编者按：</strong>衣服天天都要洗，洗剂大家都在用，但你真的用对洗剂了吗？<span style="font-weight:400">专家解答，最多人问的洗剂问题一次弄懂。</span></p><p><strong>Q1.洗衣精和洗衣粉哪个洗得比较干净？</strong></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823980-P.138-3.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823980-P.138-3.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823980-P.138-3.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823980-P.138-3.jpg" alt="（苹果屋提供）" width="300" b="453"/></picture></a><figcaption>（苹果屋提供）</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>常洗、耐洗衣物用洗衣粉，常接触肌肤的衣物用洗衣精！</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>一般洗衣粉碱度高（pH 11）且有不溶水的矿物质增加摩擦系数，所以洁净力会比较强，但相对对环境的破坏也较强。</p><p>洗衣精较温和、偏中性（pH 8-9，视厂牌而定），常接触肌肤的衣物建议用洗衣精，以免肌肤对碱过敏。</p><p><strong>Q2.洗衣服要先放衣服还是放洗剂？</strong></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823979-P.139-1.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823979-P.139-1.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823979-P.139-1.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823979-P.139-1.jpg" alt="（苹果屋提供）" width="300" b="282"/></picture></a><figcaption>（苹果屋提供）</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>洗衣精顺序没关系，但洗衣粉一定要先放！</p><p>一般来说要先放洗剂，以免溶解不均。洗衣精是液态，比较不用担心，但洗衣粉中有矿物质，如果没有先溶解均匀就会在衣服上结块。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>而且洗衣粉请不要放在洗衣机的投料盒，因为投料盒水柱不够强，无法将洗衣粉完全冲开。</p><p><strong>Q3.机洗时可同时放入洗衣粉和衣物柔软精吗？</strong></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823978-P.139-2.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823978-P.139-2.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823978-P.139-2.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823978-P.139-2.jpg" alt="（苹果屋提供）" width="300" b="537"/></picture></a><figcaption>（苹果屋提供）</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>虽然柔软精可以让衣服触感变好、穿起来更合身并增加香气，但不可和洗衣粉同时倒入，因为会削弱洗衣粉功效。</p><p>正确方法应先放洗衣粉（剂），洗洁约剩20～18分钟、进行最后一次洗清时，在水满状态倒入衣物柔软精。</p><p>柔软精用量可见瓶上说明，或以10～12公斤洗衣机为例，衣服量为1/3满时倒入1/2瓶盖的量；衣服量为1/2满时倒入1瓶盖量；衣服接近全满时则倒入5又1/2瓶盖的量。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p><strong>Q4.是不是功能越多样化的洗衣剂就越厉害呢？</strong></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>有些洗衣剂标榜能呵护皮肤、香味持久，或是可以洁净杀菌同时柔软衣物！这些功效其实都只是行销噱头。洗衣剂功能越多，清洁效果就越弱。最好的方法是选择效能单一的洗衣剂，如果需要柔软、亮白、让颜色亮丽等效果，可另做选购。</p><p>一般衣物清洁需用到的包含水晶洗衣皂或弱碱性洗衣粉；清洗或手洗贴身衣物和材质轻柔的衣物则需要中性洗剂（例如冷洗精）。</p><p>此外，正确手洗步骤是先将洗剂溶于水中，再放入衣物，等衣物浸泡10～25分钟后，再开始手洗，最后要用清水彻底地冲洗干净。</p><p><strong>Q5.不用柔软精，可以让衣物变柔软吗？</strong></p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:183px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823977-P.140-1.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823977-P.140-1.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823977-P.140-1.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823977-P.140-1.jpg" alt="（苹果屋提供）" width="183" b="337"/></picture></a><figcaption>（苹果屋提供）</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>如果害怕柔软精里的化学物质或香味，就试试纯天然的白醋吧！</p><p>柔软精会在衣服上形成阳离子薄膜，让衣服变软并保护纤维。如果不喜欢柔软精，可以用10～15c.c.的白醋代替柔软精。</p><p>虽然白醋只能让衣物恢复原本弹性、代替6成的柔软效果，不过它是纯天然弱酸洗剂，既亲肤，又能中和过多的碱。</p><p><strong>Q6.标榜增艳漂白功能的洗剂，对衣服会造成影响吗？</strong></p><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823971-P.140-2.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823971-P.140-2.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823971-P.140-2.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823971-P.140-2.jpg" alt="（苹果屋提供）" width="300" b="267"/></picture></a><figcaption>（苹果屋提供）</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>衣服染料百百种，使用增艳漂白剂之前请在衣角试试！</p><p>增艳漂白剂就是氧系漂白剂，分为过碳酸钠和过氧化氢，两者差不多。增艳是指让衣物变白，一般不会破坏衣物色料，但染料系统种类非常多，如果遇到会褪色的就糟了。所以使用前请先在衣角测试喔！</p><p><strong>Q7.担心衣物残存干洗剂，挂多久才能挥发？</strong></p><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823972-P.140-3.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823972-P.140-3.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823972-P.140-3.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823972-P.140-3.jpg" alt="（苹果屋提供）" width="300" b="252"/></picture></a><figcaption>（苹果屋提供）</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>干洗剂4～6小时就会挥发，别担心！</p><p>干洗剂是从石油提炼的一种溶剂。一般洗衣店干洗后都会将衣服挂12～24小时才进行整烫，所以问题不大。</p><p>有时纯棉衣物可能还会有油气的味道，如果害怕残存，可以把防尘袋打开，放在通风处4～6小时，味道就会消失。</p><p><strong>Q8.用太多洗剂对身体不好，有没有环保健康的洗净方法？</strong></p><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823973-P.141-1.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823973-P.141-1.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823973-P.141-1.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823973-P.141-1.jpg" alt="（苹果屋提供）" width="300" b="133"/></picture></a><figcaption>（苹果屋提供）</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>专家解答：</strong>用手工自制洗衣球，省荷包、去污强，衣物干干净净！</p><p>若不想用洗剂洗衣，却遇到有顽强脏污的衣物时，建议机洗时可丢一、两颗洗衣球到洗衣机内，借此增加洗衣时的摩擦，以增强去污力；或者也可用铝箔纸捏成鸡蛋大小球状，做成自制的洗衣球，再丢入洗衣机中，一样有去污的效果喔！</p><p><strong>（网站专文）</strong></p><p>（本文摘编自《<ahref="https://www.books.com.tw/products/0011004074"
