<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>弄脏衣物免紧张 记住5口诀轻松洗净</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2019/05/20190601_shouhiuhsu_shutterstock_1115756837_01.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr><strong>编者按：</strong>出门在外意外弄脏衣物，通常是乱擦一通，但是，这样做只会越弄越脏，回家后更难清洗，污渍会永远留在衣服上。专业洗衣达人传授现场紧急处理技巧，让你回家后就能轻松洗干净。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000">外出意外弄脏衣物 去污渍5大急救口诀</span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p><strong>一、饮料滴到衣物 急救口诀：吸</strong></p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823278-P.62-1.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="264"/></picture></a><figcaption>（苹果屋提供））</figcaption></figure></div><p>液体类的果汁、茶酒若不小心滴到衣物上，可用厨房纸巾或抹布，吸拭多余的残汁，如果能脱下衣物，就能在污渍背面垫块布或纸巾，不让污染范围扩大。所有刚沾到污渍的衣物，都适用此方法处理。</p><p><strong>二、深色酱汁喷到衣物 急救口诀：冲</strong></p><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823277-P.62-2.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p><strong>三、油脂溅到衣物 急救口诀：拍</strong></p><div><figure style="max-width:300px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823276-P.62-3.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14823275-P.63-1.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source
