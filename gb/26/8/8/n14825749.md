<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>阿妹妹成军30年再合体 音乐节点名最想看杜德伟</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14825758-20260807-taitung-02.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
