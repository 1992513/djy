<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>西澳Kwinana高速公路拓宽工程明年初动工</h2>
<h6></h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年08月06日讯】<span lang="zh-TW">（大纪元记者张小凡澳洲珀斯编译报导）西澳</span><span lang="zh-TW">州政府日前宣布，一项价值</span><span lang="en-AU">7</span><span lang="zh-TW">亿澳元的珀斯南部</span><span lang="en-AU">Kwinana</span><span lang="zh-TW">高速公路拓宽工程明年年初动工。此举是州长库克（</span><span lang="en-AU">Roger Cook</span><span lang="zh-TW">）的一项关键竞选承诺。</span></p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">据“</span><span lang="en-US">WA Today</span><span lang="zh-TW">”网站报导，交通厅长萨菲欧蒂（</span><span lang="en-US">Rita Saffioti</span><span lang="zh-TW">）表示，将在</span><span lang="en-US">Kwinana</span><span lang="zh-TW">高速公路的关键路段增设新车道，其中包括</span><span lang="en-US">Russell Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">至</span><span lang="en-US">Mortimer Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">之间长达</span><span lang="en-US">11</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里的路段。</span></p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">她表示：“升级改造将包括：在</span><span lang="en-US">Russell Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">与</span><span lang="en-US">Mortimer Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">之间双向各增设一条新车道；在</span><span lang="en-US">Roe Highway</span><span lang="zh-TW">与</span><span lang="en-US">Berrigan Drive</span><span lang="zh-TW">之间</span><span lang="en-US">11</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里的路段增设一条南行车道；以及在</span><span lang="en-US">Russell Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">至</span><span lang="en-US">Beeliar Drive</span><span lang="zh-TW">之间增设一条北行车道。</span></p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">“项目还包括增设协调式匝道信号灯，覆盖从</span><span lang="en-US">Safety Bay Road</span><span lang="zh-TW">到</span><span lang="en-US">Roe Highway</span><span lang="zh-TW">的路段。这意味着整条高速公路上将拥有</span><span lang="en-US">42</span><span lang="zh-TW">公里的匝道信号控制系统。</span></p>
<p>“我们还将升级高速公路中央分隔带及路肩的防撞护栏。”</p>
<p>萨菲欧蒂还说，工程还将包括安装新的防护栏，以防止车辆冲入火车轨道。</p>
<p>该项目将覆盖全长<span lang="en-US">29</span>公里的高速公路路段，预计于明年第一季度动工，工期约为两年。</p>
<p>西澳工党于<span lang="en-US">2025</span>年<span lang="en-US">1</span>月作出此项竞选承诺。州长库克已经确认，该项目将由联邦政府与西澳州政府共同出资，即双方各出资<span lang="en-US">3.5</span>亿澳元。</p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">上一</span><span lang="zh-TW">次</span><span lang="en-US">Kwinana</span><span lang="zh-TW">高速公路拓宽工程已于</span><span lang="en-US">2010</span><span lang="zh-TW">年代完工。</span></p>
