<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>道歉有学问 别光说I’m sorry 这4句最有效</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837921-shutterstock_2658451919.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>在海外生活的人可能经常遇到这样的情况：和同事或朋友，甚至是邻里或夫妻小俩口，头一天吵架闹了别扭，第二天后悔想道歉，那要怎样说出口最有效、最合适呢？今天就手把手教你怎么开口。</p><p>其实，道歉这件事在英语职场有一套相当固定的逻辑：不从“谁对谁错”谈起，而是先把“我做的哪一个动作不妥”讲具体，对错留给事情，道歉只针对行为。掌握这个顺序，剩下的就是句型问题。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:640px" sorry，在英语职场还有一套逻辑可循。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>第一步：先给自己一分钟</strong></span></h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>开口前，先在心里回答一个问题：昨天那场争执，我哪一个具体动作做过头了？打断他说话？当着全组的面反驳？语气太冲？email里把主管一起加进副本？</p><p>找不到具体动作，就先别去道歉。含糊的I’m sorry“昨天不好意思啊”在英语职场效果很差，对方会觉得你只是想把事情糊过去。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>第二步：找对时机和场合</strong></span></h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>私下、单独、当面，这三个条件尽量凑齐。开场句很简单：</p><p>? Do you have a minute?（你有空聊一下吗？）</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>? Can we talk for a sec?（我们能谈一下吗？）</p><p>? Can I grab you for five minutes?（借用你五分钟？）</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>第三步：</strong><strong>4</strong><strong>句完整的道歉话</strong></span></h2><p>一次有效的道歉，大致包含四块：承认、具体、承担、收尾。</p><p><strong>1. 承认</strong></p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>? I owe you an apology.（我该跟你道个歉。）</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>? I&#x27;ve been thinking about what happened yesterday.（昨天的事我想了一晚上。）</p><p><strong>2. 具体</strong></p><p>? I shouldn&#x27;t have interrupted you in the meeting.（我不该在会议上打断你。）</p><p>? I shouldn&#x27;t have raised my voice. That was out of line.（我不该大声，那样不妥当。）</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><p>out of line在词典里的意思是“超出可接受的行为范围”。这句话分量刚好，承认自己越线了，但没有把自己整个人贬下去。</p><p><strong>3. 承担</strong></p><p>? That was on me.（那是我的问题。）</p><p>? I was stressed about the deadline, but that&#x27;s no excuse for how I spoke to you.（当时截止日期给我压力好大，但那不能拿来当我对你那样说话的借口。）</p><p>注意这个句式：解释放在后面，而且立刻自己堵住退路（that&#x27;s no excuse）。若顺序颠倒，变成先讲压力再讲抱歉，听起来就成了辩解。</p><p><strong>4. 收尾</strong></p><p>? I&#x27;d like to hear your side of it.（我想听听你那边的看法。）</p><p>? I&#x27;ll be more careful about how I bring things up next time.（下次我会注意提出意见的方式。）</p><p>? Are we good?（我们没事了吧？）</p><p>最后这句Are we good?是英语职场的万用结尾，轻松、不沉重，把台阶递给对方。对方回一句We&#x27;re good，这一页就算翻过去了。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>第四步：对方先开口道歉，你怎么接？</strong></span></h2><p>? I appreciate you saying that.（谢谢你这么说。）</p><p>? Thank you. And honestly, I could have handled my end better too.（谢谢。老实说，我那边处理得也不够好。）</p><p>? We&#x27;re good. No hard feelings.（没事了，不往心里去。）</p><p>? no hard feelings的意思就是“不记恨、不放在心上”，用来收尾很体面。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>别踩这几个地雷</strong></span></h2><p><strong>? </strong><strong>I&#x27;m sorry if you felt that way.</strong>（如果你有那种感觉，那我很抱歉。）</p><p>加了一个if，等于在说“我不确定我有没有做错，错的可能是你太敏感”。这是很常见的假道歉，杀伤力比不道歉还大。</p><p><strong>? </strong><strong>I&#x27;m sorry, but you started it.</strong></p><p>一个but，前面那半句就作废了。要解释，就等对方接受道歉之后再说。</p><p><strong>? </strong><strong>Let&#x27;s just forget about it.</strong>（就当没发生过吧。）</p><p>跳过太快，等于不准对方把话说完。</p><p><strong>? </strong><strong>I apologize if anyone was offended.</strong>（若有人感到被冒犯，我深表歉意。）</p><p>这是企业公关声明的句子，不是同事之间讲的话。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>如果你想了半天 还是认为自己没错</strong></span></h2><p>也有这种时候。这时不必硬挤出一个道歉，但可以把关系先接回来：</p><p>? I still see it differently, but I don&#x27;t want this to affect how we work together.<br/>（我的看法还是不一样，但我不希望这件事影响我们的合作。）</p><p>? Can we agree to disagree on this one and move on?<br/>（这件事我们保留各自意见，先往下走，好吗？）</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>一封</strong><strong>4</strong><strong>句话的道歉信</strong></span></h2><p>如果对方在别的城市，或当面实在开不了口，那就写封简短的email：</p><p>Hi Chris,</p><p>I owe you an apology for how I reacted in yesterday&#x27;s call. Cutting you off in front of the team was out of line, and that&#x27;s on me. I was under pressure about the launch date, but that&#x27;s no excuse. I&#x27;d like to hear your thoughts on the timeline when you have time this week.</p><p>Thanks,</p><p>Alex</p><p>道歉、讲清楚哪件事、承担、把话题接回工作。共四句，不必更长。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>最后提醒一下</strong></span></h2><p>英语职场有个和华人习惯相反的地方：不宜过度道歉。事事都sorry、每封信开头都是I&#x27;m so sorry to bother you，久了会被解读成没有把握、不敢承担。真该道歉时说得具体而完整，不该道歉时就不说，这才是英语里的分寸。</p><p>记住，道歉针对的是行为，不是人格。把“我不该打断你”说清楚，跟承认“我这个人不行”相差十万八千里。@*</p><p><strong><em>作者简介：</em></strong><em>老哈利在中国大陆有超过</em><em>15</em><em>年的一线教学经验，曾在新东方英语等英语培训机构教授雅思、托福等课程，并创办自己的英语培训学校，所开设之“中老年实用英语班”广受学员欢迎。移民美国后特在此开设“新移民实用美语”栏目，希望您在此学到英语教科书上不教的、免费英语班上外教不讲的一些最实用的零基础英语，助您尽快融入北美社区生活。</em></p><p>责任编辑：沈少棋
