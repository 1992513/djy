<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>Costco番茄有好多种 挑选和保存要诀一次看懂</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837975-shutterstock_2690161985.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>番茄是日常料理很常见的食材，无论是生吃、熬汤或做成各种酱汁，都能为料理增添不少浓郁风味。番茄的品种也五花八门，无论去传统市场、超市还是Cosco，看到各种各样的番茄总让人眼花撩乱，到底该如何挑选呢？本文教大家一次学会挑选和保存要诀。</p><h2>Costco货架上的番茄</h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>Costco生鲜区的番茄走“家庭号大包装温室稳定品质”路线，常态品项包括：</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>带藤番茄（Tomatoes On The Vine）——4磅装，连藤贩售是常驻款。</p><p>樱桃番茄／带藤樱桃番茄（Cherry Tomatoes / Cherry on the Vine）——1.5磅装，多为温室栽培（常见供应商Windset Farms），保存期较长、熟成度均匀。</p><p>葡萄番茄（Grape Tomatoes）——2磅装，常见供应商是Village Farms。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>三色樱桃番茄（Tri Color Cherry on the Vine）——红、黄、橘三色混装，1.5磅装。</p><p>综合番茄拼盘（Gourmet Tomato Medley）——混装葡萄番茄、迷你李形番茄和几种特色樱桃番茄。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>季节限定款：部分分店不定期上架圣马尔扎诺番茄（San Marzano Tomatoes，意大利经典酱汁用番茄），品项因分店和季节浮动，遇到算幸运。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837963-shutterstock_2536881629_2.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837963-shutterstock_2536881629_2.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>农夫正在用园艺剪刀收割一栈成熟的红圣马尔扎诺番茄。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><p>不过，Costco基本不卖传家宝番茄这类讲究风味层次的精品款。如果想吃这类番茄，得去一般超市或传统市场。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><h2>一般超市的番茄分类</h2><p>除了Costco主打的大包装品项，Whole Foods、Trader Joe&#x27;s、Safeway、Kroger这类超市通常会依口感、甜度、用途和大小，把番茄细分成以下几类：</p><h3>1. 樱桃番茄与葡萄番茄（Cherry &amp; Grape Tomatoes）</h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p><strong>樱桃番茄（Cherry Tomatoes）</strong>：圆形、皮薄多汁，酸甜均衡，适合直接生吃。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837964-shutterstock_2609502395.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837964-shutterstock_2609502395.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>传统市场贩售的樱桃番茄。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>圣女小番茄（又称葡萄番茄，Grape Tomatoes）</strong>：椭圆形，皮稍厚、口感更脆、甜度通常更高,耐放不易烂,适合带便当。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837965-shutterstock_2370217933.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>椭圆形、皮稍厚的圣女小番茄。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><p><strong>混装彩色小番茄（Medley）</strong>：常见红、黄、绿、橘、紫黑数色一盒。黄色偏甜、绿色偏酸，可用来挑口味。</p><p>挑选要诀：表皮饱满有光泽、蒂头鲜绿不干枯,捏起来有弹性、不软烂。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837967-shutterstock_474542032.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>混装彩色小番茄（Medley）。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><h3>2. 罗马番茄（Roma / Plum Tomatoes）</h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><p>长椭圆形，水分少、果肉厚、籽少，是性价比最高的中型番茄，也是煮红酱、熬汤的首选。因为肉厚水少，熬煮后不会太稀。生吃口感相对干实，不如其它品种多汁。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837973-shutterstock_2502938265.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>适合熬汤的罗马番茄。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><h3>3. 牛番茄（Beefsteak Tomatoes）</h3><p>体型最大，扁圆形，表面有明显凹凸褶皱。果肉厚实多汁，切片后大小刚好夹进汉堡或三明治。</p><p>挑选要诀：选蒂头附近没有裂痕、拿起来手感沉的,代表水分足。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837978-shutterstock_2682616083.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>男子在木板上切牛蕃茄。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><h3>4. 带藤番茄（On-the-Vine / Cluster Tomatoes）</h3><p>连着绿色藤蔓一起卖，因为采收后仍连着藤蔓一段时间，风味比单独散卖的大番茄更浓郁。</p><p>挑选要诀：藤蔓越绿、番茄越接近藤蔓的位置越新鲜。可以直接闻一下蒂头附近，香气浓代表熟成度够。</p><h3>5. 传家宝番茄（Heirloom Tomatoes）</h3><p>非基因改造的古老品种；外观不规则、颜色多样（绿条纹、黄橙、深紫、铁锈红都有）。卖相不佳但风味浓郁，是行家的最爱，价格通常也最高。皮薄不耐久放，买回家建议2到3天内吃完。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837976-shutterstock_2690518009.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>传统市场上的传家宝蕃茄，有红色、绿色、黄色和橙色。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><h3>6.?金巴利番茄（又称坎帕里番茄，Campari Tomatoes）</h3><p>坎帕里番茄介于樱桃番茄和中型番茄之间，通常带藤贩售，甜度数一数二高，皮薄肉多，适合当开胃菜直接生吃，不太适合久煮。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14837974-shutterstock_2709341053.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>适合当开胃菜生吃的金巴利番茄。(Shutterstock)</figcaption></figure></div><h3>7. 烘烤用小番茄（Roasting Tomatoes）</h3><p>部分超市会特别标示适合烤箱烘烤的小番茄——淋橄榄油、洒海盐和蒜末，低温慢烤后糖分浓缩，甜度会倍增，拌意大利面或配面包都合适。一般樱桃或圣女小番茄品种都能拿来这样处理，不一定要买特别标示的款。</p><h2>番茄保存原则：室温优先 不要放冰箱</h2><p>多数新鲜番茄应该放在室温通风处，蒂头朝下摆放能延缓水分流失，而不是直接放进冰箱。低温会破坏番茄的细胞壁结构，让口感变得粉粉的，同时抑制芳香物质的生成酶，吃起来明显少了番茄该有的香气和甜味。唯一例外是已经完全熟透、担心很快坏掉的番茄，可以短期冷藏延缓腐坏，但吃之前建议先放回室温回温30分钟左右，能找回部分风味。切开后吃不完的番茄，才需要用保鲜盒冷藏，并尽量在1到2天内用完。</p><h2>买回家怎么挑最省事</h2><p>如果不确定要买哪一种，记住一个简单原则：要生吃、拌沙拉的选圣女小番茄或金巴利；要煮酱、熬汤,选罗马番茄；要做三明治、汉堡,选牛番茄或带藤番茄；追求风味、不在乎卖相且愿意多花点钱的可以选传家宝番茄；纯粹图方便、想一次买够一周份量的，Costco的大包装款最省事。@*</p><p>责任编辑：茉莉
