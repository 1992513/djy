<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>嘉义市“肌力全开运动会” 勇壮族闯六关卡</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838592-870378-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>肌不可挡—稳定走路赛。（李撷璎／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年08月28日讯】（大纪元记者李撷璎台湾嘉义报导）嘉义市政府卫生局今（28）日上午在东区体育馆推出“嘉义市115年肌力全开运动会”。本届赛事分为两日举行，首日由65岁以上“勇壮组”率先登场，300位年过花甲的选手组成30支劲旅，直面六大多元体能关卡。嘉义市卫生局长廖育玮也代表黄敏惠市长到场，与舞者老师带领全场齐跳热身操，为活动揭开序幕。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14838593" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14838593" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838593-870379.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14838593" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838593-870379-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14838593" class="wp-caption-text">肌风骑士—手摇竞速。（李撷璎／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>廖育玮局长指出，首日赛事特别规划六大兼具趣味与挑战性的关卡，包括“肌力全开－科技握力”、“肌中要害－神射手”、“肌不可失－眼明手快”、“肌触即发－电流急急棒”、“肌风骑士－手摇竞速”，以及“肌不可挡－稳定走路赛”。期望透过丰富的关卡，鼓励长者将运动融入退休生活，保持身心灵健康，自信迈开步伐享受人生。这不仅契合黄敏惠市长推动的健康理念，更是落实“脑力、体力、社会力”三者兼备的终身健康目标。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14838594" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14838594" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838594-870380.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14838594" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838594-870380-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14838594" class="wp-caption-text">肌触即发—电流急急棒。（李撷璎／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>廖育玮局长又补充道，今天“勇壮组”的参加者平均年龄71.4岁，最年轻的65岁，最年长的90岁，代表嘉义的长辈爱运动，也热衷参加相关赛事。嘉义市是一个全龄共享、世代宜居、幸福爱运动的城市，因此市府将持续营造友善、安全、多元的运动环境，让长辈从参与中看见自己的能力，并在团队合作及互相鼓励中享受运动乐趣。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14838595" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14838595" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838595-870381.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14838595" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838595-870381-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14838595" class="wp-caption-text">“肌不可失-眼明手快”。（嘉义市政府提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>本次团体竞赛结果由运动中心笑咪咪队夺下冠军，此外，为鼓励市民揪团参与、共同挑战健康，前10名均设有奖励，总奖金达5万5,000元，让参赛者不仅在竞赛中展现团队默契，也从运动中收获健康与快乐。卫生局表示，“肌力全开运动会”明（29）日将由18至64岁“活力组”接棒，透过团队竞赛鼓励市民培养规律运动习惯，使运动真正融入日常。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14838596" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14838596" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838596-870382.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14838596" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838596-870382-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14838596" class="wp-caption-text">“肌中要害-神射手”。（嘉义市政府提供）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14838597" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14838597" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838597-870383.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14838597" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14838597-870383-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14838597" class="wp-caption-text">大合照。（李撷璎／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
