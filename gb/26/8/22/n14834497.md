<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>千盆台湾兰花 王后区植物园绽放</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834498-193827-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>台湾兰花世界展8月21-23日在纽约王后区植物园举行，展出超过千盆兰花。（林丹／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年08月22日讯】（大纪元记者林丹纽约报导）第13届“台湾兰花世界展”8月21-23日在法拉盛的王后区植物园（QBG）举办，展出超过1000盆多姿多彩的台湾兰花。8月21日举行了开幕仪式，王后区多位民意代表出席祝贺。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14834499" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14834499" style="width: 529px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834499-193828.jpg"><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14834499" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834499-193828-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="529" b="397" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14834499" class="wp-caption-text">民意代表、嘉宾祝贺台湾兰花展在纽约王后区植物园开幕。（林丹／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14834500" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14834500" style="width: 525px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834500-193829.jpg"><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14834500" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834500-193829-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="525" b="394" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14834500" class="wp-caption-text">民意代表祝贺台湾兰花展在王后区植物园开幕。（林丹／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>在风景如画的王后区植物园内，超过1000盆风姿绰约的兰花迎风摇曳，为植物园增添了一抹温馨和优雅。主办方王后区植物园、台湾花园基金会和今年首次参与合作的协办方——台湾兰花产销发展协会（以下简称台湾兰协），为兰花展精心布置了10个艺术场景，一瓶最大的兰花插花上，花瓣是美国国旗和中华民国国旗的图案元素。</p>
<p>植物园执行总监汉佐普洛斯（Evie Hantzopoulos）欢迎大家的到来。纽约州众议员李罗莎（Nily Rozic）、魏普林（David Weprin），市议员黄敏仪等祝贺台湾兰花展的开幕，盛赞兰花是台湾的骄傲。他们也表达自己对台湾的支持，并欢迎大家利用22日、23日双休日来美丽的植物园观赏台湾兰花和游玩。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14834501" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14834501" style="width: 544px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834501-193830.jpg"><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14834501" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834501-193830-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="544" b="408" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14834501" class="wp-caption-text">千姿百态的台湾兰花。（林丹／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14834502" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14834502" style="width: 537px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834502-193831.jpg"><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14834502" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834502-193831-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="537" b="403" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14834502" class="wp-caption-text">千姿百态的台湾兰花。（林丹／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>王后区植物园大使黄百龄致词表示，台湾兰花展始于2014年，现已举办至第13届，今年适逢美国建国250周年，“这是以绝佳的庆祝理由，以兰花、艺术、文化和友谊共庆这一时刻。”</p>
<p>今年台湾兰协首次协办，他们带来了超过300株兰花，涵盖约20个不同品种，使今年的展览成为迄今最令人瞩目的一届。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14834503" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14834503" style="width: 533px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834503-193832.jpg"><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14834503" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834503-193832-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="533" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14834503" class="wp-caption-text">千姿百态的台湾兰花。（林丹／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14834504" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14834504" style="width: 514px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834504-193833.jpg"><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14834504" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834504-193833-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="514" b="386" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14834504" class="wp-caption-text">千姿百态的台湾兰花。（林丹／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>台湾花园基金会创办人吴达义感谢侨务委员会（OCAC）成为今年活动的赞助单位之一。他说，根据侨委会资料，蝴蝶兰是台湾最具代表性的农产品出口之一，2025年出口额约达1.8亿至2亿美元，行销全球60多个国家。美国是台湾最大的单一出口市场，约占台湾蝴蝶兰出口的40%，每年出口额约5,500万至6,000万美元。台湾种植业者在美国蝴蝶兰市场的占有率也接近46%。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14834505" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14834505" style="width: 493px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834505-193834.jpg"><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14834505" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834505-193834-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="493" b="370" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14834505" class="wp-caption-text">兰花的花瓣上有美国国旗和中华民国国旗的图案。（林丹／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14834506" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14834506" style="width: 510px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834506-193838.jpg"><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14834506" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834506-193838-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="510" b="383" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14834506" class="wp-caption-text">千姿百态的台湾兰花。（林丹／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>台湾兰协会长黄文荣表示，台湾素有兰花王国的美誉，拥有优良的育种技术，专业的栽培管理、完整的产业链供应以及众多育种专家与栽培业者。展场中的每一株兰花，皆凝聚台湾兰花产业多年积累的技术、创新和专业精神，“今年适逢美国独立宣言250周年，带着台湾兰花来纽约，也是向美国朋友表达台湾人民最诚挚的祝福。”</p>
<figure id="attachment_14834507" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14834507" style="width: 510px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834507-193839.jpg"><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14834507" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834507-193839-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="510" b="383" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14834507" class="wp-caption-text">千姿百态的台湾兰花。（林丹／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14834508" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14834508" style="width: 529px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834508-193840.jpg"><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-14834508" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14834508-193840-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="529" b="397" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14834508" class="wp-caption-text">千姿百态的台湾兰花。（林丹/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>台湾兰花展活动内容：8月22日（周六）有插花、果冻花艺术、水彩工作坊、扯铃示范、手工艺、兰花养护课程；8月23日（周日）有捏面人艺术、茶道/茶艺、兰花养护课程、兰花特卖（下午5点开始）。地点：王后区植物园（43-50 Main St., Flushing, NY 11355）。</p>
