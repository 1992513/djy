每周4部电影：会员每周最多可观赏4部电影，且可免费升级IMAX、Dolby Cinema、RealD 3D等高规格影厅，无需额外支付价差。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>?
