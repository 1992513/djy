<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>避开酷暑 美国五大绝佳夏日旅行胜地</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14831520-GettyImages-681549377.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
