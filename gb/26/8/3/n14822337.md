<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>白石镇海洋节吸引万人 共享75周年钻禧庆典</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822350-1-600x400.jpeg" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年08月03日讯】<span style="font-weight: 400;">（大纪元记者</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">杨清清</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">加拿大温哥华采访报导）</span>8月1日，白石镇迎来一年一度的海洋节（White Rock Sea Festival）炬光游行。今年适逢第75届钻禧庆典，庆典活动自7月31日至8月2日连续三天展开，丰富多元的表演、家庭活动及社区传统，吸引数以万计居民与游客涌入白石海滩及码头，共同感受夏日嘉年华的热闹气氛。</p>
<p>8月1日的活动从中午开始，白石码头周边设置多个活动区，包括现场音乐会、美食餐车、市集、亲子互动游戏。海滨长廊人潮络绎不绝，展现白石镇夏季最具代表性的节庆魅力。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14822340" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822340" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822340-2.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822340" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822340-2-600x399.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="399" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822340" class="wp-caption-text">游行前的趣味游戏服务生竞赛”吸引了很多年轻人参加。（大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822341" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822341" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822341-3.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822341" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822341-3-600x399.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="399" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822341" class="wp-caption-text">游行前的趣味游戏服务生竞赛”吸引了很多年轻人参加。（大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822342" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822342" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822342-11.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822342" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822342-11-600x450.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822342" class="wp-caption-text">2026年8月1日，白石镇迎来一年一度的海洋节（White Rock Sea Festival）。（大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>傍晚时分登场的传统“服务生竞赛”（Waiter&#8217;s Race）再次成为活动焦点，参赛者手持托盘比拼速度与平衡技巧，引来现场观众热烈欢呼。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14822343" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822343" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822343-4.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822343" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822343-4-600x399.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="399" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822343" class="wp-caption-text">2026年8月1日，白石镇迎来一年一度的海洋节炬光游行。（大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822344" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822344" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822344-6.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822344" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822344-6-600x399.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="399" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822344" class="wp-caption-text">2026年8月1日，白石镇迎来一年一度的海洋节炬光游行。（大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822345" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822345" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822345-5.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822345" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822345-5-600x399.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="399" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822345" class="wp-caption-text">2026年8月1日，白石镇迎来一年一度的海洋节炬光游行。（大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>晚间举行的火炬游行（Torchlight Parade）更是将节庆气氛推向高潮，缤纷多彩的各式花车在夕阳映照下缓缓前进，闪烁的灯光点缀着海滨大道。沿途两侧挤满了各族裔的人群，游行队伍中，童话故事中的主角和卡通人物充满童趣，不时向街边观众挥手致意、抛送糖果与纪念品，欢呼声与掌声此起彼落。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14822346" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822346" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822346-7.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822346" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822346-7-600x399.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="399" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822346" class="wp-caption-text">天国乐团的演奏受到观众赞赏。（大纪元）</figcaption></figure>

<p>应邀参加游行的“法轮大法”团队是本届游行阵容最大的一只队伍，由天国乐团、炼功队和腰鼓队组成。</p>
<p>天国乐团是本届游行中唯一的军乐团，蓝白相间的唐朝乐官服饰尤为亮眼，当他们经过人群时，欢呼声不绝于耳，人们纷纷举起手机拍照录影，不少年轻人随着鼓点欢快起舞。◇</p>
