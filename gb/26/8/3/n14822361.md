<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>9句华人超实用英文口语——别再说Come here了！</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/06/id14532914-shutterstock_2469858277-e1750135733578.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>在美国说英文最尴尬的处境，不是讲错，而是讲得太对。文法零缺点、单字全用对，可对方脸上还是浮出那种“你是从教科书里走出来的”的表情。差别有时就在几个词的选择上。</p><p>以下9句，都是日常里出现频率极高的替换，每一句都可以拉近你和邻居或同事的距离，每一句我都附上使用时机和地雷，因为道地不等于处处能用。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><h2>一、3个换掉就对味的说法</h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><h3>1. I&#x27;m leaving. → I&#x27;m out.</h3><p>朋友聚会结束，起身说“I&#x27;m out”就够了，比“I&#x27;m leaving”轻快得多。要注意它还有另一个意思：谈合作、揪团时说“I&#x27;m out”，是“我不参加”。</p><h3>2. Come here. → Come over.</h3><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>Come here是叫身边的人靠过来，带指令口吻，对小孩、宠物说没问题，对大人容易显得没礼貌。Come over是“到我这边来”，多半指来我家、来我这一桌。Come over this weekend就是周末来我家坐坐。</p><h3>3. Sorry. → My bad.</h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>剑桥词典把my bad标为非正式用语，意思是承认某件事是自己的错。撞到人、拿错东西、记错时间，一句My bad轻松带过。但范围就到这里了：真正犯了错、对象是长辈、上司、客户，还是要用I&#x27;m sorry或I apologize。用my bad道歉大事，听起来像不当一回事。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/12/id14401263-10_shutterstock_2175850205-e1735534864866.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/12/id14401263-10_shutterstock_2175850205.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/12/id14401263-10_shutterstock_2175850205.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/12/id14401263-10_shutterstock_2175850205.jpg" alt="使用英文口语拉近你和邻居或同事的距离（Shutterstock）" width="640" b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>使用英文口语拉近你和邻居或同事的距离（Shutterstock）</figcaption></figure></div><h2>二、3个接话用的短句</h2><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><h3>4. It&#x27;s okay. → It&#x27;s all good.</h3><p>别人跟你道歉，回一句It&#x27;s all good，等于“没事没事”，比It&#x27;s okay松快、不带勉强。</p><h3>5. Sure. → That&#x27;s cool with me.</h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>表示“我没问题、我可以”。Sure偏中性、有点公务感，That&#x27;s cool with me有“我很好说话”的意思，同事之间好用。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><h3>6. Let&#x27;s do that. → Let&#x27;s roll with it.</h3><p>这一组要小心，两句并不完全等值。Let&#x27;s do that是中性的提议；roll with it带着“顺着这个方向走下去、不再纠结”的味道，通常出现在计划已经有点走偏、大家决定将就的时候。单独说Let&#x27;s roll，才是“我们走吧、开始吧”。</p><h2>三、3句学了马上能用</h2><h3>7. I&#x27;m all set.</h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><p>“我好了”“我不用了”都能讲。这句特别有用：餐厅服务生问还要不要别的，一句“I&#x27;m all set, thanks”，就是礼貌地说不用了；办手续时对方说“You&#x27;re all set”，意思是都办好了，你可以走了。</p><h3>8. Ready when you are.</h3><p>“我准备好了，就等你。”四个字，比“I&#x27;m ready, are you ready?”自然许多，出门、开会前都能用。</p><h3>9. Make the most of it.</h3><p>“好好把握、充分利用。”孩子出国念书、朋友难得放长假，这句话送出去很得体：“You only get one summer there, so make the most of it.”</p><h2><span style="color:#008000">养成一个习惯</span></h2><p>这些替换不是要你把课本英文丢掉。课本教的是通用版本，在任何场合都不会出错；口语版本亲切、省力，但每一句都绑着特定的场合与关系。真正的流利，是手里同时握着两套，开口前先看一眼对面坐的是谁。@*</p><p>责任编辑：茉莉
