<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>三团体合办星空电影院 分享台湾历史及客家文化</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822166-DSC05719-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>8月1日晚在牛顿市高地社区公园，三个台湾人团体合办第二届“星空电影院”，分享电影“那张照片里的我们”。（刘景烨/大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年08月03日讯】（大纪元记者刘景烨牛顿市报导）8月1日晚上在麻州牛顿市（Newton）高地社区公园，三个台湾人团体合办第二届“星空电影院”，播放以客家语对白为主的电影“那张照片里的我们”，回顾台湾在“二二八”之后的历史，同时分享客语文化。</p>
<p>当晚在侨教中心室外的牛顿高地社区公园，纽英伦客家乡亲会、波士顿台湾同乡会、波士顿台湾影展协会共同举办这场电影放映活动，吸引了不同族裔的观众总计约100人。大家在草坪席地而坐，欣赏这部2025年上映的台湾时代剧/爱情剧情片。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14822162" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822162" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822162-DSC05683.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822162" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822162-DSC05683-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822162" class="wp-caption-text">8月1日，三个台湾人团体合办第二届“星空电影院”，分享电影“那张照片里的我们”。观众在草坪上等待电影开始。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822163" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822163" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822163-DSC05684.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822163" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822163-DSC05684-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822163" class="wp-caption-text">观众在草坪上等待电影开始。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822157" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822157" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822157-DSC05647.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822157" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822157-DSC05647-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822157" class="wp-caption-text">客家乡亲会赠送客家特色麻糬。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>客家乡亲会会长黄文彦说，今年之所以播放“那张照片里的我们”，除了为分享客家语文化之外，也希望与观众一同了解，台湾年轻人在风云变幻的历史中，如何对待爱情，如何保有正向的心态。</p>
<p>黄文彦除了参与客家乡亲会之外，也是另外两个合办团体的成员。他谈到，“那张照片里的我们”没能入选今年的“波士顿台湾影展”，可谓是“遗珠之憾”。因此，客家乡亲选择借星空电影院活动，与大家分享这部电影。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14822159" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822159" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822159-DSC05661.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822159" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822159-DSC05661-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822159" class="wp-caption-text">参与活动的纽英伦客家乡亲会成员合影。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822161" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822161" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822161-DSC05677.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822161" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822161-DSC05677-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822161" class="wp-caption-text">三个主办团体，客家乡亲会、台湾同乡会、台湾影展协会成员合影。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822160" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822160" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822160-DSC05669.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822160" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822160-DSC05669-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822160" class="wp-caption-text">左起：客家乡亲会会长黄文彦、侨教中心主任康雅雄、台湾同乡会会长杨慧雅、台湾影展协会会长林致中和蔡景全。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>“那张照片里的我们”的英文片名是“The Photo from 1977”。片中的对白多是客家语，也有中文国语、台语、韩语和英语。电影以1977年“中坜事件”为背景，讲述一名女摄影师和一名韩国跆拳道教练的爱情故事。</p>
<p>驻波士顿台北经济文化办事处处长廖朝宏、侨教中心主任康雅雄、侨务委员郭竞儒也参与了活动。廖朝宏感谢三个团体举办这场放映会，也欢迎当地居民有机会去台湾游览。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14822164" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822164" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822164-DSC05689.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822164" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822164-DSC05689-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822164" class="wp-caption-text">嘉宾左起：侨务委员郭竞儒、经文处处长廖朝宏、侨教中心主任康雅雄。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822158" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822158" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822158-DSC05651.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822158" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822158-DSC05651-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822158" class="wp-caption-text">波士顿台湾影展协会介绍今年9月的系列放映活动。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822167" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822167" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822167-DSC05649.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822167" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822167-DSC05649-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822167" class="wp-caption-text">波士顿台湾同乡会介绍活动，并发放防蚊贴。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>除了电影之外，客家乡亲会还提供客家特色麻糬，台湾乡亲会发放防蚊贴，台湾影展协会摊位介绍今年9月的电影活动，安多福（Andover）贡茶饮品店也在现场出售珍珠奶茶等饮品。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14822165" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822165" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822165-DSC05710.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822165" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822165-DSC05710-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822165" class="wp-caption-text">新英格兰中文学校协会会长冯文鸾介绍“双十国庆文化访问团”演出。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14822156" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14822156" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822156-DSC05638.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14822156" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/08/id14822156-DSC05638-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14822156" class="wp-caption-text">安多福（Andover）贡茶饮品店出售茶饮。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>参与活动的团体，也分别介绍了自己的特色活动。台湾同乡会会长杨慧雅欢迎大家参与9月26日的中秋迎新烤肉联欢会；台湾影展协会会长林致中介绍了9月10日至21日，即将在麻州4家电影院举办的系列放映活动；新英格兰中文学校协会会长冯文鸾介绍了9月7日的“双十国庆文化访问团”演出，鼓励大家呼朋引伴，提前购票。◇</p>
