<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>【车评】智能节能 无忧畅行 2026 Hyundai Tucson PHEV Limited AWD</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892610-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_01-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>2026 Hyundai Tucson PHEV Limited AWD。（李奥／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年11月21日讯】韩国品牌的老大哥Hyundai，旗下有多种尺寸SUV／CUV，类别为小型SUV的Tucson已是第四代产品，而今次测试的2026年款Tucson PHEV Limited，可大大减少测油开支，又不用忧愁电池的续航里程。</p>
<p><a loading="lazy" title="YouTube video player" data2-src=""></a><a href="https://d27l6dsifacvoi.cloudfront.net/R5aBBk2iQPYq"><img width="600" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/1992513/djy/master/gb/300/djtsp.jpg" title="进入原文观看"  alt="进入原文观看"></a><h3 align=center>(视频.国内建议下载<a href="https://github.com/1992513/www/blob/master/README.md#8">翻墙软件</a>进入原文观看)</h3><a src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/L473KWhDdcA?si=bOW4vmaX4LIHx1g0" width="560" b="315" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></a></p>
<p>2026年款Tucson外型延续了去年的Facelift更新，事实上中期升级后的Tucson改变不大，只是防撞器位置上移一点，车灯有了少许的改变，前脸采用格子设计，在左右各有多个LED日间行车灯，在行车时就像一双翼展翅。真正头灯却在日间行车灯下方，即雾灯位置。车侧的线条挺多，不同角度的骨线，令车门的不同角度的板块。另外，银 黑旋风形19吋合金轮圈是Limited版的标配，并配以及235／55R19轮胎。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14892577" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892577" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892577-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_05.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892577" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892577-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_05-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892577" class="wp-caption-text">2026 Hyundai Tucson PHEV Limited AWD。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Tucson车身的侧镜是与车色同色，内装有指辉灯，镜下方有摄像机，配合车前后同四个摄像机，便可提供鸟瞰式影像，另外，侧镜的摄像机可将车侧盲点（Blind-Spot View Monitor）显示在仪表板上；测试Tucson车身是深灰色，与装饰用的黑色饰版，非常相衬。Limited车尾的排气口为隐藏式，与去年N Line的双排气喉完全不同。Tucson车尾灯的设计有如四只尖锐的牙齿，在Facelift后仍有保留，颇有特式。最大分别是Tucson PHEV在车左侧装有电池充电口，可支援Level 1及Level 2充电。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14892578" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892578" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892578-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_09.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892578" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892578-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_09-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892578" class="wp-caption-text">防撞器位置上移一点。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14892580" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892580" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892580-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_11.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892580" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892580-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_11-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892580" class="wp-caption-text">头灯组。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>在车厢内，Facelift后的设计更为精致，以白、黑与银色搭配。全屏虚拟仪表板是12.3英寸，可根据驾驶模式转变主题，并与12.3英寸中控台萤幕连成一体。多功能方向盘由旧有四幅式设计改为三幅式，在两点及十点位置方向盘内圈隆起，方便驾驶者握紧。前座均有三段加热及透气功能，作为Limited版，驾驶座与前座乘客椅都是8方向电动调节，驾驶席也有2个记忆位置。变速杆“搬”到方向盘后方，节省中控台空间之余，又与其他Hyundai车款一样。中控台多了的空间，上层装了Qi手机的无线充电板，下层则是一个大空间，可放手袋或大体积物件。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14892581" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892581" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892581-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_29.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892581" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892581-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_29-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892581" class="wp-caption-text">Facelift后的车厢设计更为精致。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Tucson Limited的中控台集信息娱乐及导航于一身，因萤幕上移与中控台水平，所以下方有更多空间。无线Apple CarPlay和Android Auto，会自动连接Android或Apple智能手机，所以驾驶者可以把手机放在口袋或背包里就可连接。Tucson也可使用手机的数码钥匙（Digital Key），可用作上锁和解锁汽车，启动警报及启动引擎，所以车主可将传统钥匙留在家中，又可将数码钥匙“借”给家人和朋友。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14892582" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892582" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892582-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_21.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892582" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892582-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_21-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892582" class="wp-caption-text">12.3英寸仪表板。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14892587" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892587" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892587-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_22.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892587" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892587-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_22-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892587" class="wp-caption-text">12.3英寸中控台萤幕。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Limited内部都采用白色皮革，包括座椅、门饰和扶手上也是，更为高贵。Limited版也配备了金属踏板，方向盘也有加热功能，在后方也装有Paddle Shift变速拨片，但在Hybrid车内就只是加减Regenerate Brake的强弱。另外，车厢内也在多处加入了气氛灯，并可随喜好改变灯光颜色。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14892588" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892588" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892588-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_33.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892588" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892588-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_33-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892588" class="wp-caption-text">白色皮革座椅。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14892589" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892589" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892589-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_27.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892589" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892589-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_27-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892589" class="wp-caption-text">变速杆。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Hyundai为Tucson提供了两种动力选择。全汽油引擎版车款只会标配一副2.5升直喷式直列四缸引擎，187匹马力和178磅呎扭矩，配8速SHIFTRONIC自动变速箱，最大牵引力为2,000磅。第二款引擎为1.6升涡轮增压引擎，马力只有178匹，峰值扭矩195磅呎，但这款引擎只配Hybrid或Plug-In Hybrid车款，因为会结合了电动马达，Hybrid的最后综合动力输出为231匹马力及271磅呎扭矩，而PHEV更强，最后综合动力输出为268匹马力及271磅呎扭矩，PHEV的电池容量为13.8kWh，续航距离为32英里。在早上出门时，不知是否天气冷的关系，就算选择了EV行车，引擎仍然会启动。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14892590" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892590" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892590-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_17.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892590" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892590-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_17-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892590" class="wp-caption-text">1.6升涡轮增压引擎的Hybrid系统。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>另外，汽油版只会标配前驱系统，但不同型号也升级到HTRAC四驱，而Hybrid及Plug-In Hybrid则标配了四驱。HTRAC四驱系统使用电子可变扭矩分体式离合器，在前后轴间进行主动扭矩控制。该系统的扭矩分配，可针直线加速、中速和高速转弯以及坡道起步不同情况进行调整。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14892593" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892593" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892593-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_26.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892593" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892593-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_26-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892593" class="wp-caption-text">驾驶模式。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Hyundai Tucson在各项表现也不错，尤其在Facelift后的设备及科技的运用更加出色，变更了位置的变速杆也很方便好用。1.6升涡轮引擎加马达的Hybrid系统，马力达231匹，在高速公路行车绝不吃力，在停顿加速也很快。PHEV版的Tucson可减少对汽油的消耗，每天减少一点对消费者也是有帮助的！◇</p>
<figure id="attachment_14892596" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14892596" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892596-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_02.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14892596" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14892596-2026_Hyundai_Tucson_PHEV_02-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14892596" class="wp-caption-text">2026 Hyundai Tucson PHEV Limited AWD。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
