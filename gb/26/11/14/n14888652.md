<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>【车评】公路越野双栖皮卡 2026 Honda Ridgeline TrailSport</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888683-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_02-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>2026 Honda Ridgeline TrailSport。（李奥／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年11月14日讯】在竞争激烈的皮卡市场，很多品牌都尝试推出一款皮卡来，Honda在多年前已推出Ridgeline，这次测试的Honda Ridgeline TrailSport，是第二代Rigdeline，加上TrailSport套件，将这皮卡的越野特性更加强化，令开着这皮卡无论在道路或野外，也能轻松面对。</p>
<p><a loading="lazy" title="YouTube video player" data2-src=""></a><a href="https://d11leod1im7q2u.cloudfront.net/V0jFLkIIZo2i"><img width="600" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/1992513/djy/master/gb/300/djtsp.jpg" title="进入原文观看"  alt="进入原文观看"></a><h3 align=center>(视频.国内建议下载<a href="https://github.com/1992513/www/blob/master/README.md#8">翻墙软件</a>进入原文观看)</h3><a src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/Us5XbpbGxzk?si=dL2Y1YmF_YEWEnJN" width="560" b="315" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></a></p>
<p>第二代Honda Ridgeline自2017年推出，以取代第一代车款，前脸设计更为流线，带点同厂SUV的设计。在结构上Ridgeline与一般Pickup也是使用车身与底盘分开制造，在后期才拼合“Body on Frame”结构不同。Honda Ridgeline是探用车身底盘一体的设计，称为“Unibody”，Ridgeline使用的是Honda的ACE（Advanced Compatibility Engineering）车架，在车体坚固度及抗扭性也很高。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14888656" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14888656" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888656-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_04.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14888656" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888656-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_04-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14888656" class="wp-caption-text">2026 Honda Ridgeline TrailSport。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14888658" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14888658" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888658-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_07.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14888658" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888658-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_07-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14888658" class="wp-caption-text">前脸更为流线。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>第一代Ridgeline另一出众的尾闸设计，拥有两种开启方法，第一种是一般皮卡的方法，也就是“向下翻”，好处是占用地方少，而且也可作为搬运平台，但是关上时因闸门的重量而会较费力气；另一种就是Ridgeline独有的横揭开(从右往左开），需要后方有多一点位置，因可利用车牌下方的踏板，爬上车卡也较为容易，而且关上闸门时也没“往上关”般费劲。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14888659" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14888659" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888659-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_14.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14888659" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888659-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_14-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14888659" class="wp-caption-text">尾闸有两种开启方法。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>此外，在货卡下方有一个隐藏的储物位置，后备轮胎也藏在这，空间也挺大的，可以用作放置一些贵重物品，也可锁上，比放东西在车厢内更安全。但是也有缺点，就是如果货卡上有东西的话，使要先将东西移开，才可开启。后排座位的座垫可向椅背靠折，成为另一个高而宽的储物空间，足够放入自行车等大型物件，而且像汽车的后排座椅一样，可六四分别折叠，弹性更大。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14888660" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14888660" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888660-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_15.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14888660" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888660-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_15-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14888660" class="wp-caption-text">货卡下方隐藏的储物位置。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>TrailSport版在外型上与其他版本的最大分别，是车身上多处的“TrailSport”徽章，四个轮胎是专为越野的245／60R18 105T All-Terrain，轮圈也是专为TrailSport版而设的18英寸灰色合金轮圈。看不到的又有越野专用的悬挂系统。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14888662" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14888662" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888662-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_09.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14888662" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888662-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_09-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14888662" class="wp-caption-text">18英寸灰色合金轮圈。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Ridgeline的动力来自一副3.5升V6引擎，有VCM系统，可在不需要时减少供油给某些汽缸，减少耗油。最大马力为280匹，比上一代增加30匹；峰值扭矩262磅呎，也比旧款多出15磅呎，最大拖力为5,000磅。车身重量也比旧有减少了78磅，所以动力更显充沛。至于在初推出时的两驱版本，都已经全部改为只有四驱选择，四驱系统名为i-VTM4全轮驱动系统。变速箱是9速自动变速箱。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14888664" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14888664" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888664-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_17.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14888664" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888664-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_17-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14888664" class="wp-caption-text">3.5升V6引擎。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>第二代Ridgeline在设备上也紧追潮流，2026年款Rigeline的中控台装置了9英寸的触屏，而且可兼容Apple CarPlay或Android Auto，同时又可有USB、蓝牙连接、XM收音机等功能。恒温系统也分为三区设计，即前座左、右及后排三区。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14888665" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14888665" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888665-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_18.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14888665" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888665-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_18-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14888665" class="wp-caption-text">仪表板。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14888666" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14888666" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888666-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_19.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14888666" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888666-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_19-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14888666" class="wp-caption-text">19英寸中控台触屏。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>安全方面，Ridgeline有Honda Sensing系统，包括i-ACTIVSENSE是一套全方位的防御系统，内里由多项技术组合而成，包括盲点系统〈Blind Spot Information System〉、碰撞缓解刹车系统〈Collision Mitigation Braking System〉、道路偏离缓解系统〈Road Departure Mitigation System〉等等。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14888668" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14888668" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888668-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_25.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14888668" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888668-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_25-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14888668" class="wp-caption-text">Ridgeline内装。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>作为中型皮卡的Ridgeline，开着它上路就如开着一辆SUV一样容易操控，而尾闸的两种开启方式，可以迎合不同场合，隐藏空间放重要东西时更放心。有消息指Honda将会在不久将来推出第三代Rigeline，外形将会更硬朗，对于Ridgeline车迷绝对是好消息。◇</p>
<figure id="attachment_14888670" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14888670" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888670-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_26.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14888670" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14888670-2026_Honda_Ridgeline_Trailsport_26-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14888670" class="wp-caption-text">后排座垫可翻起。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
