<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>休士顿台湾青商会秋游农场亲子同欢乐</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882491-P01-600x400.jpeg" />
<h6>休士顿台湾青商会秋游农场与会者合影。（图/TJCCGH提供）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年11月04日讯】（大纪元讯）秋日阳光下，孩子们开心奔跑，大人们热情投入游戏，欢笑声此起彼落。休士顿台湾青商会（TJCCGH）于10月31日（星期六）在德州Blessington Farms举办“秋季农场欢乐体验”活动，吸引近40位青商会员及亲友携家带眷参与，在轻松愉快的氛围中共享亲子时光，也为秋日留下美好回忆。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14882492" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14882492" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882492-P02.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14882492" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882492-P02-600x450.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14882492" class="wp-caption-text">青商会员透过游戏与互动拉近彼此距离，在欢笑中增进情谊。（图/TJCCGH提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>有别于平日的专业讲座与商务交流，青商会此次特别安排户外农场之旅，让会员暂时放下忙碌的工作，走进大自然，透过亲子活动与团体游戏增进交流，拉近彼此距离。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14882493" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14882493" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882493-P03.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14882493" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882493-P03-600x450.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14882493" class="wp-caption-text">与会者搭乘干草车开心拍照留念。（图/TJCCGH提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>活动最热闹的莫过于拔河竞赛与滚轮胎游戏。拔河时，参赛者铆足全力、齐心合作，场边加油声不断；滚轮胎游戏则考验团队默契与协调能力，过程笑料百出。无论输赢，大家都乐在其中，大人仿佛找回童心，孩子们也从游戏中体会合作与分享的乐趣。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14882494" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14882494" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882494-P04.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14882494" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882494-P04-600x338.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="338" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14882494" class="wp-caption-text">活动的拔河参赛者铆足全力情景。（图/TJCCGH提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>农场内丰富的游乐设施同样深受欢迎。大小朋友搭乘干草车（Hay Wagon Ride）欣赏德州乡村风光，体验趣味小鸭竞赛（Duck Races）、大型溜滑梯（Slide Mountain），还有考验勇气的绳索挑战（Ropes Course）及充满寻宝乐趣的宝石淘洗（Gemstone Mining）。从动态竞赛到悠闲探索，让不同年龄层的参与者都能尽兴而归。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14882495" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14882495" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882495-P05.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14882495" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882495-P05-600x450.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14882495" class="wp-caption-text">与会者携家带眷，在轻松愉快的氛围中共享欢乐时光。（图/TJCCGH提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>青商会会长施主恩表示，青商会长期致力于促进青年专业成长、商业交流及社区连结，但更希望透过多元活动，让会员之间建立如家人般的深厚情谊。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14882496" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14882496" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882496-P06.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14882496" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882496-P06-600x450.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14882496" class="wp-caption-text">青商会员透过户外踏青互动增进彼此情谊，也在欢笑中结识新朋友，拓展友谊与交流。（图/TJCCGH提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>他感性地说，虽然当天天气炎热，但看到会员带着家人热情参与，孩子们玩得开心，大人们也放下平日忙碌，一起拔河、欢笑，令人十分感动。“青商会最珍贵的，不只是一起完成多少活动，而是透过一次次相聚，让原本陌生的人逐渐成为朋友，让彼此的情谊更加深厚。”</p>
<p>施主恩也感谢参与活动的会员、家长与小朋友，以及幕后协助筹备的理事团队，让活动顺利圆满。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14882497" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14882497" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882497-P07.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14882497" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14882497-P07-600x300.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="300" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14882497" class="wp-caption-text">会员热情投入农场绳索套牛游戏，欢笑声此起彼落。（图/TJCCGH提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>一场农场之旅，不仅让亲子共享欢乐时光，也让青商会员在游戏与互动中增进情谊。未来，休士顿台湾青商会将持续秉持“Connect ? Grow ? Lead”（连结、成长、领导）的精神，结合专业学习、生活体验与社区交流，打造兼具成长机会与归属感的青年交流平台。</p>
