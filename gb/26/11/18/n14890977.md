<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>纽约中央车站美食新地标 意式餐酒馆兼市集开张</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890978-Grand-Central-Osteria-Mercato-01.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />

Group）操刀设计，结合精致内用与快速外带双轨模式，全方位满足饕客与通勤族的味蕾。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px
