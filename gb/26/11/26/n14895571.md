<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>西澳2025至2026财年GSP增长2.4% 建筑业及私营部门活动为主要动力</h2>
<h6></h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年11月26日讯】<span lang="zh-TW">（大纪元记者林达澳洲珀斯编译报导）澳大利亚统计局（</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">ABS</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">）</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">11</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">月</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">18</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">日公布的数据显示，西澳</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2025</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">至</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2026</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">财年州生产总值（</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">Gross</span> <span lang="en-AU">State</span> <span lang="en-AU">Product</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">，简称</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">GSP</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">）增长</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2.4%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">，略高于全国</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2.3%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">的增幅。其中，建筑业对</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">GSP</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">增长的贡献最大。</span></p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">西澳政府同日公布的数据则显示，该财年西澳州内经济增长</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">3.6%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">，增幅位居全国第二；私营部门经济活动增长</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">4.3%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">，贡献了州内经济增长的近</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">90%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">。这两项数据与</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">GSP</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">增幅</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2.4%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">属于不同的统计指标。西澳</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">GSP</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">在</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2024</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">至</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2025</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">财年增长</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">1%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">，</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2025</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">至</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2026</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">财年已连续第九年实现增长。</span></p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">投资方面，商业投资增长</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">8.3%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">，达到</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">668</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">亿澳元，创</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2015</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">至</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2016</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">财年以来最高水平；住宅投资增长</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">8.9%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">，达到</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">156</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">亿澳元，创十年来年度最高纪录。家庭消费增长</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2.6%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">，高于全国</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2.2%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">的增幅。</span></p>
<p>建筑业增长<span lang="en-AU">7.6%</span>，主要受住宅及非住宅建筑活动增加推动。采矿业产出增长<span lang="en-AU">1.1%</span>，得益于金属矿石产量提高、有利的运营条件、新增产能及生产效率改善。</p>
<p>不过，电力、天然气、供水及废物处理服务业萎缩<span lang="en-AU">2.6%</span>，部分抵消了其它行业的增长。<span lang="en-AU">ABS</span>指出，这主要是由于冬季气温较为温和，导致相关需求减弱。</p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">就业方面，西澳就业人数首次突破</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">170</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">万，创历史新高。该财年就业人数增长</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">1.6%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">，平均失业率为</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">4.2%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">。</span></p>
<p><span lang="en-AU">2025</span>至<span lang="en-AU">2026</span>财年，西澳经济占全国经济总量的<span lang="en-AU">16.2%</span>，而其人口仅占全国总人口的<span lang="en-AU">11.1%</span>。西澳商品出口增长<span lang="en-AU">1.2%</span>，约占澳大利亚商品出口总额的<span lang="en-AU">45%</span>。</p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">西澳人均州生产总值继续位居全国首位。政府表示，西澳人均州生产总值比全国平均水平高出近</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">50%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">。不过，</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">ABS</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">数据显示，</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2025</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">至</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">2026</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">财年西澳实际人均州生产总值仅增长</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">0.2%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">，低于全国</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">0.8%</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">的增幅。人均州生产总值反映每名居民平均创造的经济产出，并不代表居民的平均收入。</span></p>
<p><span lang="zh-TW">此外，国际信用评级机构穆迪（</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">Moody&#8217;s</span> <span lang="en-AU">Ratings</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">）再次确认西澳的</span><span lang="en-US"><span lang="en-AU">AAA</span></span><span lang="zh-TW">最高信用评级，评级展望维持稳定。穆迪表示，西澳的信用状况受惠于具竞争力的经济基础、强劲的自主收入创造能力，以及稳健的财政管理。</span></p>
<p>全国各地区<span lang="en-AU">GSP</span>增幅方面，北领地以<span lang="en-AU">3.2%</span>居首，昆州和南澳分别增长<span lang="en-AU">3.1%</span>和<span lang="en-AU">2.6%</span>，均高于西澳的<span lang="en-AU">2.4%</span>。</p>
