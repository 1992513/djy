<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>实用美语：困了想“眯一会儿”怎么说最地道？</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2022/05/id13730168-shutterstock_1114752902.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>学英语时，我们最早学会的“睡觉”大概就是 sleep。所以晚上困了，很自然地会说：</p><p>I&#x27;m going to sleep.（我要去睡觉了）</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>这句当然没有错，但在美国人的日常口语里，“我要睡了”“找个地方睡一晚”“补个眠”其实有不少更生动、更地道的说法。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>1. hit the sack</strong><strong>：</strong><strong>我要上床睡觉了</strong></span></h2><p>如果时间不早了，你准备回房睡觉，可以说：</p><p>I&#x27;m going to hit the sack.（我要上床睡觉了）</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>sack本来是“麻袋、布袋”。以前普通人睡的床垫常常是用粗布袋填入干草、稻草做成的，所以hit the sack后来便有了“上床睡觉”的意思。类似的说法还有hit the hay，hay是“干草”。例如：</p><p>I&#x27;m exhausted. I think I&#x27;ll hit the hay.<br/>（我累坏了，我想我该睡觉了。）</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>所以hit the sack/hay不是真的“打麻袋”、“打干草”，是“上床睡觉”的意思。</p><p>[延伸阅读]：<ahref="https://www.epochtimes.com/b5/26/8/31/n14839798.md#1" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">“受委屈”英语怎么说？西方人逻辑大不同</a></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>2. crash</strong><strong>：</strong><strong>累得只想倒头就睡</strong></span></h2><p>crash最常见的意思是“撞击、碰撞”，比如：</p><p>The car crashed into a tree.（那辆车撞上了一棵树。）</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>但在美国口语里，一个人累得不行，回去往床上一倒，也可以用crash。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>I&#x27;m exhausted. I&#x27;m going home to crash.<br/>（我累死了，要回家倒头睡觉。）</p><p>crash还有一个非常实用的用法：借宿。</p><p>Can I crash at your place tonight?<br/>（我今晚能借宿你家吗？）</p><p>I just need somewhere to crash for the night.<br/>（我只需要找个地方睡一晚。）</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><p>所以，如果朋友突然问你：Do you mind if I crash at your place tonight? 意思就是“今晚能不能让我在你家凑合一宿？”</p><p>不过crash语气比较随便，适合朋友之间。如果你是在Airbnb、旅馆或比较正式的场合联络房东，最好不要一上来就说I need a place to crash。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2010/05/1005081446252054.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2010/05/1005081446252054.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2010/05/1005081446252054.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2010/05/1005081446252054.jpg" alt="想“找个地方落脚”“找地方安顿一晚”，英语要怎么说？(Getty images)" width="640" b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>想“找个地方落脚”“找地方安顿一晚”，英语要怎么说？(Getty images)</figcaption></figure></div><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>3. a place to lay my head</strong><strong>：</strong><strong>找个地方落脚</strong></span></h2><p>有一种说法比crash温和得多：I&#x27;m looking for a place to lay my head for the night.（我想找个地方过一夜。）</p><p>lay my head字面意思是“把我的头放下来”，实际表达的不只是“睡觉”，还有一点“找个地方落脚”“有个地方安顿一晚”的味道。</p><p>比如一个人出差工作，可以说：I&#x27;m working in San Rafael and just need a comfortable place to lay my head after work.（我在圣拉斐尔工作，下班后只想找个舒服的地方落脚休息。）</p><p>这种说法用在给民宿房东的留言里就很自然。</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>4. get some shut-eye</strong><strong>：</strong><strong>我得眯一会儿</strong></span></h2><p>shut是“闭上”，eye是“眼睛”，所以shut-eye很形象：把眼睛闭上，睡觉。</p><p>I need to get some shut-eye.（我得睡一会儿了。）</p><p>You should get some shut-eye before the flight.<br/>（坐飞机之前你应该先睡一会儿。）</p><p>这个说法尤其适合“休息一下、补点眠”，不一定表示正式上床睡一整晚。</p><p>[延伸阅读]：<ahref="https://www.epochtimes.com/b5/26/11/12/n14888009.md#1" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">实用美语：没听懂别光说What？这几句更自然</a></p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>5. catch some Z</strong><strong>&#x27;</strong><strong>s</strong><strong>：</strong><strong>去抓几个</strong><strong> Z</strong></span></h2><p>一看就很有美国漫画味道的说法。</p><p>I need to catch some Z&#x27;s.（我得去睡一会儿。）</p><p>为什么是Z？看看漫画里睡着的人，头顶经常飘着：Zzzzz……于是Z&#x27;s就代表睡眠，catch some Z&#x27;s 也就成了“睡一觉、补个觉”。</p><p>I&#x27;ve got an hour before my next meeting. I&#x27;m going to catch some Z&#x27;s.<br/>（下个会议还有一小时，我去眯一会儿。）</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>6. turn in</strong><strong>：</strong><strong>今天就到这儿，我睡去了</strong></span></h2><p>这个也非常实用：I think I&#x27;ll turn in.（我想我该睡了。）</p><p>It&#x27;s been a long day. I&#x27;m going to turn in early tonight.<br/>（今天真够累的，我今晚要早点睡。）</p><p>turn in在不同情境还有“呈递”的意思，例如turn in your homework是“交作业”。但是晚上聊天时，有人看看时间说：Well, I think I&#x27;ll turn in. 通常就是：“好了，我要睡觉去了。”</p><p>还有一句意思很接近：Let&#x27;s call it a night.（今晚就到这儿吧）。意思是“到此为止”，不一定说完立刻上床。</p><p>比如朋友聚会到11点，有人说：It&#x27;s getting late. Let&#x27;s call it a night.（不早了，今晚就到这儿吧。）</p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><strong>结语</strong></span></h2><p>所以，下次想说“我要睡觉了”，除了I&#x27;m going to sleep，还可以根据场合换着说：</p><p>I&#x27;m going to hit the sack.（我要上床睡觉了）</p><p>I&#x27;m going to crash.（我累得要倒头睡了）</p><p>I need to get some shut-eye.（我得睡一会儿）</p><p>I&#x27;m going to catch some Z&#x27;s.（我去补个觉）</p><p>I think I&#x27;ll turn in.（我想我该睡了）</p><p>Let&#x27;s call it a night.（今晚就到这儿吧）</p><p>而如果你人在外面，只想找个地方安安稳稳睡上一晚，一句：I just need a place to lay my head. 听起来有那么一点“但求一席安眠之地”的味道。英语里的“睡觉”原来也可以说得这么生动。@*</p><p><strong><em>作者简介：</em></strong><em>老哈利在中国大陆有超过</em><em>15</em><em>年的一线教学经验，曾在新东方英语等英语培训机构教授雅思、托福等课程，并创办自己的英语培训学校，所开设之“中老年实用英语班”广受学员欢迎。</em><em>移民美国后特在此开设“新移民实用美语”栏目，希望您在此学到英语教科书上不教的、免费英语班上外教不讲的一些最实用的零基础英语，助您尽快融入北美社区生活。</em></p><p>责任编辑；沈少棋
