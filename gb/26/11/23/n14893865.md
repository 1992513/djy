<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>日本枫红和歌山！邂逅古城与猫电车</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14893882-1-3-DSC09563.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>初冬的日本，总有一种无法形容的温柔气息。空气微凉，阳光澄澈，枝头枫叶由绿转红，仿佛大自然在季节更迭之际，特意为旅人铺展一幅浓淡相宜的风景画，走在其间，真是舒服极了！</p><p>这一回走访和歌山城，没有东京的繁华喧嚣，也少了大阪的热闹奔放，却在古城、庭园、海味与一列充满童趣的电车之间，遇见日本最耐人寻味的人文风情。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><h3>和歌山城承载着战国岁月</h3><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div
