<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>休斯顿新马美食飘香 南洋文化欢聚传情</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890384-01-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>MSAH街市美食博览会（Hawker Fair）现场，嘉宾、会员及志工欢聚一堂，合影留念。（陆希／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年11月18日讯】（大纪元记者陆希休斯顿报导）休斯顿马来西亚新加坡协会（Malaysian Singaporean Association of Houston，MSAH）于11月14日（星期六）在Bear Creek Park Pavilion #6举办一年一度的“MSAH Hawker Fair街市美食博览会”。现场香气四溢、人潮络绎不绝，吸引大批民众前来品尝道地南洋美食，体验多元文化风情，共度欢乐的周末时光。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14890386" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14890386" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890386-03.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14890386" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890386-03-600x450.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14890386" class="wp-caption-text">协会会长林焌轩（前排左三）邀请所有赞助商及协办单位和工作团队等合影。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>开幕式由协会会长林焌轩（John Lim）主持，驻休斯顿马来西亚官员Ainul Maria Abu Bakar、秘书Afiq Syah Putra Mohamad Murni，以及环球第一银行公关总裁Elisa 徐、美南银行公关总裁庄帏婷、潮州同乡会理事长许益明、金城银行等赞助商代表出席，共同为活动揭开序幕。开场以欢乐的民族歌舞登场，身着各国传统服饰的会员手持铃鼓、欢唱民谣，展现浓厚的东南亚文化特色，现场洋溢着欢乐气氛。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14890385" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14890385" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890385-02.jpeg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14890385" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890385-02-600x450.jpeg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14890385" class="wp-caption-text">活动开场，身着各国传统服饰的会员手持铃鼓，载歌载舞，展现浓郁的东南亚文化风情，为美食盛会揭开欢乐序幕。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>林焌轩致欢迎词时，以马来语、华语等多种语言向与会者问候，并介绍马来语“Selamat Datang”意为“欢迎”。他表示，美食是连结家乡与海外游子的情感桥梁，熟悉的食物香气与味道，总能唤起新马移民的家乡回忆，让身处异乡的民众感受到家乡的温暖。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14890387" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14890387" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890387-04.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14890387" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890387-04-600x444.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="444" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14890387" class="wp-caption-text">驻休斯顿马来西亚官员Ainul Maria Abu Bakar出席活动并致词。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Ainul Maria Abu Bakar致词时，特别感谢协会、志工及表演人员的辛勤付出，让不同族裔的民众齐聚一堂。她强调，活动不仅是庆祝文化传统，更重要的是让年轻一代与社区建立连结，认识自身文化根源、历史与故事，并透过分享各族群的文化特色，在不同文化之间搭建友谊与理解的桥梁。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14890391" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14890391" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890391-07.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14890391" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890391-07-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14890391" class="wp-caption-text">协会会长林焌轩（左一）颁发感谢状及礼物给驻休马来西亚官员Ainul Maria Abu Bakar。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>已有逾30年历史的街市美食博览会，旨在为协会筹募经费并推广南洋文化。今年汇聚逾20个美食摊位，由当地马来西亚、新加坡移民家庭及知名商家精心准备，推出咖喱鸡饭、叻沙、沙爹、肉骨茶、南洋粿糕、娘惹糕及特色饮品等琳琅满目的道地佳肴，让民众一饱口福。现场各摊位前人潮不断，许多民众排队选购平日难得品尝的家乡美味。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14890388" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14890388" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890388-05.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14890388" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890388-05-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14890388" class="wp-caption-text">协会会长林焌轩（左）颁发感谢状给予潮州同乡会理事长许益明。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>活动亦介绍印尼特色料理，鼓励民众勇于探索不同风味。林焌轩幽默地表示，若不知道从哪一道料理开始，不妨循着香气寻找自己喜爱的美食。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14890390" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14890390" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890390-06.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14890390" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890390-06-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14890390" class="wp-caption-text">协会会长林焌轩（左）颁发感谢状给予环球第一银行公关总裁Elisa 徐。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>此次活动获得新加坡全球网络（Singapore Global Network，SGN）、马来西亚使馆、潮州会馆及多家企业支持。主办单位特别向赞助商及合作伙伴致谢并颁发感谢状，感谢各界共同促成活动顺利举行。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14890392" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14890392" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890392-08.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14890392" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890392-08-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14890392" class="wp-caption-text">合作伙伴在活动现场介绍南洋粿糕、娘惹糕等特色点心，并合影留念，展现道地南洋美食文化的独特魅力。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14890393" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14890393" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890393-09.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14890393" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14890393-09-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14890393" class="wp-caption-text">民众手捧在美食博览会现场购得的咖喱叶植株，并介绍其为南洋料理中常用的香料植物，可为菜肴增添独特香气。（陆希／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>活动最后，嘉宾、会员及志工欢聚合影，留下美好纪念。透过美食、歌舞与社区联谊，这场一年一度的南洋盛会不仅传承文化、凝聚乡情，也为休斯顿多元族裔社区增添交流与友谊。</p>
