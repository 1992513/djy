<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>【车评】跑魂节能兼备 2026 Toyota RAV4 PHEV GR Sport</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884949-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_01-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>2026 Toyota RAV4 PHEV GR Sport。（李奥／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年11月07日讯】刚刚才从NWAPA举办的2026 Drive Revolution回来，就弄来一辆Toyota RAV4 PHEV GR Sport，RAV4已有近30年的历史，一直是中小型SUV级距的领头羊。时至今日，它不仅是美国最畅销的中小型SUV，自1996年在美国市场推出以来，已累积超过640万辆RAV4在路上奔驰。最新的Toyota RAV4登场，有着全新外貌，并且只有油电及插电式油电动力，而今次测试的RAV4 GR Sport是PHEV版。</p>
<p><a loading="lazy" title="YouTube video player" data2-src=""></a><a href="https://dycg1eql8aigr.cloudfront.net/S2cBTjwubRnj"><img width="600" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/1992513/djy/master/gb/300/djtsp.jpg" title="进入原文观看"  alt="进入原文观看"></a><h3 align=center>(视频.国内建议下载<a href="https://github.com/1992513/www/blob/master/README.md#8">翻墙软件</a>进入原文观看)</h3><a src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/R6bwm7HEh6A?si=KSX21_n4Wo0NYfkq" width="560" b="315" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></a></p>
<p>2026年款RAV4集以往各代RAV4的特点，既有优雅又有运动风格，再将各特点融入三种不同的外观风格：核心（Core）、粗犷（Rugged）与运动（Sport）放在不同型号之上。核心风格在LE、XLE Premium和Limited等级；粗犷设计则运用于Woodland版本；而SE、XSE以及首次推出的GR SPORT版则使用运动化外形。新RAV4的TNGA-K底盘部分也经过重新强化，增强了车架刚性并采用全新悬吊组件，带来顺畅且轻快好操控的驾驭体验。 从侧向看，RAV4的线条视觉是向上扩展至后部，新式LED尾灯也将尾闸带来科技感，同时后部时扩大了宽度。此外，前翼片、后扰流器、多样的饰条配色以及轮圈处理等细节，赋予RAV4独特的个人风格与气质。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884929" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884929" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884929-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_05.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884929" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884929-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_05-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884929" class="wp-caption-text">2026 Toyota RAV4 PHEV GR Sport。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>悬挂系统也进行了重新调校。重新设计的前后螺旋弹簧，大大减少了摩擦并提高乘坐舒适度；并且配备了新型避震器，增加了阻尼系数和横向力感应度，有助于在各种速度下维持平稳舒适的行驶感受。底部的悬挂臂衬套经过更新，降低车身振动和噪音。此外，前稳定杆的位置也进行了改良，借此提升连贯且精确的转向回馈感。LE、XLE Premium、SE和XSE型号的离地间隙为8.1英寸；Woodland版为8.5英寸；而GR SPORT车型则维持在7.5英寸。测试车采用红色涂装为主，再在不同部分加上黑色配件，红与黑的搭配加强了运动感。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884930" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884930" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884930-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_07.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884930" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884930-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_07-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884930" class="wp-caption-text">运动化外形。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14884932" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884932" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884932-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_09.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884932" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884932-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_09-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884932" class="wp-caption-text">大灯设计新颍。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>全新一代Toyota RAV4去掉了纯汽油版动力系统，只有油电（Hybrid）或插电式油电（Plug-in Hybrid）动力系统。油电混动版搭载了Toyota最新的第五代混合动力系统。由一副2.5公升直列四缸引擎（型号 A25A-FXS），这套动力系统透过行星式无段变速箱，结合两个马达发电机（MG1 和 MG2）来驱动前轮。当引擎与主电动机（MG2）协同运作时，能够确保极为灵敏的动力输出；同时，藉由利用两个电动马达（MG1 和 MG2）为锂离子驱动电池充电，也完美地实现了卓越的能耗效率。前轮驱动（FWD）车型提供226匹综合马力；而电子式智慧四轮驱动（E-Four）车型则拥有236匹净综合马力。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884935" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884935" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884935-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_20.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884935" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884935-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_20-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884935" class="wp-caption-text">插电式油电系统。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14884936" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884936" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884936-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_13.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884936" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884936-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_13-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884936" class="wp-caption-text">充电端口。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>PHEV采用第六代插电式混合动力系统，以一台2.5升四缸引擎配两个电马达（MG1 与 MG2）、一个独立后轮电动马达 (MGR)，以及高容量的动力电池，综合马力为324匹。其中，电驱动马达（MG2）提供203匹和201磅英尺扭力；后轮电动马达（MGR）提供91磅英尺的扭力。前轴采用了碳化硅半导体元件，以达到小型化和高效率的目标。燃油经济性和纯电续航里程。SE和XSE等级的油耗约为43／37（市区／高速）；Woodland型号为 42／35 MPG；GR SPORT型号则为 41／34 MPG。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884937" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884937" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884937-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_21.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884937" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884937-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_21-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884937" class="wp-caption-text">全数位仪表板。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>PHEV版RAV4在充满电力后，可纯电力行走约50英里（XSE &#8211; 52英里，Woodland跟GR Sport &#8211; 49英里），之后就会如一般Hybrid车运行。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884938" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884938" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884938-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_22.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884938" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884938-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_22-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884938" class="wp-caption-text">触控萤幕显示。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14884939" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884939" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884939-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_23.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884939" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884939-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_23-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884939" class="wp-caption-text">手机充电板，旁边为驾驶模式键。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>所有RAV4都配备了四种行驶模式，包括“一般（NORMAL）”： 出厂预设设定；“经济（ECO）”，在经常需要加速和刹车的行程中，能显著协助降低燃油消耗；“运动（SPORT）”，油门及方向盘的反应更为迅速；“自订（CUSTOM）”，让驾驶者能够自行调整车辆各项系统参数，满足个人最理想的驾驶偏好。另外，PHEV也有“Trail”与“Snow”，以及“Auto EV”模式。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884940" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884940" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884940-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_24.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884940" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884940-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_24-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884940" class="wp-caption-text">变速杆。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>进入车内，全新中央控制台将多功能整合在一起，包含触控萤幕显示、驾驶模式切换和置物空间，打造出灵巧井然的配置；中央手枕区则结合了排档杆、可拆卸隔板的杯架，以及双向开启的储物箱。在特定车型，采用了Toyota全新的线传排档（Shift-by-wire）系统，配备极简的拨杆切换开关，操作直觉，为全新 RAV4 注入了未来感。标配全新的12.3英寸全数位仪表板，高亮度、高对比的LCD萤幕，并使用具吸光效果的雾面处理，降低阳光直射时的眩光。此外，Hybrid Limited和PHEV XSE车型还额外配备了抬头显示器（HUD）。GR Sport车装上了金属踏板，增强运动感之余，驾车时也有实际帮助。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884941" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884941" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884941-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_29.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884941" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884941-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_29-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884941" class="wp-caption-text">车内设计简洁。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>测试的RAV4 GR Sport在强力加速时，就算是充满电时也会启动引擎，加强加速力度。除了50英里的纯电力行驶之外，RAV4 GR Sport的耗油量达到40（城市）及33（公路），非常省油，所以RAV4是既可节省汽油，又可偶尔尽情加速，而每天出行来回路程少于50英里的话，就等于可以不耗汽油了。◇</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884942" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884942" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884942-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_28.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14884942" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884942-2026_Toyota_RAV4_GR_Sport_28-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884942" class="wp-caption-text">GR方向盘。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
