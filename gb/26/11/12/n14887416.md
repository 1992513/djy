<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>波士顿北一女校友会庆11周年 大秀才艺</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887414-eebd3ba00664f72d54237830ce4abce0-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>11月7日，波士顿北一女校友会欢庆11周年，参与者合影。（冯文鸾/大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年11月12日讯】（大纪元记者冯文鸾柏灵顿镇报导）以“Lucky Eleven（幸运11）”为主题的波士顿北一女校友会第11届周年庆，于11月7日中午假Cafe Escadrille西餐厅举行。共有44位校友、嘉宾、眷属等60多人参加。会长徐丽玲团队精心策划很多新鲜有趣的节目，令与会者度过温馨热闹的午后。</p>
<div class="mceTemp"></div>
<figure id="attachment_14887408" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14887408" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887408-2dfa1354dc73fd8230865c10f74acda3.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14887408" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887408-2dfa1354dc73fd8230865c10f74acda3-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14887408" class="wp-caption-text">11月7日，波士顿北一女校友会欢庆11周年，本届理事群：前排左起李翠兰、张美惠、吴杏玫、徐丽玲、陈惠贞、叶景睿。后排左起冷沂霏、周诗茵、林游岚。（冯文鸾/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>徐丽玲首先介绍本届理事群：副会长李翠兰、财务陈惠贞、文书廖珮妤、活动冷沂霏、周诗茵、张美惠、叶景睿以及荣誉理事吴杏玫和林游岚。感谢干部们通力合作，方能顺利办好活动。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14887409" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14887409" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887409-0b2e24ba6ae3d01023929182abdc7ca9.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14887409" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887409-0b2e24ba6ae3d01023929182abdc7ca9-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14887409" class="wp-caption-text">11月7日，波士顿北一女校友会欢庆11周年，徐丽玲会长（左）感谢赵明华修女策划每月的活动。（冯文鸾/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>在“年度回顾”中，徐丽玲报告了今年度以校友会的名义开了银行新账户，成立了IRS认可的501(c)(3)非盈利组织，捐款可扣税。也新设了网址：https://sites.google.com/view/beinuboston/home。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14887411" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14887411" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887411-d2d4144ded93f3fe23367655e4ca90e8.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14887411" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887411-d2d4144ded93f3fe23367655e4ca90e8-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14887411" class="wp-caption-text">11月7日，波士顿北一女校友会欢庆11周年。波士顿北一女乐队演奏乐曲。（冯文鸾/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>徐丽玲感谢历届会长辛劳的付出，才有今天的成果。也特别感谢赵明华修女从3月份开始策划每月的活动，包括高尔夫球春季班、与文协合办卡拉OK、踏青等。注：赵明华是北一女100周年（2003）百位杰出校友之一。</p>
<p>会上还播放北一女第22任新校长蔡哲铭自我介绍及分享的视频，还有ABAF美国北一女校友基金会的影片。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14887413" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14887413" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887413-44ef77336262324c4591afa0c150310b.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14887413" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887413-44ef77336262324c4591afa0c150310b-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14887413" class="wp-caption-text">11月7日，波士顿北一女校友会欢庆11周年。舞蹈班秀恰恰舞。（冯文鸾/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>这两年来，经常出现在牛顿台湾日、双十国庆游行、升旗典礼等活动中的北一女乐仪队，在总监吴杏玫的召集下，表现亮眼。当天乐队加上吴萍萍和江佩蓉合音，演奏了Hero Hito，还穿插望春风等名谣，获得了热烈的掌声。</p>
<p>成军不久的舞蹈班成员穿着梁雅惠个人提供的绿色服装，跳起恰恰舞。张美惠说该班才练了3次，所以演出效果娱乐性很强。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14887407" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14887407" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887407-74d7c20263d20a55a79a761bf3812832.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14887407" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887407-74d7c20263d20a55a79a761bf3812832-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14887407" class="wp-caption-text">11月7日，波士顿北一女校友会欢庆11周年。不是在投票表决，而是坐着跟银幕的Cardio Workout做伸展运动。（冯文鸾/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14887415" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14887415" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887415-19938c3347a4a803aa2ed633796fad8d.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14887415" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887415-19938c3347a4a803aa2ed633796fad8d-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14887415" class="wp-caption-text">11月7日，波士顿北一女校友会欢庆11周年。校友会历届会长与黄耀良组长(中)合影，左起 赵小怡、吴国志、江佩蓉、李欣、吴萍萍、吴杏玫、林丽珠、林游岚、徐丽玲、李翠兰。（冯文鸾/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>经文处黄耀良组长代表关友钧处长出席（关处长在波士顿参加退伍军人大游行），他致词表示请大家多用经文处的官方网站、社交软体，来了解经文处的业务资讯、未来活动预告，有建议可互动。他还带来两份抽奖礼物，由创会会长赵小怡和郭明幸运被抽中。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14887412" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14887412" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887412-1e53c17582dcc2cb6685ad11d098f652.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14887412" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887412-1e53c17582dcc2cb6685ad11d098f652-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14887412" class="wp-caption-text">11月7日，波士顿北一女校友会欢庆11周年。黄耀良组长代表经文处赠送两份抽奖礼物，由赵小怡（右一）和郭明（左二）幸运获得。（冯文鸾/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14887410" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14887410" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887410-fc67457d83f0ba6355bf02f857034cb4.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14887410" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14887410-fc67457d83f0ba6355bf02f857034cb4-600x450.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="450" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14887410" class="wp-caption-text">11月7日，波士顿北一女校友会欢庆11周年。以“Lucky Eleven（幸运11）”为主题的波士顿北一女校友会第11届周年庆。（冯文鸾/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>为了营造气氛，徐丽玲特地订制了有11数字的绿色气球，还自掏腰包，以会长名义制作纪念品，送给大家当礼物，可谓诚意满满。</p>
<p>整个活动在唱校歌，大合照之后，圆满结束了。◇</p>
