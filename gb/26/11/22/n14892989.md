<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>【许润元译著】桥艺哲学家（38）</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/12/id14658116-FotoJet-2.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
<hr>【大纪元2026年11月22日讯】（大纪元记者叶诚允报导）《The bridge philosopher》一书作者James S. Kauder是位桥牌高手，喜好独辟蹊径。他在作品中通过一个个生动的小故事，教授桥牌技巧，同时寓教于乐。</p><p>中华民国桥艺教父级人物许润元先生，著书立说无数，为台湾桥坛奉献毕生精力。本书由其翻译，乃是中外二位桥牌大师合作之结晶。大纪元棋牌版块有幸付费授权连载《<ahref="https://www.epochtimes.com/b5/tag/%e6%a9%8b%e8%97%9d%e5%93%b2%e5%ad%b8%e5%ae%b6.md#1" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">桥艺哲学家</a>》这一力作，以飨读者。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><h2 data-start="861" data-end="872">三十八、<strong>反复无常的竞争者</strong></h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>好多人玩桥牌，每一个行动都在竞争。他们的目的好像任何合约都可能，而并不是挑他们最佳的成局合约。当然，好几手牌，他们的叫牌帮助了庄家的打牌。</p><p>打盘式桥牌，我坐南家，我拿：</p><p>? K J 4 2　<span style="color:#ff0000">?</span> A 2　<span style="color:#ff0000">?</span> A 10 3　? A 10 5 2</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><p>我们有身价，他们没有身价。在二家Pass（派司）以后，我开叫1NT。西家是一个很活跃而又反复无常的竞争者，超叫2?，表示他的牌组，北家叫2NT。</p><p>同伴的叫牌是响亮的。这种情形他的2NT可能纯粹为了竞争，也许祇有六七点。我手里刚好是16—18点的开叫1NT牌，并没有多余的牌力。但是我不愿丢了一个有身价的一局，我梅花上有二次控制，所以我叫3NT。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p>叫牌，在我的部分也许不够响亮，全部叫牌经过是：</p><p style="text-align:center"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14893000-Screenshot-2026-11-21-231101.jpg" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-14893000" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14893000-Screenshot-2026-11-21-231101.jpg" alt="" width="598" b="169"/></a></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p>西家首引 ? 5，同伴牌摊下来，好像他该叫 2<span style="color:#ff0000">?</span>，比 2NT 好。</p><p style="text-align:center"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14893001-Screenshot-2026-11-21-231101.jpg" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-14893001" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14893001-Screenshot-2026-11-21-231101.jpg" alt="" width="597" b="568"/></a></p><p>梦家的牌，比我想像的要好些，24点一局，等一会就要回家了。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>我由梦家跟小牌，东家跟 ? 7，我用 ? J 吃进。现在已有七磴顶张，我该怎样赢取九磴呢？</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>看起来，要发掘另外的二磴，就是向红心进军。当红心分配是3—3或西家握着 <span style="color:#ff0000">?</span> Q ×，我就能赢取四磴红心了。</p><p>从叫牌及首轮引牌看，西家好像有四张黑桃及五张梅花。于是当方块是6—1，那么红心可能是3—3。西家的分配是4—3—1—5。前途有好的路走吗？</p><p>如果西家梅花是 K Q J × ×，他当然首引梅花，他现在首引黑桃，证明了东家有一张单张梅花大牌。这意思是说祇要我敲下 ? Ace，由手上打一张小梅花向梦家的 ? 9 打去，在梅花上我又可以发展一磴牌。</p><p>西家的分配，不像是4—3—1—5，更像是4—2—2—5。如果我走梅花，那么可能对东家实施挤牌或最后迫出，如果有必须，为了第九磴，我还是可以偷红心。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><p>第二磴，我先取 ? K 吃。当我引一张小方块时，残局是如此：</p><p style="text-align:center"><strong><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14893004-Screenshot-2026-11-21-231101.jpg" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-14893004" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14893004-Screenshot-2026-11-21-231101.jpg" alt="" width="597" b="568"/></a></strong></p><p>西家用 <span style="color:#ff0000">?</span> J 吃进，如果他不取 ? 10，那我就要吃 <span style="color:#ff0000">?</span> Ace，<span style="color:#ff0000">?</span> Ace，再送出方块，使东家最后迫出。但是西家立刻取 ? 10，梦家我垫一张方块，东家也垫一张方块，但是当西家再出一张梅花时，梦家抛掉一张红心，东家在二门红牌上受挤了。全付牌是：</p><p style="text-align:center"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14893010-Screenshot-2026-11-21-231101.jpg" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class"><img class="alignnone size-full wp-image-14893010" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14893010-Screenshot-2026-11-21-231101.jpg" alt="" width="598" b="566"/></a></p><p dir="auto">西家及早打红心，就能击落合约，但是这是较难的防御。</p><p dir="auto">如果西家不叫牌，南家就无法找出胜利之路了。</p><p data-start="706" data-end="730">（转自《中国桥艺》杂志——《<ahref="https://www.epochtimes.com/b5/tag/%e6%a9%8b%e8%97%9d%e5%93%b2%e5%ad%b8%e5%ae%b6.md#1" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class">桥艺哲学家</a>》）</p><p data-start="706" data-end="730">责任编辑：王也知
