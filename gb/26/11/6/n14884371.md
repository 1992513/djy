<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>台湾高尔夫协会办2026感恩杯　Nick杨三任会长</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884378-DSC01105b-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>开赛前全员在club house前合影。（岳磊/大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2026年11月06日讯】（大纪元记者吕尚明亚特兰大报导）乔治亚州台湾高尔夫球协会（TGA）于11月1日（周日）在亚特兰大南边的Sugar Creek Golf &amp; Tennis Club举办2026年“感恩杯”高尔夫球友谊赛。会长Jessica一开放报名后不久，就达到108人额满，比赛当天所有报名球友全数到齐。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884380" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884380" style="width: 400px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884380-DSC01095-e1793935007392.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-full wp-image-14884380" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884380-DSC01095-e1793935007392.jpg" alt="" width="400" b="267" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884380" class="wp-caption-text">开赛前挥竿暖身。（岳磊/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>上球场之前，TGA为大家准备了丰盛美味的“苏姐美食屋”的含有油饭与盐酥鸡的台式便当以及大肠面线。南方蔬菜的吴政兴老板也赞助了运动饮料及香蕉。当天天气阴沉，原先预测可能下雨，让Jessica十分紧张，所幸所有球友都顺利打完全场后才开始下雨。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884392" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884392" style="width: 400px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884392-DSC_8341-e1793935695555.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-full wp-image-14884392" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884392-DSC_8341-e1793935695555.jpg" alt="" width="400" b="267" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884392" class="wp-caption-text">打球前的台式美食。（吕尚明/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14884404" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884404" style="width: 400px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884404-DSC_8336-e1793936845564.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-full wp-image-14884404" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884404-DSC_8336-e1793936845564.jpg" alt="" width="400" b="267" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884404" class="wp-caption-text">苏姐为大家盛大肠面线。（吕尚明/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14884393" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884393" style="width: 400px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884393-DSC_8344-e1793935859387.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-full wp-image-14884393" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884393-DSC_8344-e1793935859387.jpg" alt="" width="400" b="267" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884393" class="wp-caption-text">苏姐美食的全家福。（吕尚明/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>球赛结束后，众人前往与去年相同的Chamblee福临门海鲜餐厅参加晚宴。许多侨界领袖亦到场共襄盛举。安方老师还上台带领所有历任会长一起跳舞，气氛欢愉热烈。</p>
<p>会长Jessica的先生、荣誉会长Nick杨依照往年惯例，拿出珍藏的多瓶威士忌与球友们分享，现场气氛热络。晚宴中，Jessica正式将会长职务交棒给Nick杨。这是Nick第三度担任TGA会长。</p>
<p>侨教中心主任阎树荣也出席晚宴，并带来他与处长赞助的珍贵的台湾红茶与台湾之光噶玛兰威士忌作为摸彩奖品。Nick杨另外贡献两瓶威士忌，加上其他赞助者提供的丰富奖品，总价值高达6,000美元，让摸彩活动精彩不已。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14884394" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884394" style="width: 400px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884394-DSC01122-e1793935965651.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-full wp-image-14884394" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884394-DSC01122-e1793935965651.jpg" alt="" width="400" b="267" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884394" class="wp-caption-text">笑容满面，今天一定打得好。（岳磊/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14884395" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14884395" style="width: 400px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884395-DSC01272-e1793936052914.jpg"><img decoding="async" class="size-full wp-image-14884395" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14884395-DSC01272-e1793936052914.jpg" alt="" width="400" b="267" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14884395" class="wp-caption-text">好友同上场。（岳磊/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
