<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>【车评】 魂动之美 2026 Mazda CX-50 Turbo Premium Plus</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896780-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_01-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>2026 Mazda CX-50 Turbo Premium Plus。（李奥／大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2026年11月28日讯】上月由NWAPA举行的Drive Revolution，测试汽车中有Mazda的CX-50，然而测试车是Hybrid版的CX-50，而今次则是纯汽油版的CX-50，并且是有Turbo的CX-50，动力十足！</p>
<figure id="attachment_14896760" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896760" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896760-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_06.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896760" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896760-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_06-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896760" class="wp-caption-text">2026 Mazda CX-50 Turbo Premium Plus。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>CX-50在北美阿拉巴马州的亨茨维尔（Huntsville, Alabama）厂房制造，新厂房是与Toyota共同合作的Mazda Toyota Manufacturing (MTM) 。MTM厂房于2021年9月开始投入生产，每年将可制造300,000辆汽车。Mazda CX-50与CX-30及Mazda3共用同一底盘，并且同样采用横置引擎，以前置引擎、四轮驱动的驱动格式，测试车是CX-50 Turbo Premium Plus，故此搭载了2.5升涡轮增压引擎，最大马力输出227匹，峰值扭矩为310磅呎，但如果使用高级汽油的话，马力可再推高至256匹及320磅呎扭矩。四轮驱动系统名为i-Activ AWD，装有27个感应器，以每秒200次扫描道路情况，再立即调节每一个车轮的力量输出。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14896761" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896761" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896761-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_21.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896761" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896761-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_21-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896761" class="wp-caption-text">2.5升涡轮增压引擎。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Mazda CX-50装上了GVC系统，全称为G-Vectoring Control，通过主动控制引擎的扭矩输出，可减少急速转向时的离心力，让驾驶者及乘客有更佳的乘坐感，又可令汽车更稳定。这一代的G-Vectoring Control Plus，与i-Activ AWD系统结合工作，使反应更佳，也比上一代的动力流失减少60%，燃油效率也有所提高。CX-50与·Mazda3一样，配备了前进及倒车的Smart Brake系统，当感应器侦测到接近物体的话，系统会自动刹停汽车，Smart Brake可前后独立关上。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14896764" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896764" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896764-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_07.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896764" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896764-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_07-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896764" class="wp-caption-text">黑银双色轮圈。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>CX-50的外在尺寸，长宽高分别为185.8／72.9／63.9英寸，与新CX-5的长宽高184.6 / 73.2 / 66.7英寸比较，长度比CX-5稍长，然而CX-5却更高，CX-50并不是用以取代CX-5，看来CX-50是更为运动化的SAC款，而CX-5则更接近SUV款。CX-50是以新一代“Matured Kodo（魂动）”为设计语言，外型与其他新Mazda汽车一样简约，线条也更简单、直接。车前方设计没有过多的线条，进风口栏栅的网纹设计跟Mazda3也差不多。配上尖长的横型头灯，条状的雾灯在防撞器下方。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14896766" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896766" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896766-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_10.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896766" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896766-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_10-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896766" class="wp-caption-text">前灯组。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14896767" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896767" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896767-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_13.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896767" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896767-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_13-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896767" class="wp-caption-text">尾灯组。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>车内设计也是非常简洁，尤其是中控台，顶部是一个10.25英寸的宽萤幕，运行Mazda Connect资讯系统，控制的Commander Control在手枕前方，包括大圆盘及四个按键，音量控制及开关都则在其右方，使用上比触控更好用，因为实体键按下去有触感，旋扭转动的反应，与BMW的iDrive有异曲同工之妙。中控台中间则只有恒温系统的几个按键，视觉上非常清新，使用上也没有发现任何或缺。Mazda CX-50也装有方向盘及前座两段加热，在这个较冷的春夏之间非常好用。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14896768" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896768" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896768-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_29.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896768" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896768-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_29-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896768" class="wp-caption-text">简洁车内设计。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>CX-50装有Mi-Drive（Mazda Intelligent Drive Select）的驾驶模式选择，以往Mazda只有两种模式，就是Normal及Sport，而新一代的Mi-Drive新加入了Off-Road越野模式，只有简单的一个模式，没有其他复杂的控制，只要不是在柏油路面就可使用，系统就会自动调节各设定。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14896771" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896771" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896771-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_22.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896771" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896771-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_22-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896771" class="wp-caption-text">仪表板。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>仪表板虽然设计普通，都是三圆设计，但是其实中间的车速表是一个萤幕，左方引擎转速表及右方的油缸存量及温度，则是实体仪表。使用虚拟仪表的好处是可显示更多讯息，而且车速在“公里／时”与“英里／时”切换更为容易。另外，有不少功能是挺贴心的，例如指挥灯的响声，也有三种大小选择；仪表板的警示声响也可调节音量高低；驾驶座有两个位置记忆；前座拥有三段加热等。方向盘左侧的前台有一堆按键，其中的i-Stop按钮，是用作开关“引擎自动关闭”功能，让汽车停灯时，引擎不会关掉。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14896772" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896772" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896772-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_23.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896772" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896772-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_23-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896772" class="wp-caption-text">中控台萤幕。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14896773" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896773" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896773-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_27.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896773" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896773-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_27-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896773" class="wp-caption-text">Commander Control。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Mazda CX-50的抑噪也很好，行车时车厢非常静，因为路噪及风噪的声音细了，所以很容易超速也不知道，必须时常注意车速，还好CX-50装有稍为“Active Driving Display”的HUD，可视线不离路面也可看到车速。另外，测试的CX-50也装有大量高级装备，包括全景天窗、360度鸟瞰式视点摄险机、双区恒温系统、无线Apple Carplay、手机无线充电板、电动尾闸等。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14896774" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896774" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896774-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_31.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896774" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896774-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_31-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896774" class="wp-caption-text">行李舱容量巨大。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>Mazda CX-50的驾驶乐趣非常好，Turbo引擎的输出不断，引擎声沉厚有力，令整体感觉不输BMW X4。转向也相当敏锐，急速转向时CX-50仍然可跟着弯道转弯，而且倾斜度小。整体来说，CX-50的驾驶感很好，以$42,900美元起跳的顶级CX-50，实在是物超所值！◇</p>
<figure id="attachment_14896775" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14896775" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896775-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_28.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14896775" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2026/11/id14896775-2026_Mazda_CX-50_Turbo_28-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400" /></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14896775" class="wp-caption-text">前座舒适。（李奥／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
