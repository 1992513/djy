<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>高雄市祭孔仪节依明代礼制 全台最富特色</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/d9c7038ed511ef724524d8ab6cdc8c3a-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>高雄市举办的释奠典礼全部仪程皆邀集在地师生依循明代礼制参与完成，一直是全台最富有特色的祭孔仪节之一。 （高雄市立历史博物馆提供）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2018年09月28日讯】（大纪元记者方金媛台湾高雄报导）9月28日教师节，全国各地举办各式庆祝活动，高雄市9月28日上午7时在左营孔子庙举行纪念大成至圣先师孔子2568周年诞辰释奠典礼，全部仪程皆依循明代礼制，是全台最富有特色的祭孔仪节之一。</p>
<figure id="attachment_10747486" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-10747486" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/58cb7c27bf03ec9c4409ec7def1194f1.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-10747486" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/58cb7c27bf03ec9c4409ec7def1194f1-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-10747486" class="wp-caption-text">旧城国小学生担任八佾舞生。（方金媛／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_10747484" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-10747484" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/5f115161113d6d2c6b1b90390e2a92be.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-10747484" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/5f115161113d6d2c6b1b90390e2a92be-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-10747484" class="wp-caption-text">鼎金国中学生担任乐生。 （高雄市立历史博物馆提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>释奠礼在庄严的钟鼓乐声中，举行启扉、迎神、进馔、上香、献帛、献爵、献花、饮褔、受胙及送神等仪节，仪式结束后，民众拔“智慧毛”、过“聪（葱）门”、领“智慧糕”，象征聪明伶俐、满腹墨水，事事如意。</p>
<p>今年释奠典礼由左营旧城国小担任八佾舞生、鼎金国中担任麾生、乐生及歌生；左营高中担任祭典通赞、亚赞、礼生，共近250位师生组成仪礼队伍，高市府各级长官及校长分别担任正献官、分献官、陪祭官，代表高雄市政府、全体教职员生对万世师表孔子最高的敬意。现场同时有许多家长带领孩童观礼，透过祭孔大典，传承珍贵的中华传统文化，也让民众认识尊师重道的精神。</p>
<figure id="attachment_10747488" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-10747488" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/5fbc79727efdf36d4de44b9368a5fc16.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-10747488" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/5fbc79727efdf36d4de44b9368a5fc16-450x338.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="338"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-10747488" class="wp-caption-text">送神。 （方金媛／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p>高雄市自1980年孔子诞辰2530周年起举办祭孔释奠典礼，至今已38届，旧城国小师生从首届担任八佾舞生至今，几乎年年出席，指导老师曾望超与父亲曾宏一，以保存及发扬此项仪礼为使命，是八佾舞传承的重要推手。</p>
<p>曾望超说，过去，家世清白的男生才有资格担任八佾舞生，现在八佾舞已融入学校课程中，每位5、6年级学生都有机会练习。因八佾舞严肃庄重，学生必须静心才能学好，并得经过一个多月的练习，才能参与每年一度的祭典盛会。◇</p>
<figure id="attachment_10747485" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-10747485" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/a405e2b5a069829ab266803ea7c5f570.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-10747485" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/a405e2b5a069829ab266803ea7c5f570-450x274.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="274"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-10747485" class="wp-caption-text">民众拔象征智慧的牛毛笔。 （方金媛／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_10747487" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-10747487" style="width: 450px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/714bb51cbe52012b8a1aa0f534dc83a2.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-medium wp-image-10747487" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/09/714bb51cbe52012b8a1aa0f534dc83a2-450x300.jpg" alt="" width="450" b="300"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-10747487" class="wp-caption-text">过聪 （葱）门。（方金媛／大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
