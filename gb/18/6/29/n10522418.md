<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>巨鲸随船而行 当它现身时...千载难逢的惊喜</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/complete-video-link-600x65.jpg" rel="nofollow" />
<h6></h6>
<hr><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/complete-video-link-600x65.jpg" rel="nofollow" target="_blank" class="health-article-hover-class"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-10518582" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/complete-video-link-600x65-600x65.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="65"/></a></p><p></p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_2"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_2"></div></div></div><p>【大纪元2018年06月29日讯】（大纪元记者伊遐报导）澳洲的波捷游轮公司（Port Jet Cruise Adventures）专门办理澳洲东海岸麦爵里港附近海域的游览活动。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_1"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_1"></div></div></div><p>2017年7月在一次外海出游的时候，所有乘客惊喜地观赏了一场终生难忘的特别节目——神秘白鲸与我同行。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/foam.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/foam.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/foam.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/foam.jpg" alt="深海中冒出泡泡……（视频截图）" width="640" b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>深海中冒出泡泡……（视频截图）</figcaption></figure></div><p>麦爵里港（Port Macquarie）属新南威尔斯州，介于悉尼与布里斯本之间。澳洲东海岸是鲸群迁徙的路线，这儿是赏鲸的好地方。看到鲸鱼已经够让人高兴了，但是……</p><p>这只白鲸可不一般，它还有名字喔！它就是赏鲸界鼎鼎大名的米迦罗（Migaloo），是一头全身纯白的座头鲸，非常难得一见。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_3"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_3"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/white-whate-1.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/white-whate-1.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/white-whate-1.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/white-whate-1.jpg" alt="湛蓝的海面下有一抹神秘的白色身影。（视频截图）" width="640" b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>湛蓝的海面下有一抹神秘的白色身影。（视频截图）</figcaption></figure></div><p>1991第一次有人拍摄到它的身影，当时它出现在澳洲东岸新南威尔斯州的拜伦湾（Byron Bay）。到2011年之前，它是全世界保有纪录的唯一白鲸。</p><p>后来在别的景点也有人报告看到较小的白鲸。但除米迦罗之外，其它已知的白鲸也只有3、4只，罕见程度可见一斑。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_3"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_3"></div></div></div><p><span style="color:#3366ff"><strong>观赏完整影片?</strong></span></p><div class="shortcode sm:mx-auto"><!--$--><!--/$--></div><p>海洋科学家认为它是座头鲸的白色亚种，是由于基因突变而诞生的稀少种类，和所谓的“白子”（白化症）还不一样。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_4"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_4"></div></div></div><p>世界各地也有不少白色亚种的例子，如孟加拉白虎、南非白狮、白孔雀、北美柯莫德白熊等。人们都会视为珍奇而予以尊崇。</p><p>米迦罗在澳洲土语里意为“白色的家伙”。它受到澳大利亚《环境保护和生物多元性保护法案条例》中的鲸鱼和海豚观赏指南的保护。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/breathing-1400.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/breathing-1400.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/breathing-1400.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/breathing-1400.jpg" alt="“米迦罗”正在换气，有点像个可爱的小白兔。（视频截图）" width="640" b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>“米迦罗”正在换气，有点像个可爱的小白兔。（视频截图）</figcaption></figure></div><p>然而，由于米迦罗太珍贵了，澳洲昆士兰州和联邦政府立法又给了它额外的保护，明文规定，每个鲸鱼出没的季节，船只与白鲸的距离至少要有500米的范围，否则将被罚款16,500澳元。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_4"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_4"></div></div></div><p>原来这么身价不凡啊！洁白美丽的米迦罗的确充满了神秘。因为，游客并不是每年都会看到米迦罗的身影。1991正式报导后，一直到2008年才又有关于看到它的新闻。</p><div class=" lg:hidden"><div class="m-auto flex items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_M_article_336x280_5"><div id="DJY_M_article_336x280_5"></div></div></div><p>2015年有科学家解释说，随着米迦罗成熟并往深海里游去，我们可能会越来越看不到它了。所以，这次2017年的现身镜头更加弥足珍贵。</p><div><figure style="max-width:640px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/migaloo.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/migaloo.jpg" type="image/webp"/><source srcSet="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/migaloo.jpg" type="image/jpeg"/><img src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2018/06/migaloo.jpg" alt="“米迦罗”优雅地滑行，流线型的纯白身影以美丽的弧线划过海面……（视频截图）" width="640" b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>“米迦罗”优雅地滑行，流线型的纯白身影以美丽的弧线划过海面……（视频截图）</figcaption></figure></div><p>另一个幸运点是，米迦罗顺着船的方向平行前进了好一会，尽管鲸鱼距离船只有一段距离，但仍然接近到视频能够清晰拍摄的程度。</p><p>当时米迦罗正由南极迁徙至澳洲东北角的大堡礁（Great Barrier Reef）珊瑚礁群岛。两个幸运让我们得以一瞥神秘白鲸的风采。</p><p>而且当时的天候诚属完美——清澈的蓝天下，深邃湛蓝的海水与天际间还留有一抹白，水中的鱼儿若隐若现——蓝与白的共舞组曲，可遇不可求。</p><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div ”。（视频截图）" width="640" b="auto"/></picture></a><figcaption>惊鸿一瞥纯白美丽的座头鲸“米迦罗 ”。（视频截图）</figcaption></figure></div><p>请看米迦罗优雅地滑行，流线型的身影以美丽的弧线划过海面，又悄然隐没在波浪中……</p><p>真是毕生难逢的邂逅。</p><p><em>Source: </em>Ella
