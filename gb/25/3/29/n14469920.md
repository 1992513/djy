<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>孩子爱吃零食？一起来做中药棉花糖吧！</h2>

<h6></h6>
<hr><span style="font-weight:400">家里的小孩瘦巴巴的，不但食欲不好、挑食，还只喜欢吃零食，让父母伤透脑筋。爸妈不如来一点不一样的，陪孩子们一起DIY，做出好吃、好玩又健康的中药棉花糖。</span></p><h2><span style="color:#008000"><b>亲子动手做中药棉花糖 好吃好玩兼学习</b></span></h2><div style="color:#008000"><b>轻松制作中药棉花糖</b></span></h2><div class="hidden lg:block"><div class="m-auto items-center justify-center overflow-hidden print:hidden hidden md:flex djy-ad" style="min-height:266px;padding:8px 8px 8px 8px" data-testid="ad-container-DJY_D_article_300x250_5"><div id="DJY_D_article_300x250_5"></div></div></div><div><figure style="max-width:450px" class="aligncenter"><ahref="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/03/id14469926-9903a10c5ec3f9aa94d68f5eb4160732.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><picture><source b="450"/></picture></a><figcaption>中药棉花糖。（马光医疗网提供）</figcaption></figure></div><p><b>主要食材：</b></p><p><span style="font-weight:400">无盐奶油40g</span></p><p><span style="font-weight:400">棉花糖150g</span></p><p><span style="font-weight:400">奶粉90g</span></p><p><span style="font-weight:400">口味：草莓粉</span><span style="font-weight:400">、</span><span style="font-weight:400">抹茶粉或芝麻粉</span></p><p><b>各式口味的配料：</b></p><p><span style="font-weight:400">原味：红枣片、水果干</span></p><p><span style="font-weight:400">草莓口味：洛神花片、红枣片、山楂片、蔓越莓干</span></p><p><span
