<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>奔向健康跑出感恩 桃园北湖国小师生鱼米之乡马拉松</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607301-799092-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>北湖国小学校集合出发。（桃园市府教育局  提供）</h6>
<hr>
<p>【大纪元2025年10月02日讯】（大纪元记者徐乃义台湾桃园报导）桃园市新屋区北湖国小全体师生齐聚一堂，参加“2025新屋鱼米之乡马拉松”。学校将健康促进与教师节庆祝活动结合，藉由路跑展现教育的活力与师生情谊，让这一天不仅充满汗水与笑容，更蕴含着对教师的感恩与对健康生活的实践。</p>
<figure id="14607302" aria-describedby="caption-14607302" style="width: 601px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><ahref=" https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607302-799093-450x191.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607302-799093-450x191.jpg" alt="北湖国小活力健康暖身。" width="601" b="255" /></a><figcaption id="caption-14607302" class="wp-caption-text">北湖国小活力健康暖身。（桃园市府教育局 提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>桃园市新屋区北湖国民小学教导主任黄琪崴指出，马拉松赛事荣获“国际赛道AIMS认证”，是全台瞩目的十大特色马拉松之一。</p>
<figure id="14607303" aria-describedby="caption-14607303" style="width: 601px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><ahref=" https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607303-799094-450x249.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607303-799094-450x249.jpg" alt="北湖国小会场口号加油。" width="601" b="333" /></a><figcaption id="caption-14607303" class="wp-caption-text">北湖国小会场口号加油。（桃园市府教育局 提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>清晨五点，师生们在校内整队、暖身，随后移动至新屋农博环境教育园区，在“做自己的光”的口号下热情起跑。一路上，师生彼此加油打气，展现尊重、关怀与共好的精神，也体现了教育的核心价值—培养孩子面对挑战、勇敢超越的品格。</p>
<figure id="14607307" aria-describedby="caption-14607307" style="width: 602px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><ahref=" https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607307-799098-450x238.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607307-799098-450x238.jpg" alt="北湖国小师生并肩奔行。" width="602" b="318" /></a><figcaption id="caption-14607307" class="wp-caption-text">北湖国小师生并肩奔行。（桃园市府教育局 提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>活动中，老师与学生并肩奔行，用行动诠释“不怯失败而跨步前行，只为超越而挑战自我”的运动精神。</p>
<figure id="14607304" aria-describedby="caption-14607304" style="width: 451px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><ahref=" https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607304-799095-450x600.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607304-799095-450x600.jpg" alt="北湖国小现场人潮聚集。" width="451" b="602" /></a><figcaption id="caption-14607304" class="wp-caption-text">北湖国小现场人潮聚集。（桃园市府教育局 提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>透过马拉松，不仅培养学生养成运动习惯、提升体适能，也在奔跑的过程中，深化了师生情感联系，让教育的力量在跑道上延续。</p>
<figure id="14607305" aria-describedby="caption-14607305" style="width: 601px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><ahref=" https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607305-799096-450x234.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607305-799096-450x234.jpg" alt="倒数计时出发。" width="601" b="313" /></a><figcaption id="caption-14607305" class="wp-caption-text">倒数计时出发。（桃园市府教育局 提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>赛后，师生们凭号码布领取完赛礼，并带着满满的成就感步行回校，结束这场别具意义的教师节庆祝活动。</p>
<figure id="14607306" aria-describedby="caption-14607306" style="width: 602px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><ahref=" https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607306-799097-450x279.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607306-799097-450x279.jpg" alt="热情加油打气。" width="602" b="373" /></a><figcaption id="caption-14607306" class="wp-caption-text">热情加油打气。（桃园市府教育局 提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>校长余豪杰表示：“教育不只在课堂，更在生活；跑道上的每一步与脚印，都是孩子学习成长的精神淬炼基底。”</p>
<figure id="14607308" aria-describedby="caption-14607308" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><ahref=" https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607308-799099-450x338.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2025/10/id14607308-799099-450x338.jpg" alt="领取完赛证书。" width="600" b="451" /></a><figcaption id="caption-14607308" class="wp-caption-text">领取完赛证书。（桃园市府教育局 提供）</figcaption></figure>
<p>黄琪崴说，北湖国小期盼透过参与“鱼米之乡马拉松”，让孩子体会团队合作与自我挑战的精神，进而将健康运动的习惯融入日常，为未来的学习与生活奠定基础。◇</p>
