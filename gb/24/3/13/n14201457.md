<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>纽英崙溯源公所春宴 新年敬老扶幼</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201452-DSC00556-600x400.jpg" />
<h6>纽英崙溯源公所3月10日举办龙年春宴，向九旬长者们赠送敬老红包。（刘景烨/大纪元）</h6>
<hr>
	<p>【大纪元2024年03月13日讯】（大纪元记者刘景烨波士顿报导）“我们溯源公所也秉承‘敬老扶幼’这个立家安邦的基础，一直以来都颁发敬老金和奖学金。”纽英崙溯源公所主席邝炎彬说。
</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400">在3月10日的龙年春宴中，溯源公所与200多位宾客共同庆祝新年，并且延续“敬老扶幼”的传统，赠送敬老金和奖学金红包。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14201453" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14201453" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201453-DSC00560.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14201453" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201453-DSC00560-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14201453" class="wp-caption-text">纽英崙溯源公所3月10日举办龙年春宴，向优秀学子们赠送奖学金红包。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14201449" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14201449" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201449-DSC00545.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14201449" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201449-DSC00545-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14201449" class="wp-caption-text">纽英崙溯源公所3月10日举办龙年春宴，200多人热情参与。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p style="font-weight: 400">晚宴中，邝炎彬首先欢迎来宾，感谢大家参与2024年龙年春宴。“今天我们借帝苑酒楼，准备佳肴美酒，同大家一齐把酒言欢，共聚一堂。”他说。他也提到，多年来溯源公所一直与中华公所及其它侨社紧密合作，一起帮扶社区。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14201444" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14201444" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201444-DSC00538.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14201444" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201444-DSC00538-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14201444" class="wp-caption-text">纽英崙溯源公所主席邝炎彬欢迎来宾。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14201454" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14201454" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201454-DSC00564.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14201454" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201454-DSC00564-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14201454" class="wp-caption-text">纽英崙溯源公所3月10日举办龙年春宴，职员们上台敬酒。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<p style="font-weight: 400">在嘉宾致词之后，另一位主席邝元杰邀请获颁敬老金的九旬长者们上台落座，全体宗亲及嘉宾一起向这些耆英鼓掌致敬。除了敬老之外，溯源公所亦向学业和才艺优秀的年轻学子们赠送了奖学金，鼓励他们未来学有所成后参与侨社服务。</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400">晚宴期间，胡清白鹤派武馆带来醒狮贺岁。吴皓医师以高亢的嗓音领唱美国国歌。接下来还有精彩的舞蹈和歌唱文艺表演，宴会现场喜气洋洋。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14201439" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14201439" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201439-DSC00527.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14201439" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201439-DSC00527-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14201439" class="wp-caption-text">胡清白鹤派舞狮贺岁。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14201443" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14201443" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201443-DSC00534.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14201443" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201443-DSC00534-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14201443" class="wp-caption-text">纽英崙溯源公所职员、嘉宾及舞狮团合影。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14201455" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14201455" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201455-DSC00571.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14201455" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201455-DSC00571-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14201455" class="wp-caption-text">溯源公所宗亲的优秀后辈带来舞蹈表演。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<h3 style="font-weight: 400"><strong>总堂宗亲参与春宴</strong></h3>
<p style="font-weight: 400">当晚，旧金山溯源总堂有20多位宗亲特地来到波士顿参加春宴，包括总堂主席邝耀荣、顾问雷超雄等等。他们代表全美溯源宗亲，感谢纽英崙溯源公所在疫情的艰难时期仍坚持服务。</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400">“纽英崙溯源公所是有光荣历史的侨社，堂务活跃，多年来为繁荣侨社的经济，谋求侨胞的权益，弘扬中华文化做出积极的贡献。”邝耀荣说。在送上新年祝福的同时，他也期待继续与纽英崙溯源公所增进合作，维护侨社的权益。</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400">雷超雄也谈到，虽然纽英崙溯源公所并非全美溯源堂最大的分会，但今晚看到这么多宗亲和嘉宾参加春宴，确实令他感到溯源堂将有越来越好的前景。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14201448" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14201448" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201448-DSC00544.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14201448" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201448-DSC00544-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14201448" class="wp-caption-text">旧金山溯源总堂主席邝耀荣来到波士顿参与溯源公所春宴。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>
<figure id="attachment_14201451" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14201451" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201451-DSC00553.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14201451" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201451-DSC00553-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14201451" class="wp-caption-text">旧金山溯源总堂顾问雷超雄来到波士顿参与溯源公所春宴。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>

<h3 style="font-weight: 400"><strong>嘉宾贺年</strong><strong>?</strong><strong>纪念历史</strong></h3>
<p style="font-weight: 400">当晚宴会的主持人是方柳君。参与春宴的嘉宾包括麻州众议员麦家威（Aaron Michlewitz）、波士顿市长办公室华埠联络员黄楚岚、驻波士顿台北经济文化办事处处长廖朝宏、侨教中心主任潘昭荣、中华公所主席雷国辉等等。</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400">廖朝宏首先用粤语说：“今天很开心来到这里与大家见面。”他感谢溯源公所宗亲们一直以来支持经文处，以及为社区提供服务，为华埠居民和商家营造良好环境。</p>
<figure id="attachment_14201447" aria-describedby="caption-attachment-14201447" style="width: 600px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a target="_blank" href="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201447-DSC00543.jpg" rel="nofollow"><img decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14201447" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/03/id14201447-DSC00543-600x400.jpg" alt="" width="600" b="400"></a><figcaption id="caption-attachment-14201447" class="wp-caption-text">波士顿经文处处长廖朝宏感谢溯源宗亲们的支持。（刘景烨/大纪元）</figcaption></figure>

<p style="font-weight: 400">代表雷、方、邝3姓氏宗亲的溯源堂成立于1846年。距今178年前，宗亲们在广东开平建立了溯源慈善协会。北美溯源堂则于1880年在旧金山成立，并于1886年建立了协会大楼。现在，溯源堂在美国、加拿大、古巴、墨西哥、新加坡、菲律宾、马来西亚、斐济、泰国、澳大利亚、新西兰、缅甸、中国等国家都有分会，帮扶雷、方、邝宗族，并从事公益活动。◇</p>
