<tr><td VALIGN=TOP width="626"><h2 align=center>为台湾流浪狗续命 美籍护理师跨海认养21只犬</h2>
<img width="600" src="https://i.epochtimes.com/assets/uploads/2024/05/id14241808-2405060102222378.jpg" class="" data-thumbnail="true" />
<h6></h6>
